# Topic: Managing a Corporate Crisis of Product Liability / Consumer Safety Lawsuit

**Scenario:** You are the Head of Product Safety at a consumer electronics company. A customer has filed a class action lawsuit claiming that your company's flagship laptop model has a battery defect that causes overheating and, in some cases, fires. The lawsuit cites several incidents, including one that resulted in minor injuries. The media is covering the story, and your stock has dropped 10%. You need to convene an emergency meeting with the CEO, General Counsel, Head of PR, Head of R&D, Head of Operations, and Head of Customer Service to: **assess** the validity of the claims, **engage** with the plaintiffs and their attorneys, **investigate** the root cause of the defect, **determine** whether a recall is necessary, **develop** a communication strategy for customers, regulators, and the media, **coordinate** with insurance providers, **address** the financial impact, and **implement** corrective actions to prevent future incidents.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CEO** | *(opening the emergency meeting)* We have a serious product liability crisis. A class action lawsuit has been filed. The media is circling, and our reputation is at risk. We need to respond quickly and responsibly. |
| **You** | We've conducted an initial investigation. The issue appears to be isolated to a specific batch of batteries from a supplier. We've identified the batch and are reviewing our quality control records. |
| **General Counsel** | We need to engage external counsel with product liability expertise. We'll also need to preserve all relevant documents and prepare for discovery. |
| **Head of R&D** | Our engineers are working on a fix. We believe we can resolve the issue with a software update that limits charging on affected units. We're also testing a hardware replacement option. |
| **Head of Operations** | We need to decide on a recall. Should we recall all units in the affected batch, or only those that have reported issues? |
| **You** | I recommend a targeted recall of the affected batch—approximately 50,000 units. We'll notify customers and offer a free battery replacement or a software update. |
| **Head of PR** | What's our public statement? |
| **You** | I propose: *"We have identified a potential issue with a specific batch of batteries. We are voluntarily recalling those units and will replace them at no cost. Customer safety is our top priority. We apologize for any inconvenience."* |
| **Head of Customer Service** | My team will need a script for customer calls. |
| **You** | We'll set up a dedicated hotline and provide a clear script: *"We have identified a potential issue with your product. We are offering a free battery replacement. Please visit our website or call this number to arrange it."* |
| **CEO** | What about the financial impact? |
| **You** | The recall will cost approximately $5M—but that's far less than the cost of a prolonged lawsuit or reputational damage. We'll work with our insurance provider to cover the costs. |
| **General Counsel** | We'll also need to engage with the plaintiffs' attorneys to explore a settlement. |
| **You** | *(closing)* To summarize: we're issuing a targeted recall, offering a free solution, communicating transparently, and cooperating with legal and regulatory authorities. We'll reconvene in 24 hours to track progress. |
| *(48 hours later – follow-up)* | |
| **You** | *(to the leadership team)* The recall is underway. We've contacted 80% of affected customers. The media coverage has been balanced—most outlets are crediting our transparency. |
| **Head of R&D** | The software update is working, and we're shipping replacement batteries. |
| **General Counsel** | We're in settlement discussions with the plaintiffs. |
| **CEO** | We've handled this with speed and integrity. Now let's ensure we prevent future issues by reviewing our quality control processes. |
| **You** | We'll conduct a full audit of our battery suppliers and enhance our testing protocols. |
