## Topic: Listening to Your Body

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been feeling so tired lately, but I keep ignoring it and pushing through. I think my body is trying to tell me something. |
| **Alexa** | I know that feeling. We often override our physical signals because we think we need to keep going. But your body is always speaking to you. |
| **Alexis** | What do you mean? |
| **Alexa** | Fatigue, tension, aches, even cravings—they're all messages. They're saying, "Slow down," "Rest," "Drink water," or "Move." |
| **Alexis** | I've been so disconnected from my body. I live in my head most of the time. |
| **Alexa** | It's easy to do. But your body is wise. It holds wisdom that your mind doesn't always access. |
| **Alexis** | How do I start listening more? |
| **Alexa** | Start small. Pause during the day and check in with yourself. Ask, "What do I need right now?" |
| **Alexis** | Usually, the answer is rest or water or a break. But I ignore it. |
| **Alexa** | That's the habit you can change. The first step is just noticing. Without judgment. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been treating my body like a machine instead of a living being. |
| **Alexa** | That's so common. But your body isn't a machine. It's a garden that needs care and attention. |
| **Alexis** | I've been ignoring hunger, staying up too late, not moving enough. No wonder I feel off. |
| **Alexa** | Your body is telling you it's out of balance. And it's asking you to come back. |
| **Alexis** | I want to start honoring what my body tells me. Even small things. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. Start with one simple promise: when you feel tired, rest. |
| **Alexis** | I can do that. I don't have to push through every time. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. You're not weak for listening. You're wise for honoring your own needs. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to move my body in ways that feel good, not just for exercise. |
| **Alexa** | That's a great shift. Movement should be joyful, not a punishment. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so focused on what my body looks like that I forgot to listen to how it feels. |
| **Alexa** | That's the deeper work. Learning to feel your body from the inside, not just see it from the outside. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to practice this. I'll check in with myself right now. What do I need? |
| **Alexa** | What's the answer? |
| **Alexis** | I need to breathe deeply and stretch my shoulders. |
| **Alexa** | Then do that. Right now. That's listening. |
| **Alexis** | I already feel more connected. Thank you for guiding me back. |
| **Alexa** | You guided yourself. I just reminded you to listen. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Pushing through** (fatigue) | Phrasal Verb | Continuing to work or act despite feeling tired or in pain |
| **Physical signals** | Noun (plural) | Messages from your body like fatigue, hunger, or tension |
| **Wisdom** (of your body) | Noun | Deep, instinctive knowledge that your body holds |
| **Check in** (with yourself) | Phrasal Verb | To pause and assess how you feel emotionally and physically |
| **Judgment** (without) | Noun | Forming a critical opinion about yourself |
| **Machine** (treating body like) | Noun / Metaphor | Seeing your body as a tool rather than a living being |
| **Garden** (metaphor) | Noun | Something that needs care, attention, and gentle tending |
| **Off** (feeling off) | Adjective | Not feeling right; out of balance |
| **Honoring** (what your body says) | Verb | Respecting and acting on your body's needs |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Punishment** (exercise as) | Noun | Treating movement as a penalty or obligation |
| **Feel from the inside** | Phrase | Being aware of your internal physical sensations |
| **Connected** (to your body) | Adjective | Feeling in tune with your physical self |
