### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"Happy Birthday!"* – The standard greeting for someone's birthday.
  - *"Thank you so much for coming!"* – Expressing gratitude to a guest.
  - *"Here, this is for you."* – Offering a gift.
  - *"Should I open it now or wait?"* – Asking about gift-giving etiquette.
  - *"Can I get you a drink?"* – Offering refreshments.
  - *"Make yourself at home."* – Encouraging guests to relax.
  - *"It's time for the birthday cake!"* – Announcing the cake-cutting moment.
  - *"Make a wish!"* – Encouraging the birthday person to make a secret wish.
  - *"I had a wonderful time."* – Expressing enjoyment of the party.
  - *"Thank you for inviting me."* – Expressing gratitude for the invitation.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"Come in, come in!"* – A warm, welcoming phrase.
  - *"You didn't have to!"* – A humble response when receiving a gift.
  - *"Oh my gosh!"* – Expressing surprise or excitement.
  - *"How did you remember?"* – Showing appreciation for a thoughtful gift.
  - *"You're so thoughtful."* – Complimenting someone's consideration.
  - *"I completely agree."* – Expressing strong agreement.
  - *"Deal!"* – A casual way to agree to a plan.
  - *"I can't tell you, or it won't come true!"* – A playful response about wishes.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Arrive with a gift** – It's customary to bring a gift to a birthday party. Say: *"Happy Birthday! This is for you."* Even a small, thoughtful gift is appreciated.
  2. **Compliment the decorations and preparation** – Acknowledge the host's efforts: *"Your place looks amazing!"* and *"The decorations are beautiful!"* It shows you notice their work.
  3. **Thank the host when you arrive** – Say: *"Thank you so much for inviting me."* It sets a positive tone and shows appreciation.
  4. **Ask about gifts** – If you're unsure whether to open gifts immediately, ask: *"Should I open it now or wait?"* This shows respect for the host's preference.
  5. **Mingle with other guests** – Ask: *"How do you know [the birthday person]?"* This helps you connect with others and makes the party more enjoyable.
  6. **Participate in the cake ceremony** – Gather around, sing along, and cheer when the candles are blown out. Say: *"Make a wish!"* and *"Yay!"* after the candles are blown out.
  7. **Accept food and drinks graciously** – When offered, say: *"I'll take a [drink], please"* and *"Thank you."* It's polite to accept what's offered.
  8. **Know when to leave** – As the party winds down, thank the host again: *"I had a wonderful time. Thank you for inviting me."* And if you're driving, say: *"Drive safe"* to other guests when leaving.
  9. **Follow up after the party** – The next day, send a text: *"Thanks again for a great party!"* It leaves a positive impression.
