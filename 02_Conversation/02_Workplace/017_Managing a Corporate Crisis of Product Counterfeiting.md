# Topic: Managing a Corporate Crisis of Product Counterfeiting / IP Infringement

**Scenario:** You are the Chief Legal Officer at a global consumer goods company. Your company has discovered that a counterfeit version of its flagship product is being sold online and in physical stores across three countries. The counterfeit product is of poor quality and has caused skin irritation in some customers. This is a severe crisis affecting brand reputation, customer safety, and revenue. You need to convene an emergency meeting with the CEO, Head of Brand Protection, Head of Supply Chain, Head of PR, and Head of Customer Service to: **assess** the scope and origin of the counterfeiting operation, **coordinate** with law enforcement and customs authorities, **protect** customers from counterfeit products, **communicate** with the public and regulators, **initiate** legal action against the counterfeiters, and **strengthen** supply chain and anti‑counterfeiting measures—all while minimizing reputational damage and restoring trust.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **You** | *(opening the emergency call)* Thank you for joining. We have a serious crisis: counterfeit versions of our flagship product are being sold in three countries. Customers have reported skin irritation from the counterfeit product. This is damaging our brand, our revenue, and most importantly, our trust with customers. We need to act immediately. |
| **CEO** | What's the scope of the counterfeit operation? |
| **Head of Brand Protection** | We've identified over 500 online listings and 30 physical stores selling the counterfeit. The counterfeit product is made from cheap, harmful ingredients. We've traced the supply chain to a network in Southeast Asia. |
| **You** | We need to coordinate with law enforcement and customs authorities in all three countries. I'll lead the legal action—we'll file for trademark infringement, and we'll seek seizure orders and criminal charges. |
| **Head of Supply Chain** | We also need to ensure our genuine product is secure. We'll increase physical security at our manufacturing and distribution centers. |
| **Head of PR** | What's our public message? We need to protect our reputation while warning customers. |
| **You** | I propose a two‑part communication: **#1**—A public warning: *"We have identified counterfeit products in the market. We advise customers to buy only from authorized retailers. We are working with authorities to stop the counterfeiters."* **#2**—A customer reassurance: *"Our genuine product is safe and undergoes rigorous quality testing. We stand behind our quality."* |
| **Head of Customer Service** | My team needs a script. Customers will call to verify their product. |
| **You** | We'll set up a dedicated counterfeit hotline where customers can verify the authenticity of their product using a serial number or QR code. We'll also create an online verification tool. |
| **CEO** | What about the legal action? |
| **You** | We'll file lawsuits in all three countries. We'll also work with online marketplaces to remove counterfeit listings. I'll coordinate with external counsel and local law firms. |
| **Head of Brand Protection** | We'll also work with customs to intercept counterfeit shipments at the border. |
| **You** | *(assigning tasks)* **Head of Brand Protection**—coordinate with law enforcement and customs. **Head of Supply Chain**—secure the supply chain. **Head of PR**—manage public communications. **Head of Customer Service**—set up the verification hotline. **Legal**—lead the lawsuits and enforcement actions. I'll coordinate the overall response and provide regular updates to the board. |
| **You** | *(closing)* This is a serious threat, but we have a clear plan: protect customers, stop the counterfeiters, and restore trust. We'll reconvene in 24 hours for an update. |
| *(48 hours later – follow-up)* | |
| **You** | *(to the leadership team)* We've filed lawsuits in all three countries. Customs have seized several shipments. The online marketplaces have removed over 300 listings. The verification hotline has received 2,000 calls, and we've been able to reassure customers. |
| **Head of PR** | The public response has been positive. Customers appreciate our transparency and action. |
| **CEO** | Now we need to ensure this never happens again. |
| **You** | We'll implement a global anti‑counterfeiting strategy: **#1**—Strengthen supply chain security. **#2**—Invest in track‑and‑trace technology. **#3**—Establish a rapid response team for future incidents. I'll present a detailed proposal at the next board meeting. |

## Vocabulary

| **Word / Phrase** | **Part of Speech** | **Meaning** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Counterfeit** | Adjective/ Noun | A fake or imitation product made to deceive. |
| **Intellectual property (IP) infringement** | Noun Phrase | Unauthorised use of a company's patents, trademarks, or copyrights. |
| **Trademark infringement** | Noun Phrase | Unauthorised use of a registered trademark. |
| **Seizure orders** | Noun Phrase | Legal orders to confiscate counterfeit goods. |
| **Criminal charges** | Noun Phrase | Legal accusations of a crime. |
| **Law enforcement** | Noun Phrase | Police and government agencies that enforce the law. |
| **Customs authorities** | Noun Phrase | Government agencies that control imports and exports. |
| **Verification tool** | Noun Phrase | A system to confirm the authenticity of a product. |
| **Serial number** | Noun Phrase | A unique identifier on a product. |
| **QR code** | Noun Phrase | A scannable code that contains product information. |
| **Track‑and‑trace technology** | Noun Phrase | Systems to monitor a product's journey through the supply chain. |
| **Rapid response team** | Noun Phrase | A team that can react quickly to future incidents. |
| **Counterfeit hotline** | Noun Phrase | A dedicated phone line for reporting fakes. |
| **Authorized retailers** | Noun Phrase | Approved sellers of genuine products. |
| **Quality testing** | Noun Phrase | Procedures to ensure product safety and standards. |
| **Supply chain security** | Noun Phrase | Measures to protect the product journey from factory to customer. |
| **Reputational damage** | Noun Phrase | Harm to a company's public image. |
| **Trust** | Noun | Confidence in the brand's reliability and safety. |
| **Enforcement actions** | Noun Phrase | Legal measures taken to stop infringements. |
| **Legal counsel** | Noun Phrase | Lawyers who represent the company. |
