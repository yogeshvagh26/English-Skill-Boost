## Topic: The Importance of a Good Night's Sleep

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been running on five hours of sleep for the past week, and I feel like a zombie. I know I need more rest, but I can't seem to turn my brain off at night. |
| **Alexa** | That's so tough. Sleep is literally the foundation of everything—mood, focus, health. When you skimp on it, everything else suffers. |
| **Alexis** | I always think I can just push through, but by mid-afternoon I'm completely useless. |
| **Alexa** | That's your body telling you something. You can't cheat sleep forever. |
| **Alexis** | I keep telling myself I'll go to bed early, but then I get distracted by my phone or work. |
| **Alexa** | It's a vicious cycle. The more tired you are, the harder it is to make good decisions. |
| **Alexis** | I think I need a proper wind-down routine. Something that tells my brain it's time to rest. |
| **Alexa** | That's a great idea. I've started doing a few things that really help. |
| **Alexis** | Like what? I need practical tips. |
| **Alexa** | I dim the lights an hour before bed, put my phone away, and read a physical book. |
| **Alexis** | That sounds so peaceful. I usually scroll until my eyes burn. |
| **Alexa** | The blue light from screens tricks your brain into thinking it's daytime. |
| **Alexis** | I know, but I still do it. It's like I'm addicted to the stimulation. |
| **Alexa** | You can start small. Even 15 minutes of screen-free time before bed can make a difference. |
| **Alexis** | I'll try that tonight. And maybe I'll make a cup of chamomile tea. |
| **Alexa** | That's a lovely idea. Warm tea is so calming. |
| **Alexis** | I also think I need to stop working so late. My brain is still in work mode when I try to sleep. |
| **Alexa** | Setting a hard stop for work is so important. It gives your mind time to transition. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to set an alarm that tells me to wrap up and start winding down. |
| **Alexa** | That's a great boundary. Sleep is non-negotiable. |
| **Alexis** | I really want to prioritize my health more. I can't keep burning the candle at both ends. |
| **Alexa** | You deserve rest. It's not a reward—it's a necessity. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you for reminding me of that. I needed to hear it. |
| **Alexa** | Always. Now go get some sleep tonight. Your body will thank you. |
| **Alexis** | I will. And I'll text you tomorrow and tell you how it went. |
| **Alexa** | Please do. I'm rooting for you. |


### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Zombie** (metaphor) | Noun | Someone who is moving slowly and without energy |
| **Foundation** | Noun | The base or core on which something is built |
| **Skimp on** (sleep) | Verb | To use less than what is needed |
| **Useless** | Adjective | Not able to function effectively |
| **Cheat sleep** | Phrase | To try to avoid needing proper rest |
| **Vicious cycle** | Idiom / Phrase | A repeating pattern that makes a problem worse |
| **Wind-down routine** | Phrase | A series of relaxing activities before bed |
| **Dim the lights** | Verb Phrase | To reduce brightness in a room |
| **Physical book** | Noun | A printed book (as opposed to an e-book) |
| **Blue light** | Noun | Light from screens that can disrupt sleep |
| **Stimulation** | Noun | Something that excites or arouses the mind |
| **Addicted** | Adjective | Unable to stop doing something |
| **Calming** | Adjective | Making you feel relaxed and peaceful |
| **Transition** | Verb / Noun | The process of changing from one state to another |
| **Boundary** | Noun | A limit you set to protect your well-being |
| **Non-negotiable** | Adjective | Absolutely necessary; cannot be compromised |
| **Prioritize** | Verb | To treat something as more important than others |
| **Burning the candle at both ends** | Idiom | Working or staying up late while also getting up early |
| **Necessity** | Noun | Something that is required, not optional |
| **Rooting for you** | Phrase | Encouraging and supporting you to succeed |
