## Topic: The Power of Support

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been feeling really down lately, and I just want to thank you for always being there. You don't even have to say anything—just knowing you're there helps. |
| **Alexa** | That means so much to me. And I want you to know that you don't have to go through anything alone. I'm here. Always. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so used to carrying everything by myself that I forgot how good it feels to lean on someone. |
| **Alexa** | That's a hard habit to break. But support isn't a sign of weakness—it's a sign of trust. |
| **Alexis** | I guess I've been afraid of being a burden. |
| **Alexa** | You're never a burden. You're a person I love, and I want to be there for you. |
| **Alexis** | I feel like I've been holding so much inside, and just talking to you makes it feel lighter. |
| **Alexa** | That's what support does. It doesn't take the problem away, but it makes it easier to carry. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to be better at supporting others. I feel like I've been so caught up in my own struggles that I haven't shown up for people. |
| **Alexa** | That happens. But you can always start today. Even a small gesture—a text, a call, a listening ear—can make a huge difference. |
| **Alexis** | I think sometimes we underestimate how much a little support can mean to someone. |
| **Alexa** | We really do. Sometimes the smallest act of kindness is the one that someone needed most. |
| **Alexis** | I'm grateful for the people in my life who support me—and I want to be that person for others too. |
| **Alexa** | You already are. I've seen you do it. You just don't always see it in yourself. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. That means a lot coming from you. |
| **Alexa** | I'm just being honest. You're a good friend, Alexis. And you're allowed to need support too. |
| **Alexis** | I'm learning that. Slowly. |
| **Alexa** | That's all any of us can do. One step at a time. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Support** | Noun | Help, encouragement, and emotional backing from others |
| **Lean on** (someone) | Phrasal Verb | To rely on someone for emotional or practical help |
| **Burden** | Noun | A heavy load or responsibility |
| **Lighter** (feeling) | Adjective | Less burdened; more free and at ease |
| **Carry** (a problem) | Verb | To hold onto emotional weight or stress |
| **Show up** (for people) | Phrasal Verb | To be present and available for someone |
| **Gesture** (small act) | Noun | An action that expresses care or kindness |
| **Listening ear** | Phrase | Being available to hear someone's thoughts and feelings |
| **Underestimate** | Verb | To think something is less powerful or important than it is |
| **Grateful** | Adjective | Feeling thankful and appreciative |
| **Allowed** (to need support) | Adjective | Having permission; being free to do something |
| **Slowly** (learning) | Adverb | At a gradual pace, not rushing |
| **One step at a time** | Phrase | Taking things gradually and patiently |
