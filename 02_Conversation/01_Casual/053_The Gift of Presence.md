## Topic: The Gift of Presence

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking about how often I'm physically present but mentally somewhere else. I want to be more fully here. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a meaningful intention. Being present is one of the greatest gifts you can give to yourself and to others. |
| **Alexis** | I've been so distracted by my phone, my worries, my to-do list. I miss so much of what's right in front of me. |
| **Alexa** | It happens to all of us. But you can always come back. The present moment is patient—it waits for you. |
| **Alexis** | I want to practice being more present in my conversations. I want people to feel like they have my full attention. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful goal. When you give someone your full presence, you're telling them, "You matter." |
| **Alexis** | I also want to be present with myself. To sit quietly and just be, without filling every moment with noise. |
| **Alexa** | That's where self-connection lives. In the quiet moments, you meet yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been afraid of the silence. But maybe that's where I'll find what I'm looking for. |
| **Alexa** | The silence isn't empty. It's full of answers waiting to be heard. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to try putting my phone away during meals and just eat. Taste my food, be with my thoughts. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful practice. You'll start to notice the richness of simple moments. |
| **Alexis** | And I'll try to listen more deeply when others speak. Not just to respond, but to understand. |
| **Alexa** | That's the heart of connection. It's not about words—it's about presence. |
| **Alexis** | I feel like I've been rushing through life. I want to start savoring it. |
| **Alexa** | Savoring is presence in action. It's choosing to fully experience the moment you're in. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to practice this today. One moment at a time. |
| **Alexa** | I'll join you. Let's be present, together. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Presence** (being present) | Noun | The state of being fully aware and engaged in the current moment |
| **Mentally somewhere else** | Phrase | Thinking about other things instead of focusing on the here and now |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Gift** (presence as a gift) | Metaphor | Something valuable that you offer freely to others or yourself |
| **Distracted** | Adjective | Unable to concentrate because something is taking your attention |
| **Come back** (to the present) | Phrasal Verb | To return your attention to the current moment |
| **Patient** (the present moment is) | Adjective | Willing to wait; the present doesn't rush |
| **Full attention** | Phrase | Complete focus on someone or something |
| **Silence** | Noun | A quiet state without noise or distraction |
| **Self-connection** | Noun | The relationship and understanding you have with yourself |
| **Richness** (of simple moments) | Noun | Depth, meaning, and value in everyday experiences |
| **Savoring** | Verb | Enjoying and appreciating something fully |
| **Rushing through** | Phrasal Verb | Moving quickly without paying attention |
| **One moment at a time** | Phrase | Taking things gradually, focusing on the present moment |
