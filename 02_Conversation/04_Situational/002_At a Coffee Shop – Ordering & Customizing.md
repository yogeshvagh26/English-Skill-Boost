### 2. Conversation

*Setting: A busy coffee shop. Alex is a first-time visitor, and Jamie is the barista.*

| Speaker     | Dialogue                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Barista** | Hi there! Welcome to Brew & Bean. What can I get started for you today?                                                                                                  |
| **Alex**    | Hi! I'd like a medium latte, please.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Barista** | Sure thing. Hot or iced?                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Alex**    | Hot, please. It's a bit chilly outside.                                                                                                                                  |
| **Barista** | Got it. And what kind of milk would you prefer? We have whole, oat, almond, and skim.                                                                                    |
| **Alex**    | Oat milk sounds good. I'll try that.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Barista** | Great choice. Any flavor shots? We've got vanilla, caramel, hazelnut, and seasonal pumpkin spice.                                                                        |
| **Alex**    | Could I add a shot of vanilla? Just one pump, though – I don't like it too sweet.                                                                                        |
| **Barista** | Of course, one pump of vanilla. Would you like any whipped cream on top?                                                                                                 |
| **Alex**    | No, thanks. I'll skip that.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Barista** | No problem. That comes to $5.75. Will that be for here or to go?                                                                                                         |
| **Alex**    | For here, please. I'm planning to sit and work for a bit.                                                                                                                |
| **Barista** | Perfect. You can take a seat, and I'll bring it over to you when it's ready. Name for the order?                                                                         |
| **Alex**    | Alex.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Barista** | Alex, your latte will be ready in just a few minutes.                                                                                                                    |
| *(A few minutes later – Barista brings the drink)*                                                                                                                       |
| **Barista** | Here you go, Alex. One medium oat milk latte with a pump of vanilla. Enjoy!                                                                                              |
| **Alex**    | Thank you so much! Looks great.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Barista** | You're welcome. Let me know if you need anything else – napkins, a stirrer, or a water refill.                                                                           |
| **Alex**    | Actually, could I get a napkin? Thanks!                                                                                                                                  |

### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"What can I get started for you?"* – Standard greeting at a café or restaurant.
  - *"Would you prefer A or B?"* – Offering a clear choice between two options.
  - *"Could I add / get …?"* – A polite way to request an extra item or modification.
  - *"That comes to [price]."* – Telling the customer the total cost.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"Sure thing."* – A friendly, enthusiastic way to say "yes."
  - *"Got it."* – Means "I understand" or "I've noted that."
  - *"I'll skip that."* – A polite way to decline an offer.
  - *"Here you go."* – Used when handing something to someone.
  - *"Enjoy!"* – A common warm wish when serving food or drink.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Customize confidently** – Don't be afraid to ask for substitutions (e.g., oat milk) or adjust sweetness (e.g., half a pump). Cafés are used to custom orders.
  2. **Clarify your intention** – Always specify "for here" or "to go" upfront so they use the right cup and charge correctly.
  3. **Listen for the name call** – When they ask for your name, speak clearly and listen for it later to avoid missing your order.
  4. **Use polite follow-ups** – After receiving your drink, it's nice to say *"Looks great!"* or *"Perfect, thanks!"* to show appreciation.
