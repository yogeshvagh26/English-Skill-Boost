## Topic: The Art of Being Alone (Without Feeling Lonely)

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I used to think being alone meant I was lonely. But lately, I've been craving more time by myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful distinction. Loneliness is a feeling of disconnection—solitude is a choice to be with yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I've started going to cafés alone, reading books without my phone nearby. It feels strange but also peaceful. |
| **Alexa** | That's beautiful. You're learning to enjoy your own company. |
| **Alexis** | I always thought I needed to be around people to feel happy. But I'm finding that I can feel happy just being with me. |
| **Alexa** | That's a huge realization. When you're comfortable alone, you don't rely on others to fill a void. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been afraid of my own thoughts. When I'm alone, they get loud. |
| **Alexa** | That's the hard part. But facing your thoughts is how you understand yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I've been journaling more, and it's helping. I'm starting to hear my own voice. |
| **Alexa** | That's the voice that knows what you truly want. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to try taking walks alone. Just me and nature. |
| **Alexa** | That's a wonderful practice. You'll notice things you never see when you're with others. |
| **Alexis** | I'm a little scared, honestly. It's unfamiliar. |
| **Alexa** | That's okay. New things often feel scary at first. But you're growing. |
| **Alexis** | I think I need to learn to be my own best friend. |
| **Alexa** | That's one of the most important relationships you'll ever have. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to schedule alone time this week. Not as a punishment, but as a gift. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful shift. Alone time is self-care, not isolation. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm finally learning to be comfortable in my own skin. |
| **Alexa** | That's where true peace begins. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Solitude** | Noun | The state of being alone, especially by choice |
| **Loneliness** | Noun | The feeling of being sad and disconnected from others |
| **Distinction** | Noun | A clear difference between two things |
| **Craving** (more time) | Verb | Wanting something intensely |
| **Peaceful** | Adjective | Calm, quiet, and free from disturbance |
| **Company** (of yourself) | Noun | Being with someone (here, yourself) |
| **Void** (to fill) | Noun | An empty feeling that needs to be filled |
| **Journaling** | Noun / Verb | The practice of writing down thoughts and feelings |
| **Unfamiliar** | Adjective | Not known or experienced before |
| **Growing** | Verb | Developing and improving |
| **Your own best friend** | Metaphor / Phrase | Treating yourself with kindness and care |
| **Shift** (in perspective) | Noun | A change in the way you view things |
| **Isolation** | Noun | The state of being separated from others (often negative) |
| **Self-care** | Noun | Actions taken to protect your mental and physical health |
| **Comfortable in your own skin** | Idiom / Phrase | Feeling at ease with who you are |
| **True peace** | Phrase | Deep, lasting calm within yourself |
