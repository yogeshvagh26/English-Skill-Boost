# Topic: Managing a Corporate Innovation Fund / Venture Studio Model

**Scenario:** You are the Head of Corporate Innovation at a large insurance company. Your company has decided to launch a $50M Corporate Innovation Fund with a "Venture Studio" model—where you don't just invest in external startups, but also incubate and build new ventures internally. The goal is to create new revenue streams and stay ahead of market disruption. You are now leading a strategy session with the CEO, CFO, Head of Strategy, and Head of Legal to: **define** the venture studio's focus areas (e.g., InsurTech, AI, sustainability), **design** the operating model, **set** investment criteria and governance, **address** concerns about risk and cannibalization, **establish** metrics for success, and **secure** the committee's commitment to launch the initiative.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **You** | *(opening the session)* Thank you for joining. We're here to launch our Corporate Innovation Fund—but with a twist. Rather than just investing in external startups, we're going to build a Venture Studio: a dedicated team that will incubate new ventures from idea to launch. This is about creating new revenue streams and staying ahead of disruption. |
| **CEO** | What's the investment? |
| **You** | We're proposing a $50M fund over 3 years: $30M for venture building (internal incubation), and $20M for strategic investments in external startups that align with our focus areas. |
| **CFO** | What are the focus areas? |
| **You** | We're targeting three themes: **#1**—InsurTech (AI‑driven underwriting and claims). **#2**—Sustainability (climate risk analytics). **#3**—Customer experience (personalized digital journeys). |
| **Head of Strategy** | How does this differentiate us from competitors? |
| **You** | We're not just investing—we're building. We'll have a dedicated team of entrepreneurs‑in‑residence, designers, engineers, and product managers. We'll incubate ventures for 12‑18 months, test them with real customers, and either scale them or shut them down quickly. |
| **Head of Legal** | What about governance? How do we manage conflicts of interest and IP ownership? |
| **You** | Each venture will operate as a separate legal entity, with a clear IP assignment agreement. The parent company will have a minority equity stake, with options to acquire the venture if it succeeds. We'll have a governance board with representatives from the parent company and external experts. |
| **CFO** | How will we measure success? |
| **You** | Three key metrics: **#1**—Number of ventures launched and scaled. **#2**—Revenue generated from new ventures. **#3**—Strategic value—including talent development and brand reputation. We'll report progress quarterly. |
| **CEO** | What about risk? We're investing in unproven ideas. |
| **You** | We'll use a stage‑gate process: each venture moves through phases—ideation, validation, pilot, and scale. At each gate, we'll review progress and decide whether to continue, pivot, or kill the venture. This minimizes downside and maximizes learning. |
| **You** | *(closing)* I'll draft a detailed operating model, governance framework, and budget. This initiative will position us as a leader in innovation—not just in insurance, but in the broader ecosystem. Thank you for your support. |
| *(12 months later – progress update)* | |
| **You** | *(to the committee)* I'm pleased to report that we've launched 3 ventures: an AI‑powered claims platform, a climate risk analytics tool, and a personalized digital advisor. We've also made 5 strategic investments. Early revenue from the ventures is already $5M. |
| **CFO** | The financials are tracking ahead of projections. |
| **CEO** | I'm impressed. This is becoming a significant driver of growth. |
| **You** | This is just the beginning. We'll continue to build and scale the venture studio model over the next 2 years. |
