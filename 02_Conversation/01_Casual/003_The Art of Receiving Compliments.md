## Topic: The Art of Receiving Compliments

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking about how bad I am at receiving compliments. Whenever someone says something nice, I immediately deflect it. |
| **Alexa** | That's so common. Many of us feel awkward when praised. We're taught to be humble, but we end up dismissing the kindness. |
| **Alexis** | I always say things like "Oh, it was nothing" or "Anyone could have done it." I don't know how to just say thank you. |
| **Alexa** | That's the habit you can change. Receiving a compliment is a skill, and it starts with letting the words land. |
| **Alexis** | But I feel like if I accept it, I'm being arrogant. |
| **Alexa** | There's a big difference between arrogance and grace. Saying "thank you" shows confidence and respect for the person complimenting you. |
| **Alexis** | I never thought of it that way. I thought I was being modest. |
| **Alexa** | Modesty is good, but deflecting can actually feel like a rejection to the other person. They're offering you something positive, and you're pushing it away. |
| **Alexis** | That's a good point. I don't want to make others feel rejected. |
| **Alexa** | Next time someone compliments you, just say "thank you." Nothing more. No explanation, no self-deprecating joke. |
| **Alexis** | Just "thank you"? That feels so simple, but it's hard. |
| **Alexa** | It will be uncomfortable at first. But with practice, it will start to feel more natural. |
| **Alexis** | I think deep down I don't feel worthy of praise. That's why I deflect. |
| **Alexa** | That's the deeper issue. But you are worthy of appreciation. You don't have to earn the right to receive kindness. |
| **Alexis** | I want to work on that. I want to be able to accept compliments gracefully. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. Start with one compliment today. Just say "thank you" and stop yourself from adding anything else. |
| **Alexis** | I'll try. Even if it feels awkward. |
| **Alexa** | That's all it takes. And over time, you'll start to believe the words you're hearing. |
| **Alexis** | I hope so. I'm tired of feeling like I don't deserve good things. |
| **Alexa** | You do deserve them. And accepting a compliment is a way of letting that truth in. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Receiving compliments** | Verb Phrase | Accepting praise or positive words from others |
| **Deflect** (a compliment) | Verb | To redirect or avoid accepting something positive |
| **Awkward** | Adjective | Uncomfortable and embarrassing |
| **Dismissing** (the kindness) | Verb | Rejecting or not accepting something offered |
| **Arrogance** | Noun | Excessive pride or self-importance |
| **Grace** | Noun | Elegance and politeness in accepting things |
| **Confidence** | Noun | A feeling of self-assurance |
| **Modest** | Adjective | Not thinking you are better than others |
| **Self-deprecating** (joke) | Adjective | Making fun of yourself, often to avoid praise |
| **Worthy** (of praise) | Adjective | Deserving of something good |
| **Appreciation** | Noun | Recognition and gratitude for something |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Believe** (the words) | Verb | To accept something as true in your mind |
| **Deserve** | Verb | To be worthy of something good |
