## Topic: The Art of Saying No

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I have such a hard time saying no. I always feel like I'm letting people down. |
| **Alexa** | That's a tough habit to break. But saying no is a way of saying yes to yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I never thought of it that way. I just feel so guilty. |
| **Alexa** | The guilt is understandable. But your time and energy are precious. |
| **Alexis** | I've been saying yes to things I don't want to do, and I end up exhausted and resentful. |
| **Alexa** | That's the cost of over-committing. You're not being fair to yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I think I'm scared of being seen as selfish or rude. |
| **Alexa** | Setting boundaries isn't selfish. It's self-respect. |
| **Alexis** | That's a powerful distinction. I'm going to try to remember that. |
| **Alexa** | You can say no kindly. You don't have to be harsh. |
| **Alexis** | What would you say in a situation where you need to decline? |
| **Alexa** | I usually say, "I'd love to, but I can't right now," or "That won't work for me, but thank you for asking." |
| **Alexis** | Those are really gentle but clear. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. You're not leaving them guessing, but you're also not over-explaining. |
| **Alexis** | I tend to over-explain. I give a whole list of reasons. |
| **Alexa** | You don't owe anyone a full explanation. A simple no is enough. |
| **Alexis** | That's going to take practice. I'm so used to justifying myself. |
| **Alexa** | It does take practice. But every time you say no without guilt, it gets easier. |
| **Alexis** | I want to protect my peace more. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. Protecting your peace is an act of self-love. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to start small. Say no to one thing this week that I would usually say yes to. |
| **Alexa** | That's a perfect start. Celebrate that small win. |
| **Alexis** | I will. And I'll check in with you to stay accountable. |
| **Alexa** | I'd love that. I'm so proud of you for even wanting to try. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel lighter just talking about it. |
| **Alexa** | You're doing the work. And that's what matters. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Letting people down** | Phrase / Verb | Disappointing or failing to meet someone's expectations |
| **Guilty** | Adjective | Feeling regret or responsibility for something |
| **Precious** (time and energy) | Adjective | Very valuable and important |
| **Exhausted** | Adjective | Extremely tired and drained |
| **Resentful** | Adjective | Feeling bitterness or anger because of unfair treatment |
| **Over-committing** | Verb | Taking on more responsibilities than you can handle |
| **Selfish** | Adjective | Caring only about yourself, not others |
| **Rude** | Adjective | Impolite; not showing good manners |
| **Boundaries** | Noun (plural) | Limits you set to protect your mental and emotional health |
| **Self-respect** | Noun | Pride and confidence in yourself |
| **Distinction** | Noun | A clear difference between two things |
| **Decline** (a request) | Verb | To politely refuse something |
| **Gentle** (communication) | Adjective | Kind, soft, and not aggressive |
| **Clear** (communication) | Adjective | Easy to understand; unambiguous |
| **Over-explaining** | Verb | Giving too many details to justify a decision |
| **Owe** (an explanation) | Verb | To be obligated to give something |
| **Justifying** (myself) | Verb | Explaining or defending your reasons |
| **Protect my peace** | Verb Phrase | To guard your emotional calm and well-being |
| **Self-love** | Noun | The practice of caring for and valuing yourself |
| **Accountable** | Adjective | Responsible for your actions or promises |
| **Lighter** (feeling) | Adjective | Less burdened; more free and at ease |
