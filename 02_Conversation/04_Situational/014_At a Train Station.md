### 1. Situation Name : At a Train Station – Buying Tickets & Finding the Platform

### 2. Conversation

*Setting: A busy train station. Tom needs to buy a ticket to a nearby city and find his platform. He speaks with a ticket agent (Clara) and then asks a station attendant (Marta) for directions.*

| Speaker                 | Dialogue                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Ticket Agent (Clara)** | Good morning! Welcome to Central Station. How can I help you?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Tom**                 | Hi. I need to buy a one-way ticket to Brighton, please.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Clara**               | Sure. Are you looking for a standard ticket or a first-class ticket? First-class gives you larger seats and complimentary drinks.                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Tom**                 | Just standard, please.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Clara**               | That'll be $28.50. Do you have a railcard? We offer discounts for students, seniors, and families.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Tom**                 | No, I don't. I'm just visiting.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Clara**               | No problem. That comes to $28.50. How would you like to pay – cash or card?                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Tom**                 | Card, please. *(pays)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Clara**               | Here's your ticket. Your train departs from Platform 9 at 10:15 AM. It's a direct train, and the journey takes about 1 hour and 20 minutes.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Tom**                 | Great. Is there a specific carriage I should go to?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Clara**               | Any carriage is fine for standard class. However, you'll notice the first-class carriages are marked with a yellow stripe near the doors. For standard class, just board anywhere in the rear carriages.                                                                                         |
| **Tom**                 | Perfect. And where can I find Platform 9?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Clara**               | Walk straight through the main concourse, take the escalator up to the footbridge, and then Platform 9 is on the left side. There are digital displays showing the train times.                                                                                                                  |
| **Tom**                 | Thanks. Just one more thing – are there restrooms and a café on the platform?                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Clara**               | Yes. There's a small café at the top of the escalator, and there are toilets near the end of the platform.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Tom**                 | Perfect. Thanks for your help!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

| **Tom**                 | Actually, yes. I'm a little confused. The digital board shows the train for Brighton but it says "Delayed." So I can sit anywhere?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Marta**               | Yes, anywhere in standard class. Just avoid the seats marked "Reserved" with a paper sign – those are for passengers who booked in advance.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Tom**                 | Got it. Thank you. I'll just grab a coffee and wait near the board.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Enjoy your trip to Brighton!                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

### 3. Vocabulary and Expressions

| Word/Phrase                         | Part of Speech | Meaning                                                                                             | Example Sentence                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **One-way ticket**                  | Noun phrase    | A ticket for travel to a destination without a return trip                                         | *I need a one-way ticket to Manchester, please.*                                                    |
| **Return ticket / Round-trip**      | Noun phrase    | A ticket for travel to a destination and back (in some countries, "return" is used)                | *I bought a return ticket because I'm coming back tomorrow.*                                        |
| **Standard class**                  | Noun phrase    | The regular, more affordable seating option on a train                                            | *Standard class seats are comfortable for short journeys.*                                          |
| **First class**                     | Noun phrase    | The premium, more expensive seating with extra amenities                                           | *First class passengers get complimentary snacks and more legroom.*                                 |
| **Railcard**                        | Noun           | A discount card that gives reduced fare for certain groups (students, seniors)                     | *With a student railcard, you save 30% on train tickets.*                                           |
| **Journey**                         | Noun           | The act of traveling from one place to another                                                     | *The journey takes about 90 minutes.*                                                               |
| **Platform**                        | Noun           | The raised area beside the train track where passengers board the train                            | *Your train departs from Platform 3.*                                                              |
| **Carriage**                        | Noun           | A separate section or coach of the train                                                           | *First-class carriages are at the front of the train.*                                              |
| **Concourse**                       | Noun           | A large open area inside a train station or airport where passengers walk through                  | *Meet me at the main concourse near the ticket machines.*                                           |
| **Footbridge**                      | Noun           | A bridge that allows pedestrians to cross over tracks or roads                                     | *Take the footbridge to reach the platforms on the other side.*                                     |
| **Digital display / Information board** | Noun phrase | An electronic screen showing train times, platforms, and delays                                    | *Check the digital display for the latest departure updates.*                                       |
| **Delayed**                         | Adjective      | Later than scheduled                                                                               | *The train is delayed due to bad weather.*                                                          |
| **Reserved seat**                   | Noun phrase    | A seat that has been booked in advance by a specific passenger                                     | *Look for the "Reserved" sign on the seat before sitting down.*                                     |
| **Announcement**                    | Noun           | A public spoken message, usually over a loudspeaker                                                | *The station made an announcement about a change of platform.*                                      |
| **Restrooms / Toilets**             | Noun           | Public bathrooms                                                                                   | *There are restrooms near the entrance of the station.*                                             |
| **Café / Coffee shop**              | Noun           | A small place where you can buy drinks and snacks                                                  | *I'll grab a coffee from the café while I wait.*                                                    |
| **Approaching**                     | Verb (present participle) | Coming closer (used for trains, buses, etc.)                               | *The train is approaching Platform 9.*                                                              |

### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"I need to buy a [one-way/return] ticket to [destination]."* – Stating your ticket need.
  - *"Are you looking for standard or first class?"* – Offering seating options.
  - *"Do you have a railcard?"* – Asking about discounts.
  - *"Your train departs from Platform [number] at [time]."* – Giving essential travel details.
  - *"The journey takes about [time]."* – Sharing travel duration.
  - *"Is there a [restroom/café] on the platform?"* – Asking about facilities.
  - *"The train is running [time] late / is delayed."* – Reporting a delay.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"I'm just visiting."* – Explaining why you don't have a railcard.
  - *"No problem."* – A standard, friendly response.
  - *"Definitely not."* – A reassuring answer to a worry.
  - *"Got it."* – Acknowledge understanding.
  - *"Enjoy your trip!"* – A common, warm wish when someone travels.
  - *"Don't worry."* – Reassuring someone who is anxious.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Be clear about your ticket type** – Say *"one-way"* or *"return"* upfront to avoid confusion. If you're unsure, ask: *"What's the difference in price?"* or *"Can I change the return date if I buy a return ticket?"*
  2. **Always check the platform number and time** – Train schedules can change quickly. Confirm details with the clerk: *"So it's Platform 9 at 10:15 – correct?"* and then verify on the digital board before heading to the platform.
  3. **Ask about delays early** – If you see a delay, ask an attendant: *"Is this train delayed? When will it arrive?"* They can give you the most up-to-date information.
  4. **Know where to find amenities** – Asking about restrooms and food is practical. Say: *"Is there a café or a restroom nearby?"* Also, check if there's a waiting room if the delay is long.
  5. **Understand seating rules** – If you don't have a reserved seat, ask: *"Can I sit anywhere in standard class?"* Also, look for signs like *"Reserved"* or *"First Class only"* to avoid getting into the wrong section.
