## Topic: The Strength to Keep Going

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I feel like giving up. Everything feels so hard right now. I don't know if I have the strength to keep going. |
| **Alexa** | I'm so sorry you're feeling this way. It's okay to feel tired. It's okay to feel like you're at your limit. |
| **Alexis** | I keep trying, but nothing seems to get easier. I feel like I'm running in place. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a heavy place to be. But the fact that you're still here, still trying, is a sign of strength. |
| **Alexis** | I don't feel strong. I feel broken. |
| **Alexa** | You're not broken—you're human. And even when you feel like you're falling apart, you're still holding on. |
| **Alexis** | What if I can't do this anymore? What if I don't have anything left? |
| **Alexa** | Then you rest. And when you've rested, you take one small step. That's all you need to do. |
| **Alexis** | I've been trying to do everything at once. Maybe that's why I'm so exhausted. |
| **Alexa** | That's the trap. You don't have to fix everything today. You just have to do the next right thing. |
| **Alexis** | What is the next right thing? |
| **Alexa** | Maybe it's just getting out of bed. Maybe it's drinking a glass of water. Maybe it's calling a friend. |
| **Alexis** | That sounds so small. |
| **Alexa** | It is. But small things add up. And they keep you moving forward. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so focused on how far I have to go that I forgot to look at how far I've come. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful shift. You've already survived so much. You're stronger than you think. |
| **Alexis** | I want to believe that. I want to believe I can keep going. |
| **Alexa** | You can. And you don't have to do it alone. I'm right here with you. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I can take the next step. |
| **Alexa** | That's all you need to do. One step, and then another. You've got this. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Giving up** | Phrasal Verb | Stopping trying; quitting |
| **At your limit** | Phrase | Having reached the maximum you can handle |
| **Running in place** | Phrase | Making effort but not making progress |
| **Heavy** (place to be) | Adjective | Emotionally difficult and burdensome |
| **Broken** (feeling) | Adjective | Feeling emotionally shattered or deeply hurt |
| **Holding on** | Phrasal Verb | Continuing to cope despite difficulty |
| **Exhausted** | Adjective | Extremely tired and drained |
| **Trap** (of overdoing) | Noun / Metaphor | A situation that seems productive but leads to burnout |
| **Next right thing** | Phrase | The next small, positive step you can take |
| **Add up** | Phrasal Verb | Accumulate; become significant over time |
| **Shift** (in perspective) | Noun | A change in the way you view things |
| **Survived** | Verb | Made it through a difficult experience |
| **Believe** (in yourself) | Verb | To have confidence in your own abilities |
| **Take the next step** | Phrase | To move forward, even in a small way |
| **You've got this** | Phrase / Slang | You can do it; I believe in you |
