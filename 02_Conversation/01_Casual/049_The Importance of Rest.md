## Topic: The Importance of Rest

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been going non-stop for weeks. I thought if I just kept pushing, I'd eventually catch up. But I'm more exhausted than ever. |
| **Alexa** | That's the thing about burnout. Pushing harder doesn't fix it. Rest isn't a reward—it's a requirement. |
| **Alexis** | I feel so guilty when I rest. Like I'm being lazy or wasting time. |
| **Alexa** | I used to feel that way too. But I've learned that rest is part of the work. Your brain and body need time to recover. |
| **Alexis** | I've been treating rest as optional. But clearly it's not, because I'm falling apart. |
| **Alexa** | That's the wake-up call. You can't sustain that pace forever. Something has to give. |
| **Alexis** | What does rest actually look like for you? |
| **Alexa** | It's not just sleeping. It's moments of genuine pause—no screens, no obligations, just being. |
| **Alexis** | I don't even know how to do that anymore. I'm always multitasking or thinking about the next thing. |
| **Alexa** | Start small. Set aside ten minutes tomorrow where you just sit. No phone, no music, no agenda. |
| **Alexis** | Just sit? That sounds so uncomfortable. |
| **Alexa** | It can be at first. But you'll learn to appreciate the stillness. |
| **Alexis** | I need to stop viewing rest as unproductive. It's what allows me to be productive in the long run. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. Rest restores your energy, sharpens your focus, and helps you show up as your best self. |
| **Alexis** | I've been running on adrenaline for so long. I don't even know what being rested feels like. |
| **Alexa** | You'll remember. It might take time, but your body will thank you. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to schedule rest into my week like I would any other commitment. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful step. Treat rest as non-negotiable. |
| **Alexis** | I also need to stop checking emails late at night. That's part of rest too. |
| **Alexa** | Boundaries around work and technology are part of rest. Protect your evenings. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to start tonight. No work after 8 PM. |
| **Alexa** | I'm proud of you. You deserve to feel restored. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I think I've been forgetting that I'm human. |
| **Alexa** | We all do sometimes. But you're taking a step back toward balance. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Non-stop** | Adjective | Without pausing or stopping |
| **Pushing through** | Phrasal Verb | Continuing despite difficulty or fatigue |
| **Exhausted** | Adjective | Extremely tired and drained |
| **Burnout** | Noun | A state of physical and emotional exhaustion from overwork |
| **Requirement** | Noun | Something that is necessary and not optional |
| **Guilty** | Adjective | Feeling regret or responsibility for something |
| **Lazy** | Adjective | Unwilling to work or use energy |
| **Genuine pause** | Phrase | A true, intentional break from activity |
| **Obligations** | Noun (plural) | Things you are required or expected to do |
| **Multitasking** | Verb | Doing several things at the same time |
| **Stillness** | Noun | A quiet, calm state without movement |
| **Unproductive** | Adjective | Not achieving anything useful or valuable |
| **Restores your energy** | Verb Phrase | Brings your energy back to full strength |
| **Sharpens** (your focus) | Verb | Makes your ability to concentrate stronger |
| **Adrenaline** | Noun | A hormone that gives you a burst of energy |
| **Non-negotiable** | Adjective | Absolutely necessary and not up for discussion |
| **Boundaries** | Noun (plural) | Limits you set to protect your well-being |
| **Restored** | Adjective | Brought back to a state of energy and well-being |
| **Balance** | Noun | A state where different elements are in harmony |
