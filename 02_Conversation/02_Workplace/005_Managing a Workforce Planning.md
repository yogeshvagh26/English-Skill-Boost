# Topic: Managing a Workforce Planning / Talent Forecasting Session

**Scenario:** You are the Head of Talent Strategy at a rapidly growing technology company. The company has grown 40% year-over-year and is projected to double in size over the next 3 years. The CEO has asked you to lead a workforce planning session with department heads (Engineering, Sales, Marketing, Product, and Operations) to: **forecast** talent needs for the next 18 months, **identify** critical skill gaps, **align** hiring plans with budget and revenue projections, **address** retention risks, and **develop** a talent acquisition and development strategy. You need to balance ambitious growth goals with financial realism and ensure every department has the talent it needs to execute its strategy.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **You** | *(opening the planning session)* Thank you all for joining. We're here to plan for our most critical asset—our people. Over the next 18 months, we expect to double in size. That means we need to be intentional about who we hire, where we invest, and how we develop talent. I want us to leave this session with a clear, actionable workforce plan. |
| **CEO** | Growth is our top priority, but we also need to be financially disciplined. We can't hire everyone—we need to prioritize. |
| **You** | Exactly. We'll start by mapping our strategic objectives to our talent needs. Then we'll identify the most critical roles and skills, and build a hiring and development plan around them. |
| **Engineering Lead** | We're already struggling to find senior engineers. The market is competitive. We need to expand our talent sourcing and improve our employer brand. |
| **You** | Good point. Let's include employer branding and sourcing strategy in the plan. We'll also explore alternative talent pools—like returning professionals, apprenticeships, and remote talent. |
| **Sales Lead** | My team will double in size over the next 12 months. We need a scalable onboarding program and a clear career progression path. |
| **You** | We'll design a sales academy to accelerate onboarding and ramp-up time. We'll also define promotion criteria and development paths for each role. |
| **Marketing Lead** | We're shifting to a more data-driven marketing approach. We need to upskill our existing team in analytics and hire a data scientist. |
| **You** | We'll invest in upskilling programs and add a data scientist role to the hiring plan. I'll work with you to define the competencies and the timeline. |
| **Operations Lead** | We need to improve our operational efficiency. We're looking to automate processes and hire a process improvement manager. |
| **You** | I'll add that role to the plan and work with Operations to define the scope and timeline. |
| **Finance Lead** | What's the total headcount growth we're projecting? And what's the budget impact? |
| **You** | Based on our current projections, we're looking at 150 net new hires over the next 18 months. That's a significant investment, but it's aligned with our revenue targets. I'll provide a detailed budget breakdown in the final plan. |
| **You** | *(closing)* To summarize, we have a clear growth trajectory, prioritized roles, a focus on employer branding and upskilling, and a budget framework. I'll consolidate this into a workforce plan with owners, timelines, and success metrics. Thank you for your input and commitment. |
| *(3 months later – progress review)* | |
| **You** | *(to the department heads)* We're on track with our hiring plan. We've already filled 30 of the 150 roles, and we've launched the sales academy and upskilling programs. Retention is up 8% year-over-year. |
| **Engineering Lead** | The employer branding initiatives are working—we're seeing more quality applicants. |
| **Sales Lead** | The sales academy has cut ramp-up time by 20%. |
| **You** | This is a great start. Let's keep the momentum and continue to refine our approach. |

## Vocabulary

| **Word / Phrase** | **Part of Speech** | **Meaning** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Workforce planning** | Noun Phrase | The process of forecasting talent needs and developing plans to meet them. |
| **Talent forecasting** | Noun Phrase | Predicting future talent requirements based on business strategy. |
| **Headcount growth** | Noun Phrase | The increase in the number of employees. |
| **Critical skill gaps** | Noun Phrase | Missing competencies needed to achieve business goals. |
| **Employer branding** | Noun Phrase | The perception of a company as a place to work. |
| **Talent pools** | Noun Phrase | Groups of potential candidates for future hiring. |
| **Onboarding program** | Noun Phrase | A structured process for integrating new employees. |
| **Ramp-up time** | Noun Phrase | The time it takes for a new employee to become productive. |
| **Career progression path** | Noun Phrase | A clear route for advancement within the organization. |
| **Upskilling** | Noun | Training employees to develop new skills. |
| **Hiring plan** | Noun Phrase | A detailed roadmap for recruitment activities. |
| **Budget framework** | Noun Phrase | A financial structure for allocating resources. |
| **Success metrics** | Noun Phrase | Measures of achievement and progress. |
| **Revenue targets** | Noun Phrase | Financial goals related to income generation. |
| **Talent acquisition** | Noun Phrase | The process of attracting and hiring employees. |
| **Talent development** | Noun Phrase | Programs and activities to improve employee skills. |
| **Process improvement** | Noun Phrase | Efforts to make operations more efficient. |
| **Scalable** | Adjective | Able to grow without losing efficiency. |
| **Remote talent** | Noun Phrase | Employees who work from outside the office. |
| **Retention** | Noun | The ability to keep employees over time. |
