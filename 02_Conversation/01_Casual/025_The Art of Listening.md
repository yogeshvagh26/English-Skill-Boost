## Topic: The Art of Listening

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been trying to be a better listener lately. I realize I often just wait for my turn to talk instead of really hearing people. |
| **Alexa** | That's such an honest observation. Most of us are guilty of that. |
| **Alexis** | I had a conversation with a friend yesterday where I just let her talk without interrupting. She thanked me afterward. |
| **Alexa** | That says a lot. People rarely get the space to speak without being interrupted. |
| **Alexis** | I think we're so used to multitasking and quick replies that we forget to slow down and just be present. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. Listening isn't just hearing words. It's about understanding the emotion behind them. |
| **Alexis** | How do you practice active listening? |
| **Alexa** | I try to put my phone away, make eye contact, and nod or say small things like "mm-hmm" to show I'm following. |
| **Alexis** | That's helpful. I tend to jump in with advice too quickly. |
| **Alexa** | I used to do that too. But sometimes people don't want advice. They just want to feel heard. |
| **Alexis** | That's so true. I'm learning to ask, "Do you want me to listen, or do you want help?" before I respond. |
| **Alexa** | That's a game-changer. It gives the other person control over what they need. |
| **Alexis** | I feel like being a good listener also helps me understand people better. |
| **Alexa** | Yes! You learn so much about someone when you truly listen. |
| **Alexis** | And it builds trust. When people feel heard, they open up more. |
| **Alexa** | That's the foundation of any strong relationship. |
| **Alexis** | I also notice that when I listen well, I feel more connected. Less lonely. |
| **Alexa** | That's powerful. Listening is a gift you give to others and yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I want to make it a daily practice. Even in small conversations. |
| **Alexa** | Me too. Let's hold each other accountable. If one of us is interrupting, the other can gently point it out. |
| **Alexis** | I'd like that. Kindly, though. No judgment. |
| **Alexa** | Always. We're just helping each other grow. |
| **Alexis** | This conversation made me realize how much I still have to learn. |
| **Alexa** | We all do. But the fact that you're aware is already a big step. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you for always listening to me. |
| **Alexa** | You're so welcome. And thank you for always being a safe space for me too. |
| **Alexis** | We're a good team. |
| **Alexa** | The best. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Guilty of that** | Phrase | Responsible for or guilty of doing the same thing |
| **Rarely** | Adverb | Not often; seldom |
| **Interrupted** | Verb | Broke into someone's speech or activity |
| **Multitasking** | Verb | Doing several things at the same time |
| **Present** (for someone) | Adjective | Fully focused on the current moment and the person you're with |
| **Emotion behind them** | Phrase | The feelings that are hidden beneath the words |
| **Active listening** | Noun | A communication technique where you fully focus on the speaker |
| **Eye contact** | Noun | Looking directly into another person's eyes |
| **Jump in** (with advice) | Phrasal Verb | To interrupt or start speaking before someone finishes |
| **Feel heard** | Phrase | To feel understood and validated |
| **Game-changer** | Noun | Something that completely changes a situation |
| **Builds trust** | Phrase | Creates a feeling of safety and reliability |
| **Foundation** | Noun | The base or core on which something is built |
| **Gift** (to give) | Metaphor | Something valuable and kind that you offer freely |
| **Daily practice** | Phrase | An activity you do every day to improve a skill |
| **Hold each other accountable** | Phrase | To remind or encourage each other to follow through |
| **Gently** | Adverb | In a kind, careful, and not aggressive way |
| **No judgment** | Phrase | Accepting someone without criticism or blame |
| **Safe space** | Phrase | A place or relationship where you feel emotionally secure |
| **Team** (good team) | Metaphor | Two people who work well together and support each other |
