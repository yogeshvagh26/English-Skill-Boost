### 2. Conversation

*Setting: A modern office meeting room. Sarah (team lead), Mike (project manager), and Lisa (designer) are having a weekly project update meeting.*

| Speaker      | Dialogue                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Sarah**    | Good morning, everyone. Thanks for being on time. Let's kick off this week's meeting. Mike, could you give us a quick update on the website project?                                                                                                                      |
| **Mike**     | Sure, Sarah. We're making good progress. The development team has completed about 80% of the front-end work. We're slightly behind schedule on the back-end integration, though.                                                                                       |
| **Sarah**    | Okay. What's causing the delay?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Mike**     | The main issue is that we're waiting on third-party API documentation. The vendor hasn't sent it yet, so we can't proceed with the payment gateway integration. I've already followed up twice via email.                                                                |
| **Sarah**    | That's frustrating. Let's escalate that. I'll reach out to their account manager today. In the meantime, can we shift focus to other tasks while we wait?                                                                                                                |
| **Mike**     | Absolutely. The team can start working on the user testing phase for the parts we've already completed. That way, we won't lose momentum.                                                                                                                                |
| **Sarah**    | Great idea. Lisa, what's your take on the new homepage design?                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Lisa**     | I've finished the first draft, but I'd love some feedback. I tried to make it more modern and mobile-friendly, as we discussed. I've shared the mockups in the project folder.                                                                                          |
| **Sarah**    | I took a quick look earlier. I really like the color scheme – it's fresh and on-brand. However, I think the call-to-action button could be more prominent. It's a bit easy to miss.                                                                                    |
| **Lisa**     | That's a fair point. I was worried it might look too aggressive if it's too big. But I can increase the size and change the color to a brighter shade to make it stand out.                                                                                             |
| **Mike**     | I agree with Sarah. The button needs to be more visible – that's our primary conversion point. Also, maybe we could add a short testimonial section below it? That might boost user trust.                                                                              |
| **Lisa**     | I like that suggestion. I'll incorporate both changes and send the updated version by end of day.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Sarah**    | Perfect. Before we wrap up, let's quickly review action items. Mike, you'll coordinate the user testing. Lisa, you'll revise the design. And I'll handle the vendor escalation. Let's touch base again on Thursday to check progress.                                    |
| **Mike**     | Sounds good. Just one more thing – we're also running low on our project budget. I think we need to request a budget increase for the next phase.                                                                                                                        |
| **Sarah**    | Noted. I'll prepare a budget proposal and present it to the finance team. Can you send me the projected costs by tomorrow morning?                                                                                                                                       |
| **Mike**     | Sure. I'll compile everything and send it over.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Sarah**    | Excellent. Any other issues or concerns?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Lisa**     | No, I'm all set.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Mike**     | Nothing from my side.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Sarah**    | Alright then, let's call it a day. Thanks for your hard work, everyone. See you on Thursday!                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **All**      | Thanks, Sarah! / See you!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"Let's kick off [meeting/project]."* – Starting a meeting or initiative.
  - *"Could you give us a quick update on [topic]?"* – Requesting progress information.
  - *"What's causing the delay?"* – Asking for the reason behind a slowdown.
  - *"I'd love some feedback on [work]."* – Politely requesting opinions or critique.
  - *"Let's review action items."* – Moving to the final, task-oriented phase of a meeting.
  - *"Let's touch base again on [day/time]."* – Scheduling a follow-up conversation.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"That's a fair point."* – Acknowledging someone's criticism or suggestion respectfully.
  - *"Great idea."* – Showing enthusiasm and agreement with a suggestion.
  - *"Noted."* – A professional way to say "I've made a mental note of that."
  - *"I'm all set."* – A way to say "I have everything I need" or "I'm ready."
  - *"Nothing from my side."* – Meaning "I have no issues or updates to share."
  - *"Thanks for your hard work."* – Showing appreciation to your team.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Use transitional phrases to structure meetings** – Use phrases like *"Let's kick off"* (start), *"Before we wrap up"* (transition to close), and *"Let's call it a day"* (end). This shows leadership and organization.
  2. **Give specific, constructive feedback** – Instead of saying *"This looks bad,"* say *"I think the call-to-action button could be more prominent because it's easy to miss."* Be specific and suggest a solution.
  3. **Practice active listening in meetings** – Use confirmations like *"Got it,"* *"Noted,"* and *"I agree"* to show you're engaged. Summarize others' points: *"So what you're saying is…"* to ensure understanding.
  4. **Know how to ask for clarification** – If you don't understand a technical term or instruction, say: *"Could you clarify what you mean by [term]?"* or *"Could you elaborate on that?"* It's better to ask than to do the wrong thing.
  5. **Use "we" instead of "I" to build teamwork** – Even when talking about your own tasks, frame it as *"I'll handle the vendor escalation"* but use *"we"* when discussing team goals: *"We need to maintain momentum."* This fosters collaboration.
