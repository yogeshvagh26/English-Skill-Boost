## Topic: The Power of Trust

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking about trust lately. It's such a fragile thing, isn't it? |
| **Alexa** | It really is. It takes so long to build and can be broken in an instant. |
| **Alexis** | I've had my trust broken before, and it made me build walls around myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's a natural response. When you've been hurt, you protect yourself. |
| **Alexis** | But I don't want to live behind walls forever. |
| **Alexa** | That's the tricky part. You have to find the balance between being open and staying safe. |
| **Alexis** | How do you learn to trust again after being betrayed? |
| **Alexa** | Slowly. You don't give someone your full trust immediately. You let them earn it over time. |
| **Alexis** | That makes sense. Trust should be earned, not freely given. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. And you can start with small steps. See how they handle small things before you trust them with bigger ones. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been too trusting in the past, and too guarded now. |
| **Alexa** | That's a common pendulum swing. You'll find your middle ground. |
| **Alexis** | What about trusting yourself? I don't always trust my own judgment. |
| **Alexa** | That's so important too. When you trust yourself, you're less afraid of being let down by others. |
| **Alexis** | How do I build self-trust? |
| **Alexa** | Start by keeping small promises to yourself. If you say you'll do something, follow through. |
| **Alexis** | That's simple but powerful. I tend to break promises to myself all the time. |
| **Alexa** | We all do. But each time you keep one, you build evidence that you're reliable. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be someone I can trust. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful goal. And it starts with small, consistent actions. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to start today. Just one small promise to myself. |
| **Alexa** | I'm so proud of you. Trust starts within. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm finally healing. |
| **Alexa** | You are. And you're doing the work. That's what matters. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Fragile** | Adjective | Easily broken or damaged |
| **Build walls** (metaphor) | Phrase / Verb | To emotionally protect yourself from being hurt |
| **Betrayed** | Verb | To have someone break your trust or be disloyal |
| **Tricky** (balance) | Adjective | Difficult to navigate or handle |
| **Open** (emotionally) | Adjective | Willing to share feelings and be vulnerable |
| **Safe** (emotionally) | Adjective | Not at risk of being hurt or harmed |
| **Earn** (trust) | Verb | To deserve or gain something through effort and actions |
| **Freely given** (trust) | Phrase | Given without hesitation or caution |
| **Guarded** | Adjective | Emotionally closed off and protective |
| **Pendulum swing** | Noun / Metaphor | Moving between two opposite extremes |
| **Judgment** | Noun | The ability to make good decisions and assessments |
| **Self-trust** | Noun | Confidence and belief in your own abilities and decisions |
| **Follow through** | Phrasal Verb | To complete something you committed to doing |
| **Reliable** | Adjective | Trustworthy; able to be depended on |
| **Healing** | Noun / Verb | The process of becoming emotionally healthy again |
| **Doing the work** | Phrase | Putting in effort to improve yourself or a situation |
