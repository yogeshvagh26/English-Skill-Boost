## Topic: Learning to Give Without Losing Yourself

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been giving so much to others lately that I feel like I've lost parts of myself. I'm tired, but I don't know how to stop. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a common struggle. When we give too much, we can end up feeling depleted and resentful. |
| **Alexis** | But I want to be a good friend, a good person. How can I stop giving without being selfish? |
| **Alexa** | There's a difference between giving from love and giving out of obligation. One fills you up, the other drains you. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been giving out of guilt and fear of letting people down. |
| **Alexa** | That's the trap. Giving from an empty cup doesn't help anyone—it only leads to burnout. |
| **Alexis** | I want to learn to give in a way that's sustainable. I want to be there for others without losing myself. |
| **Alexa** | That starts with setting boundaries. You can say yes to what matters and no to what doesn't. |
| **Alexis** | But what if people are disappointed? |
| **Alexa** | Disappointment is okay. You're not responsible for everyone's expectations. You're responsible for your own well-being. |
| **Alexis** | I've always been the one everyone leans on. It's hard to let go of that role. |
| **Alexa** | You can still be a support system, but it doesn't have to cost you your peace. |
| **Alexis** | How do I know when I'm giving too much? |
| **Alexa** | When you start feeling exhausted, resentful, or like you're not being seen. That's a sign. |
| **Alexis** | I've been feeling all of those things lately. |
| **Alexa** | Then it's time to pause. Ask yourself: "What do I need right now?" and honor that. |
| **Alexis** | I need rest. I need time to breathe. I need to reconnect with myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's beautiful. Giving yourself permission to rest is a gift to everyone you care about. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so focused on others that I forgot I matter too. |
| **Alexa** | You do matter. And you can't pour from an empty cup. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to start saying no more often, without guilt. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful step. You're learning to give from fullness, not emptiness. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm finally putting myself back into the picture. |
| **Alexa** | You always belonged in it. You just needed permission to stay. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Depleted** | Adjective | Having your energy or resources used up; feeling empty |
| **Resentful** | Adjective | Feeling bitterness or anger because of unfair treatment |
| **Selfish** | Adjective | Caring only about yourself, not others |
| **Obligation** | Noun | A duty or commitment that you feel required to do |
| **Guilt** | Noun | A feeling of responsibility for something wrong |
| **Letting people down** | Phrase / Verb | Disappointing or failing to meet someone's expectations |
| **Trap** (giving from guilt) | Noun / Metaphor | A situation that seems good but leads to problems |
| **Empty cup** (metaphor) | Phrase | Having nothing left to give emotionally |
| **Burnout** | Noun | A state of physical and emotional exhaustion from overwork |
| **Sustainable** (giving) | Adjective | Able to continue over time without harming yourself |
| **Boundaries** | Noun (plural) | Limits you set to protect your well-being |
| **Expectations** | Noun (plural) | Beliefs about what will happen in the future |
| **Well-being** | Noun | The state of being healthy and happy |
| **Support system** | Phrase / Noun | People who provide emotional or practical help |
| **Peace** | Noun | A state of mental calm and quiet |
| **Exhausted** | Adjective | Extremely tired and drained |
| **Honor** (that need) | Verb | To respect and act on something important |
| **Permission** (to rest) | Noun | The freedom to allow yourself to do something |
| **Pour from an empty cup** | Idiom / Phrase | To give to others when you have nothing left to give |
| **From fullness** | Phrase | Giving from a place of abundance and health |
