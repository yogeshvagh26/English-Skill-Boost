## Topic: The Power of Listening to Your Inner Voice

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been feeling so confused lately. I keep asking everyone for advice, but I feel like the answers are already inside me. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful realization. We often look outside ourselves for guidance, but our inner voice already knows the way. |
| **Alexis** | But how do I know if it's my inner voice or just fear or wishful thinking? |
| **Alexa** | That takes practice. Your inner voice is usually calm and steady. Fear is loud and urgent. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been ignoring that calm voice because I didn't trust it. |
| **Alexa** | I understand. But your inner wisdom is built on all your experiences, feelings, and truths. It's worth listening to. |
| **Alexis** | I've been so used to outsourcing my decisions to others that I forgot I have my own compass. |
| **Alexa** | That's the perfect word—a compass. Your inner voice is always pointing you toward what's true for you. |
| **Alexis** | I want to start trusting it more. Even when it's uncomfortable. |
| **Alexa** | Start with small decisions. Choose something without asking anyone else. See how it feels. |
| **Alexis** | Like what? |
| **Alexa** | Like what to eat, what to wear, or how to spend your free time. Practice listening to yourself. |
| **Alexis** | That sounds so simple, but it's a big step for me. |
| **Alexa** | It is. But each time you trust yourself, you strengthen that muscle. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to trust my feelings more. I often dismiss them as irrational. |
| **Alexa** | Feelings aren't irrational—they're information. They tell you what you need and what matters to you. |
| **Alexis** | I've been so good at ignoring my feelings that I don't even know what I feel sometimes. |
| **Alexa** | That's where stillness comes in. When you stop distracting yourself, your feelings can finally speak. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been afraid of what I might hear. |
| **Alexa** | That's a real fear. But what you'll hear is your own truth. And your truth is always kinder than the noise. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to try to sit with myself more often. To listen without judgment. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. And I'll be here to remind you that you already have the answers. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm finally learning to trust myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's the most important trust you'll ever build. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Inner voice** | Noun / Phrase | Your internal intuition, wisdom, and personal truth |
| **Wishful thinking** | Noun / Phrase | Believing something is true because you want it to be |
| **Compass** (metaphor) | Noun | Something that guides you in the right direction |
| **Outsourcing** (decisions) | Verb | Asking others to decide for you instead of trusting yourself |
| **Muscle** (trusting as) | Metaphor | A skill you can strengthen with practice |
| **Rationally** (dismissing feelings) | Adverb | Judging emotions as not logical |
| **Information** (feelings as) | Noun | Useful data that tells you something about your needs |
| **Stillness** | Noun | A quiet, calm state without movement or distraction |
| **Distracting yourself** | Verb Phrase | Filling your time with noise to avoid your own thoughts |
| **Sit with myself** | Phrase | To be alone with your own mind and feelings without distraction |
| **No judgment** | Phrase | Without criticism or evaluation |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
