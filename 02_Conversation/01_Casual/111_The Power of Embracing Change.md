## Topic: The Power of Embracing Change

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been feeling so unsettled lately. Everything around me is shifting—work, relationships, even my own sense of who I am. |
| **Alexa** | That sounds really overwhelming. Change can be so disorienting, even when it's for the better. |
| **Alexis** | I keep trying to hold onto how things used to be. I want things to go back to how they were before. |
| **Alexa** | I understand that pull. But when we cling to the past, we miss the opportunities that the present is offering us. |
| **Alexis** | I know I need to let go. But it's so hard. |
| **Alexa** | Letting go doesn't mean you forget or stop caring. It means you accept what is, and you trust that you'll find your footing in the new. |
| **Alexis** | I've been so afraid of losing what I had that I haven't been able to see what could be. |
| **Alexa** | That's a common trap. But change isn't always a loss—sometimes it's a redirection toward something better. |
| **Alexis** | I want to believe that. I want to be able to trust the unknown. |
| **Alexa** | You can start by taking it one day at a time. You don't have to have the whole path mapped out. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been trying to control everything because I'm scared of the uncertainty. |
| **Alexa** | It's natural to want control. But the truth is, we can't control life—we can only respond to it. |
| **Alexis** | So maybe I need to work on how I respond instead of trying to stop the change from happening. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. You can't stop the waves, but you can learn to surf them. |
| **Alexis** | I like that. Surfing the waves of change. |
| **Alexa** | It takes practice. But each time you ride a wave, you get a little more confident. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so focused on the fear that I forgot to look for the opportunity. |
| **Alexa** | There's always an opportunity. Sometimes it's just hidden under the fear. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to try to look for the hidden opportunity in this change. Even if it's small. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. And I'll be here to help you look. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm starting to see the door opening. |
| **Alexa** | That's the beauty of embracing change. When you stop fighting it, you start seeing the doors. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Unsettled** | Adjective | Feeling uneasy or not at peace |
| **Disorienting** | Adjective | Making you feel confused or lost |
| **Cling to** (the past) | Phrasal Verb | To hold onto something too tightly, refusing to let go |
| **Let go** (of the past) | Phrasal Verb | To release emotional attachment to what was |
| **Footing** (in the new) | Noun | A stable position or sense of security |
| **Redirect** (toward something better) | Noun / Verb | A change in direction that leads to something positive |
| **Map out** (the path) | Phrasal Verb | To plan every detail in advance |
| **Surf the waves** (metaphor) | Phrase | To adapt and move with change rather than against it |
| **Opportunity** (hidden) | Noun | A chance for something good or beneficial |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Embrace** (change) | Verb | To accept something willingly and enthusiastically |
