## Topic: The Art of Self-Discovery

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking a lot about who I really am. I feel like I've been playing a role for so long that I lost touch with myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a powerful thing to admit. So many of us go through life wearing masks without even realizing it. |
| **Alexis** | I've been so busy being what others expect me to be that I never asked myself what I actually want. |
| **Alexa** | That's a common journey. But the good news is, it's never too late to start discovering yourself. |
| **Alexis** | How do I even begin? I feel like I don't know where to start. |
| **Alexa** | Start by asking yourself simple questions. What makes you feel alive? What do you love doing when no one is watching? |
| **Alexis** | I love reading and writing, and I feel most myself when I'm in nature. |
| **Alexa** | Those are clues. They're showing you who you really are beneath the roles. |
| **Alexis** | I've been ignoring those parts of me because they didn't seem "productive." |
| **Alexa** | That's the trap of productivity culture. But joy and self-expression are productive too—they nourish your soul. |
| **Alexis** | I want to stop hiding. I want to let the real me come out. |
| **Alexa** | That takes courage. But each time you choose to be yourself, you're honoring who you truly are. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been afraid that if I show my true self, people won't like me. |
| **Alexa** | That's a valid fear. But the people who are meant to be in your life will love the real you. |
| **Alexis** | I'm starting to realize that living authentically is more important than being liked by everyone. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful realization. Authenticity attracts the right people and repels the wrong ones. |
| **Alexis** | I want to live more honestly. I want to stop pretending. |
| **Alexa** | That's a brave intention. Start with one small act of honesty today. Even if it's just with yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I think I'm ready. I'm ready to meet myself. |
| **Alexa** | You're about to meet the most important person you'll ever know. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Self-discovery** | Noun | The process of learning about your own character, values, and desires |
| **Playing a role** | Phrase / Verb | Acting in a way that isn't your true self |
| **Masks** (metaphor) | Noun (plural) | False appearances you show the world |
| **Clues** | Noun (plural) | Signs or hints that help you understand something |
| **Productive** (trap) | Adjective | Focused on achievement, often at the expense of joy |
| **Nourish** (your soul) | Verb | To provide what is needed for emotional and spiritual health |
| **Courage** | Noun | The ability to face fear and difficulty |
| **Honoring** (yourself) | Verb | Showing respect and appreciation for who you are |
| **Authentically** | Adverb | In a way that is true to your real self |
| **Authenticity** | Noun | The quality of being real and genuine |
| **Repels** (wrong ones) | Verb | Drives away or pushes back |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Honesty** (with yourself) | Noun | The quality of being truthful, even about hard things |
| **Meet myself** | Metaphor / Phrase | To connect with your true self and understand who you are |
