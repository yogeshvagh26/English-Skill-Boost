### 2. Conversation

*Setting: A traveler (Mr. Chen) arrives at a city hotel. The receptionist (Emma) greets him at the front desk.*

| Speaker           | Dialogue                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Receptionist**  | Good afternoon, sir. Welcome to Grand Central Hotel. How may I assist you today?                                                                                                   |
| **Mr. Chen**      | Good afternoon. I have a reservation under the name Chen.                                                                                                                          |
| **Receptionist**  | Certainly. Let me pull that up for you. … Yes, I see it here. A deluxe single room for three nights, is that correct?                                                              |
| **Mr. Chen**      | That's right.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Receptionist**  | Perfect. Could I please see your passport and a credit card for the security deposit?                                                                                              |
| **Mr. Chen**      | Here you are.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Receptionist**  | Thank you. We'll just make a copy. Are you traveling for business or leisure?                                                                                                      |
| **Mr. Chen**      | Business. I have meetings downtown tomorrow.                                                                                                                                       |
| **Receptionist**  | I see. Well, we have complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the hotel. The password is on your key card envelope.                                                               |
| **Mr. Chen**      | Great. And what time is breakfast served?                                                                                                                                          |
| **Receptionist**  | Breakfast is from 6:30 to 10:00 AM in the Garden View Restaurant on the ground floor. It's a buffet style.                                                                         |
| **Mr. Chen**      | Excellent.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Receptionist**  | Your room number is 804. Here's your key card. The elevators are just to your left. Check-out time is 11:00 AM. Would you like a wake-up call?                                    |
| **Mr. Chen**      | Yes, please. Could I get a wake-up call at 7:00 AM?                                                                                                                                |
| **Receptionist**  | Absolutely, I've set that for 7:00 AM. Is there anything else I can help with – luggage assistance, restaurant reservations, or taxi arrangements?                                 |
| **Mr. Chen**      | Actually, yes. Could you book me a taxi for 8:30 AM tomorrow morning?                                                                                                              |
| **Receptionist**  | Of course. I'll arrange that for you. The taxi will be waiting at the main entrance.                                                                                               |
| **Mr. Chen**      | Perfect. Thank you very much.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Receptionist**  | You're most welcome. Enjoy your stay, Mr. Chen. If you need anything, just dial 0 from your room.                                                                                  |
| **Mr. Chen**      | I will. Thanks!                                                                                                                                                                    |

### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"I have a reservation under the name [Name]."* – Stating your booking at check-in.
  - *"Could I please see your [document]?"* – Polite request for identification or payment.
  - *"What time is [service] served / available?"* – Asking about hotel facilities.
  - *"Could you [action] for me?"* – Polite way to request a service (e.g., book a taxi).

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"Let me pull that up for you."* – Used when staff access your information on a computer.
  - *"Is that correct?"* – Confirming details to avoid mistakes.
  - *"You're most welcome."* – A more formal, polite version of "you're welcome."
  - *"Just dial 0."* – Simple instruction for contacting reception.
  - *"I'll arrange that for you."* – Reassuring the guest that their request will be handled.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Have documents ready** – At check-in, have your passport/ID and credit card in hand to make the process smooth and efficient.
  2. **Ask about amenities proactively** – Don't wait; ask about Wi-Fi, breakfast, gym hours, and check-out time upfront to save time later.
  3. **Use specific requests** – Instead of saying "wake me up," say *"Could I get a wake-up call at 7:00 AM?"* to be clear and exact.
  4. **Don't hesitate to ask for extras** – Hotels expect requests like taxi bookings or restaurant recommendations. Use *"Could you…?"* or *"Would it be possible to…?"* to sound polite.
