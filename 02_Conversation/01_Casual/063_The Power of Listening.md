## Topic: The Power of Listening

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been realizing lately that I'm not always a great listener. I hear people, but I don't always truly listen. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful realization. Listening is more than just hearing words—it's about being fully present with someone. |
| **Alexis** | I'm often thinking about what I'm going to say next instead of actually absorbing what the other person is saying. |
| **Alexa** | It's a common habit. But when you truly listen, you give the other person the gift of being seen and understood. |
| **Alexis** | I want to become a better listener. I want people to feel like they really matter when they talk to me. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. It starts with being quiet and curious, not just waiting for your turn to speak. |
| **Alexis** | I've started putting my phone away during conversations. It's amazing how much more I notice. |
| **Alexa** | That's a huge step. When you remove distractions, you can pick up on tone, body language, and the emotions behind the words. |
| **Alexis** | I think listening is also a form of respect. It says, "What you have to say matters." |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. And when people feel truly heard, they open up more. The connection deepens. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to practice listening to myself more. I often ignore my own needs and feelings. |
| **Alexa** | That's just as important. When you listen to yourself, you honor your own truth. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so focused on being heard that I forgot to listen. |
| **Alexa** | It's a balance. Listening to others and listening to yourself are both acts of love. |
| **Alexis** | I want to carry this intention into my daily life. One conversation at a time. |
| **Alexa** | I'll join you. We can practice listening more deeply—to each other and to ourselves. |
| **Alexis** | I'm grateful for this conversation. It's made me think differently about how I show up for people. |
| **Alexa** | That's the power of listening. It changes us, and it changes our relationships. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to try to be more present and less distracted when people speak to me. |
| **Alexa** | That's a gift you can give to everyone you meet. The gift of your full attention. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Fully present** | Phrase / Adjective | Completely focused on the current moment and the person you're with |
| **Absorbing** (what someone says) | Verb | Taking in and truly understanding someone's words |
| **Gift** (of being seen) | Metaphor | Something valuable that you offer freely |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Curious** | Adjective | Eager to learn and understand |
| **Distractions** | Noun (plural) | Things that take your attention away |
| **Tone** | Noun | The quality or attitude of someone's voice |
| **Body language** | Noun | Physical gestures and expressions that communicate feelings |
| **Honor** (your own truth) | Verb | To respect and act on what is true for you |
| **Balance** | Noun | A state where different elements are in harmony |
| **Acts of love** | Phrase | Actions that show care, kindness, and respect |
| **Carry this intention** | Phrase | To hold onto a purpose and apply it in daily life |
| **Show up** (for people) | Phrasal Verb | To be present, available, and engaged |
| **Full attention** | Phrase | Complete focus on someone or something |
