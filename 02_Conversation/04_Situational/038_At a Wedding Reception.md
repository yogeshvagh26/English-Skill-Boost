### 1. Situation Name : At a Wedding Reception – Attending as a Guest & Giving a Toast

---

### 2. Conversation

*Setting: A beautiful wedding reception at a hotel ballroom. Sam is a guest at his friend's wedding. He speaks with the coordinator, chats with another guest, and later gives a toast.*

| Speaker               | Dialogue                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Coordinator**       | Good evening! Welcome to the celebration. May I see your invitation, please?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Sam**               | Good evening! Here you are.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Coordinator**       | Thank you, sir. You're on Table 7, which is near the stage. There's a seating chart just inside the ballroom if you need help finding it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Sam**               | Great. Thank you.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| *(Sam enters the ballroom and finds his table)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Fellow Guest**      | Hi there! Are you a friend of the bride or the groom?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Sam**               | I'm a friend of the groom – we went to college together. What about you?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Fellow Guest**      | I'm the bride's cousin. I've known her since we were kids. It's wonderful to see everyone here celebrating.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Sam**               | Absolutely. The venue is stunning, and the decorations are beautiful.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Fellow Guest**      | I know! The bride did most of the planning herself. She has great taste.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Sam**               | That doesn't surprise me. She's always been detail-oriented.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| *(The coordinator comes to the table)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Coordinator**       | Excuse me, everyone. The dinner buffet will open in about 15 minutes. There will be a salad bar, a pasta station, a carving station with roast beef and turkey, and a dessert table with a variety of cakes and pastries. Please feel free to go up when we announce it.                                                                                                                       |
| **Sam**               | That sounds delicious. Thank you.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Fellow Guest**      | I heard the cake is a three-tier red velvet cake – the bride's favorite.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Sam**               | I'll definitely save room for that.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| *(After dinner, the DJ announces it's time for the toasts)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **DJ**                | Ladies and gentlemen, it's now time for the toasts. First, we'll hear from the father of the bride, followed by the best man, and then we'll open the floor to other guests who would like to say a few words.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| *(The father of the bride gives a heartfelt speech, followed by the best man. The DJ then speaks)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **DJ**                | Thank you to the father of the bride and the best man for those beautiful words. Now, we're opening the floor. Does anyone else want to say a few words?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Sam**               | *(raises his hand)* I'd like to say something, if I may.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **DJ**                | Please, come up to the microphone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| *(Sam walks up to the stage)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Sam**               | Good evening, everyone. For those who don't know me, I'm Sam, and I've been friends with the groom, Mark, for over ten years. When Mark told me he was going to propose to Emily, I wasn't surprised – he had been talking about her non-stop for months. He said he knew she was the one because she could make him laugh like no one else and she always saw the best in people.                                                                           |
| *(Sam pauses, smiling)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Sam**               | I've had the privilege of watching their relationship grow. I've seen how Mark lights up when Emily walks into a room. And I've seen how Emily brings out the best in Mark. Together, they're a team, a partnership, a perfect match. So to Mark and Emily – may your love be as endless as the ocean, as strong as the mountains, and as beautiful as this day we're celebrating. Please raise your glasses with me. To Mark and Emily! Cheers!                                                                               |
| **Crowd**             | Cheers!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| *(Sam returns to his table)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Fellow Guest**      | That was a beautiful toast, Sam! You really captured who they are.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Sam**               | Thank you. They deserve all the happiness in the world. It's a privilege to be here.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| *(The DJ announces the first dance)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **DJ**                | Now it's time for the couple's first dance as husband and wife. Please join me in welcoming Mark and Emily to the dance floor!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| *(The newlyweds dance, and then the DJ invites other guests to join)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **DJ**                | Everyone, please join us on the dance floor!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Sam**               | *(to fellow guest)* Would you like to dance?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Fellow Guest**      | I'd love to!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| *(Sam and the guest dance and enjoy the rest of the reception)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Sam** (later)       | *(approaching the couple)* Congratulations again, you two! It was a beautiful wedding.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Mark** (the groom)  | Thanks so much for coming, Sam. And that toast – it was perfect. It meant the world to us.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Emily** (the bride) | Yes, thank you! We're so happy you could celebrate with us.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Sam**               | I wouldn't have missed it for anything. Safe travels on your honeymoon!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Mark**              | Thank you, my friend. We'll see you soon!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"May I see your invitation, please?"* – Requesting entry verification.
  - *"You're on Table [number]."* – Directing guests to their seats.
  - *"The dinner buffet will open in about [time]."* – Announcing meal times.
  - *"It's now time for the toasts."* – Announcing the speech segment.
  - *"First, we'll hear from [speaker]."* – Introducing speakers.
  - *"Please raise your glasses with me."* – Inviting a toast.
  - *"Congratulations again, you two!"* – Offering congratulations to the couple.
  - *"I wouldn't have missed it for anything."* – Expressing gratitude and importance of attending.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"Absolutely."* – Enthusiastic agreement.
  - *"It's wonderful to see everyone here celebrating."* – Expressing joy about the gathering.
  - *"That doesn't surprise me."* – Showing you expected something.
  - *"I'll definitely save room for that."* – Expressing excitement about food.
  - *"Beautiful words."* – Complimenting a speech.
  - *"They deserve all the happiness in the world."* – A warm sentiment about the couple.
  - *"It meant the world to us."* – Expressing deep appreciation.
  - *"Safe travels!"* – A common wish for someone going on a trip.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Prepare a toast or speech** – If you're asked to speak at a wedding, keep it warm and appropriate. Say: *"I'd like to say a few words"* and share a short, positive memory. Avoid embarrassing stories, and always end by raising your glass.
  2. **Use proper introductions** – When giving a toast, introduce yourself to the crowd: *"For those who don't know me, I'm [name]."* Then share how you know the couple.
  3. **Follow the reception flow** – Weddings follow a schedule: cocktail hour, dinner, speeches, cake-cutting, first dance, and dancing. Listen for announcements like: *"The buffet is now open,"* *"It's time for the toasts,"* and *"Please join us on the dance floor."*
  4. **Be a gracious guest** – Compliment the venue, food, and decorations. Say: *"The venue is stunning!"* or *"The decorations are beautiful!"* It makes the couple feel appreciated.
  5. **Know how to give congratulations** – When you see the couple, say: *"Congratulations! The wedding was beautiful,"* and if appropriate, *"I'm so happy for you both."* Keep it warm and sincere.
  6. **Dance and have fun** – If the DJ invites people to the dance floor, don't be shy. You can say: *"Would you like to dance?"* to a fellow guest and enjoy the celebration.
  7. **Interact with other guests** – Ask: *"Are you a friend of the bride or the groom?"* This is a great icebreaker to learn how people know the couple and to make new connections.
