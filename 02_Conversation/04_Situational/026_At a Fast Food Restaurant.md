### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"I'd like a [number] combo, please."* – Ordering a set meal.
  - *"What size would you like – medium or large?"* – Offering size options.
  - *"What drink would you like with that?"* – Asking for beverage preference.
  - *"Would you like to add any extra toppings?"* – Upselling additional items.
  - *"Anything else?"* – Checking for additional orders.
  - *"That's an extra [amount]."* – Informing about additional charges.
  - *"Will that be for here or to go?"* – Asking about dining preference.
  - *"Your order number is [number]. It'll be ready in about [time]."* – Giving order details.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"I'm really hungry."* – Explaining why you want a large size.
  - *"Got it."* – Acknowledging understanding.
  - *"Sure, why not."* – Agreeing casually to an offer.
  - *"That's me!"* – Confirming that an order belongs to you.
  - *"No problem at all."* – A friendly response to an apology.
  - *"Thanks for the quick fix!"* – Appreciating a fast correction.
  - *"Enjoy your meal!"* – A common wish when serving food.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Know the menu numbers** – Fast food menus often list combo meals by number (e.g., #5). Say the number clearly: *"Number 5 combo, please."* If you don't know the number, describe the item: *"I'll have the double cheeseburger meal."*
  2. **Customize confidently** – Don't be afraid to ask for changes: *"No pickles"*, *"Extra cheese"*, *"Add bacon."* Also ask about sauce: *"Can I get barbecue sauce instead of ketchup?"* Most places are flexible.
  3. **Clarify size and drink** – When they ask about size, choose quickly: *"Medium"* or *"Large."* For the drink, know what they offer. If you're unsure, ask: *"What drinks do you have?"*
  4. **Be ready for upselling** – Cashiers will often ask if you want to add items (e.g., pie, milkshake). If you want it, say yes; if not, say: *"No, that's all."* or *"I think that's enough."*
  5. **Check your order before leaving** – After receiving your bag, check if everything is inside, especially sauces. If something is missing or wrong, say: *"I ordered barbecue sauce but got ketchup – could I get the right one?"* It's better to fix it before you leave.
  6. **Payment and rounding** – When they ask about rounding up for charity, you can say yes or no politely: *"Sure, round it up"* or *"No thanks, maybe next time."* It's a personal choice.
