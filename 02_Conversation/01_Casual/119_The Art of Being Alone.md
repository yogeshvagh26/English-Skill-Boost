## Topic: The Art of Being Alone

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been spending more time alone lately, and at first it felt uncomfortable. But now I'm starting to enjoy it. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful shift. Learning to be alone without feeling lonely is a skill that takes time to develop. |
| **Alexis** | I used to fill every quiet moment with noise—podcasts, TV, social media. I was afraid of the silence. |
| **Alexa** | That's so common. We often fill the silence because we're scared of what we might find in it. |
| **Alexis** | But when I finally sat with it, I realized the silence wasn't scary—it was peaceful. |
| **Alexa** | That's the gift of solitude. It gives you space to hear your own voice, your own thoughts, your own heart. |
| **Alexis** | I've started going for walks without my phone. Just me and the world. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful practice. When you're not distracted, you notice so much more. |
| **Alexis** | I also started journaling. I write down whatever comes to mind—no structure, no judgment. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a healthy outlet. Journaling helps you process your feelings and make sense of your experiences. |
| **Alexis** | I think I used to be afraid of being alone because it meant facing myself. But now I realize that's not a bad thing. |
| **Alexa** | It's actually one of the most important relationships you'll ever have—the one with yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I feel more grounded now. Less dependent on others to fill my time or validate me. |
| **Alexa** | That's the beauty of learning to be alone. You become your own source of stability. |
| **Alexis** | I still love spending time with people, but I no longer need it to feel okay. |
| **Alexa** | That's a huge shift. You're choosing connection, not clinging to it. |
| **Alexis** | I think everyone should learn to be alone sometimes. It teaches you so much about yourself. |
| **Alexa** | It really does. And it makes your time with others even more meaningful. |
| **Alexis** | I'm grateful for this time I've had. It's changed how I see myself and the world. |
| **Alexa** | That's the gift of solitude—it gives you back to yourself. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Solitude** | Noun | The state of being alone, especially by choice |
| **Lonely** | Adjective | Feeling sad and disconnected from others |
| **Fill the silence** | Verb Phrase | To add noise or activity to avoid quietness |
| **Gift** (of solitude) | Metaphor | Something valuable and special |
| **Journaling** | Noun / Verb | The practice of writing down thoughts and feelings |
| **No judgment** | Phrase | Without criticism or evaluation |
| **Grounded** | Adjective | Balanced, stable, and connected to yourself |
| **Validate me** | Verb Phrase | To seek approval or confirmation from others |
| **Source of stability** | Phrase | Something that provides grounding and security |
| **Choosing connection, not clinging** | Phrase | Enjoying relationships without depending on them |
| **Give you back to yourself** | Phrase | Help you reconnect with your own identity and inner life |
