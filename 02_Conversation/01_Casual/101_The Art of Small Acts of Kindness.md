## Topic: The Art of Small Acts of Kindness

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking about how the smallest gestures can make the biggest difference. A smile, a held door, a kind text—they stay with you. |
| **Alexa** | It's true. We often think we need to do something grand to matter, but the quiet, consistent acts of kindness are what truly weave the fabric of connection. |
| **Alexis** | I remember once I was having a terrible day, and a colleague just left a sticky note on my desk that said, "You've got this." It changed everything. |
| **Alexa** | That's the beauty of it. That person probably doesn't even remember doing it. But you'll never forget it. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be that person for others. Someone who leaves small traces of light wherever I go. |
| **Alexa** | You already are. I've seen you do it. You have a way of making people feel seen. |
| **Alexis** | I think we underestimate how much people need that. A little reminder that they matter. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. And the best part? It costs nothing but time and intention. |
| **Alexis** | I've been trying to practice more active kindness—not just reacting, but looking for opportunities to give. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. It's like planting seeds. You never know when they'll bloom. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to be kinder to myself. I'm so quick to give grace to others, but I hold myself to impossible standards. |
| **Alexa** | Self-kindness is just as important. You can't pour from an empty cup. |
| **Alexis** | I think self-kindness and kindness to others go hand in hand. When I'm gentle with myself, I have more to give. |
| **Alexa** | Yes. Kindness is a cycle. It grows the more you practice it. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to try to do one small act of kindness every day. Not for recognition—just for the sake of it. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful commitment. And I'll join you. We can share our small acts with each other. |
| **Alexis** | I'd love that. It'll keep us both inspired. |
| **Alexa** | And maybe it'll ripple out further than we can see. |
| **Alexis** | I believe it will. Kindness has a way of traveling. |
| **Alexa** | It does. And it always comes back around. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Gestures** (small) | Noun (plural) | Actions or movements that express care or kindness |
| **Fabric of connection** | Metaphor / Phrase | The underlying structure that holds relationships together |
| **Sticky note** | Noun | A small piece of paper with adhesive, often used for quick notes |
| **Traces of light** | Metaphor / Phrase | Small positive impacts you leave on others |
| **Seen** (making people feel) | Verb (metaphor) | Acknowledged, understood, and valued |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Active kindness** | Phrase | Deliberate, conscious acts of care for others |
| **Planting seeds** | Metaphor | Doing small good things that may grow later |
| **Bloom** | Verb (metaphor) | To develop or flourish over time |
| **Grace** (giving grace to others) | Noun | Kindness, patience, and forgiveness toward someone |
| **Impossible standards** | Phrase | Unattainable expectations set for yourself |
| **Self-kindness** | Noun | Treating yourself with compassion and care |
| **Pour from an empty cup** | Idiom / Phrase | To give to others when you have nothing left to give |
| **Cycle** (kindness as a) | Noun | A repeating pattern that grows with practice |
| **Recognition** (not for) | Noun | Praise or acknowledgment from others |
| **Commitment** | Noun | A promise or dedication to doing something |
| **Ripple out** | Verb Phrase | To spread gradually to affect others |
| **Travels** (kindness travels) | Verb | Spreads or moves from person to person |
| **Comes back around** | Phrase | Returns or reappears, often in unexpected ways |
