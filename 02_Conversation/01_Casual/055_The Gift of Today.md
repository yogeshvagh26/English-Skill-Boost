## Topic: The Gift of Today

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been so focused on the future that I've forgotten to appreciate today. I'm always waiting for tomorrow to be better. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a common trap. But today is the only day we truly have. The future is just a promise—today is a gift. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so worried about what's next that I've missed what's now. |
| **Alexa** | That's why it's called the present. It's a gift. And it's yours to open right now. |
| **Alexis** | I want to start living more in the present. To stop waiting for the perfect moment and start enjoying this one. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. What would that look like for you today? |
| **Alexis** | Maybe I'll take a walk without my phone. Just notice the world around me. |
| **Alexa** | That's a perfect start. When you're present, even a simple walk becomes an adventure. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to stop worrying about things that haven't happened yet. Most of them never do. |
| **Alexa** | That's so true. We spend so much energy on imaginary problems. Today is real. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so focused on the destination that I forgot to enjoy the journey. |
| **Alexa** | The journey is where life happens. The destination is just a moment. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to try to be more intentional about today. To notice the small joys. |
| **Alexa** | Those small joys are the threads that weave a beautiful life. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be present with the people I love. To really see them, not just pass through. |
| **Alexa** | That's the greatest gift you can give—your full attention. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to put my phone away during meals and just talk. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful practice. Connection happens when we're fully there. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been saving happiness for later. But happiness is available now. |
| **Alexa** | It is. It's in the warmth of the sun, the taste of your coffee, the sound of laughter. |
| **Alexis** | I want to stop waiting and start living. Right now. |
| **Alexa** | That's the best decision you'll ever make. Today is a gift—and you're unwrapping it. |

---

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Trap** (waiting for tomorrow) | Noun / Metaphor | A situation that seems logical but leads to missing the present |
| **Present** (as a gift) | Noun / Adjective | The current moment; also means a gift |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Adventure** | Noun | An exciting, unusual experience |
| **Imaginary problems** | Phrase | Worries about things that haven't happened and may never happen |
| **Destination** (vs. journey) | Noun | The final stop or goal, often less important than the process |
| **Journey** | Noun | The experience of living and growing, not just the end result |
| **Intentional** | Adjective | Done with purpose and awareness |
| **Threads** (metaphor) | Noun (plural) | Small elements that combine to create something greater |
| **Full attention** | Phrase | Complete focus on someone or something |
| **Connection** | Noun | A feeling of closeness and understanding with someone |
| **Saving happiness for later** | Phrase | Delaying joy instead of finding it in the present |
| **Unwrapping it** (the gift) | Metaphor | Opening and experiencing the gift of today |
