### 1. Situation Name : At a Shopping Mall – Returning an Item & Asking for Directions

---

### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"I'd like to return [item], please."* – Stating your intention at the counter.
  - *"Do you have the receipt?"* – The staff's first question for returns/exchanges.
  - *"Would you prefer a refund or an exchange?"* – Offering the customer a choice.
  - *"I'll process that for you."* – Confirming that the transaction is being handled.
  - *"Could you point me in the right direction to [place]?"* – A polite way to ask for directions.
  - *"Is there a map I can look at?"* – Asking for visual guidance.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"It doesn't really fit."* – A common, polite reason for returns.
  - *"I see."* – Acknowledging what the other person said.
  - *"No problem."* – An informal, friendly response to a request or thank you.
  - *"You can't miss it."* – Reassuring someone that the destination is easy to find.
  - *"Got it."* – A quick way to say "I understand" or "I've noted that."
  - *"Enjoy your meal!"* – A warm wish when someone is going to eat.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Know the return policy** – Before you go to a store, check if they have a specific timeframe (e.g., *"30 days"*) or conditions (e.g., *"item must be unworn with tags"*). This saves you from being rejected. If you're unsure, ask: *"What's your return policy?"*
  2. **Be prepared with your proof of purchase** – Always bring the receipt, your ID, and the original payment card if possible. This speeds up the process. Say: *"I have the receipt and the card I used."*
  3. **Give a clear, simple reason for the return** – It's okay to say *"It doesn't fit"*, *"It's defective"*, or *"I changed my mind."* No need for long explanations unless they ask.
  4. **When asking for directions, be specific** – Instead of just *"Where's the food court?"*, ask *"Could you point me in the right direction to the food court?"* and follow up with *"After the escalator, which way?"* if you need more detail.
  5. **Confirm directions back** – Repeat the directions to show you understood: *"So I go past the bookstore and it's behind the fountain – right?"* This helps catch any mistakes before you walk off.
