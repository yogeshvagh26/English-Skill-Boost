## Topic: The Importance of Showing Up

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking about how often I hide. When I'm scared, when I'm sad, when I don't feel good enough—I just disappear. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a natural instinct. We want to protect ourselves from being seen in our vulnerable moments. But hiding also means we miss out on connection. |
| **Alexis** | I've missed so many opportunities because I was too afraid to show up. |
| **Alexa** | What kind of opportunities? |
| **Alexis** | Like speaking up in meetings, having hard conversations, even just showing up to social events. I'd rather stay home and avoid the discomfort. |
| **Alexa** | I understand that. But every time you choose to stay hidden, you're telling yourself that you're not worthy of being seen. |
| **Alexis** | That's a harsh truth. But you're right. I've been shrinking myself to feel safe. |
| **Alexa** | And that safety comes at a cost. You miss the chance to be known, to grow, to surprise yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I want to start showing up more. Even when it's scary. Especially when it's scary. |
| **Alexa** | That's a brave choice. Showing up doesn't mean you have to be perfect. It just means you're present. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been waiting until I feel ready. But I never feel ready. |
| **Alexa** | You'll never feel fully ready. But you can be willing. And willingness is enough. |
| **Alexis** | I want to show up for the people I love. To be there when they need me, not just when it's comfortable. |
| **Alexa** | That's the most beautiful kind of showing up. It says, "I see you, and I'm here." |
| **Alexis** | I also want to show up for myself. To keep the promises I make to myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's the foundation. When you show up for yourself, you build trust with your own heart. |
| **Alexis** | I feel like I've been letting myself down by hiding. It's time to step forward. |
| **Alexa** | You're already stepping forward just by having this conversation. That's showing up. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to try to be more present in my own life. Not perfect, just present. |
| **Alexa** | That's the goal. Presence over perfection. Every single time. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you for always being here. For showing up for me. |
| **Alexa** | I always will. That's what we do for the people we love. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Showing up** | Phrasal Verb | Making an effort to be present and engaged, even when it's hard |
| **Hiding** | Verb | Concealing yourself or your true feelings out of fear or shame |
| **Vulnerable** | Adjective | Open and exposed, allowing yourself to be seen emotionally |
| **Discomfort** | Noun | A feeling of unease, awkwardness, or mild pain |
| **Shrinking myself** (metaphor) | Phrase | Making yourself small or invisible to avoid attention or judgment |
| **Cost** (of hiding) | Noun | The negative result or consequence of an action |
| **Being known** | Phrase | Being understood and accepted for who you truly are |
| **Brave** | Adjective | Facing difficulty or fear without backing down |
| **Present** | Adjective | Fully focused on the current moment and the person/place you're with |
| **Willing** | Adjective | Ready and open to do something, even if you're scared |
| **Keep the promises** (to yourself) | Verb Phrase | To follow through on commitments you've made to yourself |
| **Foundation** | Noun | The base or core on which something is built |
| **Letting myself down** | Phrase | Disappointing or failing yourself |
| **Presence over perfection** | Motto / Phrase | Prioritizing being fully present over being flawless |
