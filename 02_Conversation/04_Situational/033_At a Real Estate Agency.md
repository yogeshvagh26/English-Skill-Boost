### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"I'm looking for a [number]-bedroom apartment to rent."* – Stating your housing need.
  - *"What's your budget for monthly rent?"* – Asking about financial limits.
  - *"Do you have a preferred neighborhood?"* – Asking about location preferences.
  - *"Are you looking for a furnished or unfurnished apartment?"* – Asking about furniture needs.
  - *"I have three options that match your criteria."* – Presenting available properties.
  - *"Could you tell me more about that one?"* – Asking for more details on a specific listing.
  - *"Can I view the apartment today?"* – Requesting a showing.
  - *"The security deposit is one month's rent."* – Explaining financial requirements.
  - *"You'll need to fill out a rental application form."* – Giving application instructions.
  - *"The approval process takes [time]."* – Setting expectations.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"That's a realistic budget."* – Confirming the budget is reasonable.
  - *"Understood."* – Acknowledging preferences.
  - *"That sounds promising."* – Expressing interest.
  - *"That's reasonable."* – Accepting a cost or condition.
  - *"Perfect."* – Expressing satisfaction.
  - *"I hope it works out!"* – Expressing optimism about the outcome.
  - *"I'll be in touch soon."* – Promising future communication.
  - *"I think it's a great fit."* – Giving a positive opinion about the match.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Know your budget and requirements** – Before contacting an agent, decide on your budget, preferred neighborhood, essential features (e.g., pet-friendly, parking, elevator). This saves time and helps the agent find suitable options quickly.
  2. **Ask about included utilities** – Clarify: *"What utilities are included in the rent?"* and *"What additional costs should I expect?"* This helps you calculate the true monthly cost.
  3. **Inquire about the lease terms** – Ask: *"What's the length of the lease?"* and *"Can I renew or go month-to-month after?"* Also ask: *"Is there a penalty for breaking the lease early?"*
  4. **View the apartment in person** – Always visit the unit before signing. Say: *"Can I see the apartment?"* and *"What's the current condition?"* Look for signs of damage, noise, or maintenance issues.
  5. **Ask about additional fees** – Besides rent, ask: *"Are there any application fees, pet fees, or maintenance fees?"* and *"Is the security deposit refundable?"* Understanding all costs prevents surprises.
  6. **Prepare required documents** – Have your photo ID, pay stubs, references, and proof of employment ready. Say: *"I have all the documents ready for the application."* This speeds up the approval process.
  7. **Understand the application process** – Ask: *"How long does the approval take?"* and *"When can I get the keys?"* This helps you plan your move-in timeline.
  8. **Read the lease carefully before signing** – Before signing, say: *"I'd like to review the lease agreement carefully."* Look for important details like maintenance responsibilities, guest policies, and any restrictions (e.g., on painting walls, subletting).
  9. **Ask about neighborhood and building** – Inquire about the area: *"Is it quiet at night?"* and *"Are there grocery stores or restaurants nearby?"* This gives you a sense of daily life in the location.
