# Topic: Managing a Crisis of Product Safety / Liability Issue

**Scenario:** You are the Chief Quality Officer at a consumer goods company. Your company has received reports that a popular children's toy—a glow‑in‑the‑dark figurine—has caused skin irritation in several children. The reports are from three different regions, and one child required medical attention. You have launched an internal investigation and confirmed that a batch of the product contains a chemical that exceeds safety limits. The product is still on store shelves. You now need to convene an emergency crisis team (CEO, General Counsel, Head of PR, Head of Operations, and Head of Customer Service) to: **assess** the severity of the issue, **initiate** a product recall, **notify** regulators, **communicate** with affected customers and the public, **coordinate** with retailers to remove the product, **develop** a remediation plan (e.g., refunds, replacements), and **prepare** for potential legal action—all while protecting the company's reputation and demonstrating accountability.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **You** | *(opening the emergency call)* Thank you for joining. I have a serious safety issue to report. Our glow‑in‑the‑dark figurine has caused skin irritation in several children. We've confirmed that a batch contains a chemical above safe limits. The product is still in stores. We need to act now. |
| **CEO** | Have we identified the affected batch? |
| **You** | Yes—batch #A732, manufactured last month. We've traced it to 15 retailers in 5 regions. We estimate approximately 10,000 units are in circulation. |
| **General Counsel** | We need to initiate a voluntary recall immediately. We also need to notify the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) and similar bodies in other regions. I'll prepare the regulatory notifications. |
| **Operations Lead** | I'll coordinate with retailers to remove the product from shelves and organize the return process. We'll also set up a dedicated hotline for customers. |
| **PR Lead** | What's our public message? We need to be transparent but reassuring. |
| **You** | I propose: *"We have identified a safety issue with a specific batch of our glow figurines. We are voluntarily recalling the affected products. Customer safety is our top priority. We are offering full refunds and replacements. We apologize for any inconvenience or concern."* |
| **CEO** | And what about the affected families? |
| **You** | We'll reach out to each reported case directly—with a personal apology and a goodwill gesture, such as a gift card or free product. We'll also cover any medical expenses. |
| **Customer Service Lead** | We need a script for customer calls. I'll prepare one that is empathetic and clear. |
| **General Counsel** | We also need to preserve all documentation—production records, quality tests, and communications. This is critical for the investigation and any potential litigation. |
| **You** | *(assigning tasks)* **Operations**—coordinating the recall with retailers. **Customer Service**—handling inbound calls. **PR**—managing external communications. **Legal**—regulatory notifications and documentation. I'll oversee the overall response and provide hourly updates to the CEO. |
| **You** | *(closing)* I know this is a difficult moment, but we are acting with integrity and speed. Let's ensure the safety of our customers and earn back their trust. We'll reconvene in 4 hours. |
| *(48 hours later – follow-up)* | |
| **Operations Lead** | We've recalled 90% of the affected units. Retailers are cooperating. |
| **Customer Service Lead** | We've handled 200+ calls—most customers are satisfied with the response. |
| **General Counsel** | The regulatory notifications have been filed. We're cooperating fully. |
| **You** | We need to conduct a root cause analysis to understand how the chemical exceeded limits and implement corrective actions to prevent recurrence. |
| **CEO** | I'm pleased with the speed and thoroughness of our response. Let's learn from this and ensure it never happens again. |
| **You** | We'll share the findings with the board and implement new quality checks. |

| **Liability** | Noun | Legal responsibility for harm caused by a product. |

| **CPSC** | Noun | Consumer Product Safety Commission (US regulator). |
| **Batch** | Noun | A specific group of products manufactured together. |
| **Skin irritation** | Noun Phrase | An allergic reaction or rash caused by a substance. |
| **Goodwill gesture** | Noun Phrase | An action to maintain positive relationships. |
| **Hotline** | Noun | A dedicated phone line for handling inquiries. |
| **Documentation** | Noun | Written records and evidence. |
| **Litigation** | Noun | The process of taking legal action. |
| **Root cause analysis** | Noun Phrase | A systematic investigation to find the underlying reason for a problem. |
| **Corrective actions** | Noun Phrase | Steps taken to fix a problem and prevent recurrence. |
| **Transparency** | Noun | Openness and honesty about actions. |
| **Accountability** | Noun | The willingness to take responsibility. |
