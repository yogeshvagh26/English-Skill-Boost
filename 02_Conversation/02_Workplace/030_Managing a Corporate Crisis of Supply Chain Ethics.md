# Topic: Managing a Corporate Crisis of Supply Chain Ethics / Modern Slavery Allegations

**Scenario:** You are the Chief Ethics & Compliance Officer at a global apparel company. An investigative journalist has published a report alleging that one of your key suppliers in Southeast Asia is using forced labor and employing underage workers. The report includes photos and interviews with workers. The news is spreading rapidly, and your brand is being condemned on social media. You need to convene an emergency crisis team (CEO, Head of Supply Chain, Head of PR, General Counsel, and Head of CSR) to: **verify** the allegations, **suspend** the supplier immediately, **launch** an independent investigation, **coordinate** with authorities, **communicate** with the public and customers, **support** affected workers, **review** and strengthen your supplier code of conduct, and **develop** a long‑term strategy to ensure ethical sourcing.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CEO** | *(opening the emergency call)* This is a serious crisis. Our brand is being associated with forced labor. We need to act decisively and transparently. |
| **You** | I've already initiated an investigation. We've suspended all orders from the supplier and are sending a team to the factory. We've also contacted the local authorities. |
| **General Counsel** | We need to be careful about legal exposure. We'll conduct a full investigation and cooperate with any government inquiries. We also need to review our legal obligations to the affected workers. |
| **Head of Supply Chain** | We'll conduct a comprehensive audit of all our suppliers—not just this one. We'll also implement enhanced monitoring and verification processes. |
| **Head of PR** | What's our public statement? We need to show we're taking this seriously. |
| **You** | I propose: *"We are deeply concerned by the allegations. We have suspended the supplier and launched an independent investigation. We are committed to ethical sourcing and will take all necessary actions to protect workers' rights."* |
| **Head of CSR** | What about the workers? We need to ensure they're supported. |
| **You** | We'll work with NGOs and local authorities to provide support to the affected workers, including access to legal assistance and counseling. |
| **CEO** | What about our customers? We need to reassure them that our products are ethically made. |
| **You** | We'll be transparent about our actions and communicate the steps we're taking to strengthen our supplier oversight. We'll also invite independent auditors to review our practices. |
| **General Counsel** | We also need to review our supplier contracts and strengthen the ethical clauses. We'll require all suppliers to sign updated agreements with clear consequences for violations. |
| **You** | *(assigning tasks)* **Supply Chain**—conduct the independent investigation and audit. **Legal**—review contracts and manage regulatory engagement. **PR**—manage public communications and media inquiries. **CSR**—coordinate worker support and NGO partnerships. **CEO**—lead the public messaging and stakeholder engagement. |
| **You** | *(closing)* This is a defining moment for our company. Our response must demonstrate our commitment to human rights and ethical business practices. We'll reconvene in 48 hours with an update. |
| *(48 hours later – follow-up)* | |
| **You** | *(to the leadership team)* The investigation is ongoing. The supplier has been suspended indefinitely. We've offered support to affected workers and are working with NGOs on the ground. |
| **Head of PR** | The public response has been measured—our transparency is being appreciated. |
| **CEO** | We need to ensure this never happens again. |
| **You** | We'll implement a new supplier monitoring system, conduct regular unannounced audits, and publish an annual ethical sourcing report. I'll present a detailed plan to the board next week. |
| **CEO** | Good. Let's show the world that we're serious about ethical business. |
