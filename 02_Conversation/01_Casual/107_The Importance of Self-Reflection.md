## Topic: The Importance of Self-Reflection

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been taking some time each evening to reflect on my day. It's been really eye-opening. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a healthy habit. I've been meaning to do that more consistently. |
| **Alexis** | I used to just rush from one thing to the next without pausing. Now I sit for a few minutes and ask myself questions. |
| **Alexa** | What kind of questions do you ask? |
| **Alexis** | Simple ones. Like, "What went well today?" and "What could I have done differently?" |
| **Alexa** | That sounds so grounding. It helps you learn from your own life. |
| **Alexis** | Exactly. I used to repeat the same mistakes because I never stopped to notice them. |
| **Alexa** | Reflection is how we grow. Without it, we just keep running in circles. |
| **Alexis** | I've also started asking myself, "What am I grateful for today?" It shifts my focus. |
| **Alexa** | I love that. Gratitude and reflection go hand in hand. |
| **Alexis** | I think the hardest part is being honest with myself. Sometimes I don't want to admit I was wrong. |
| **Alexa** | That takes courage. But that honesty is where the real growth happens. |
| **Alexis** | I'm learning to be gentler with myself when I find something I could have done better. |
| **Alexa** | That's so important. Reflection isn't about judging yourself—it's about understanding yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I've also been reflecting on my relationships. Am I showing up the way I want to? |
| **Alexa** | That's a deep question. I think relationships thrive when we reflect on them. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be more present with the people I love. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. Reflection helps us realign with what matters. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to keep this habit. Even just five minutes a day. |
| **Alexa** | That's all it takes. Small moments of reflection lead to big changes. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you for always reflecting with me. |
| **Alexa** | I learn so much from our conversations too. You help me see things I might have missed. |
| **Alexis** | We're growing together. That's the best part. |
| **Alexa** | It really is. Here's to more reflection and more growth. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Reflect** | Verb | To think deeply about your experiences and actions |
| **Eye-opening** | Adjective | Revealing new and important information |
| **Healthy habit** | Phrase | A regular practice that benefits your well-being |
| **Grounding** | Adjective | Making you feel present and connected to yourself |
| **Repeating the same mistakes** | Phrase | Making the same errors without learning from them |
| **Running in circles** | Idiom / Phrase | Making no progress; staying in the same place |
| **Shifts my focus** | Phrase | Changes my attention or perspective |
| **Go hand in hand** | Idiom | Are closely connected and work well together |
| **Courage** | Noun | The ability to face fear and difficulty |
| **Gentler** (with myself) | Adjective | More kind and forgiving toward myself |
| **Judging yourself** | Verb Phrase | Being overly critical of your own actions |
| **Understanding yourself** | Verb Phrase | Knowing your motivations, strengths, and weaknesses |
| **Thrive** | Verb | To grow, develop, or be successful |
| **Present** (with people) | Adjective | Fully focused and engaged in the moment |
| **Realign** | Verb | To adjust and bring back into balance |
| **Small moments** | Phrase | Short but meaningful periods of time |
| **Lead to big changes** | Phrase | Cause significant shifts over time |
| **Growing together** | Phrase | Developing and improving as a team |
