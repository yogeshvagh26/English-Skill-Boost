## Topic: The Feeling of Home

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking about what the word "home" really means. It's not just the place where you live. It's a feeling. |
| **Alexa** | I agree. Home is where you feel safe enough to be yourself, without masks or pretenses. |
| **Alexis** | I've lived in a few different places, but only some of them ever felt like home. It's about the people, the warmth, the sense of belonging. |
| **Alexa** | Yes. Home is where you're seen and accepted. Where you don't have to perform or prove anything. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been carrying a sense of "home" inside me, even when I'm far from the place I grew up. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful thought. Home can also be an inner sanctuary, not just an outer space. |
| **Alexis** | I've been trying to create that feeling in my current apartment. Adding little touches that make it feel like mine. |
| **Alexa** | What kind of touches? |
| **Alexis** | Photos of people I love, a cozy blanket, candles that smell like the forest. Small things that make me feel grounded. |
| **Alexa** | Those small things matter. They transform a house into a home. |
| **Alexis** | I also think about the homes of people I love—how they feel warm and welcoming. I want to create that for others too. |
| **Alexa** | You already do. When people visit you, they feel your warmth. I know I do. |
| **Alexis** | That means a lot. I want my home to be a place where people can exhale. |
| **Alexa** | That's the best kind of home. A place where you can drop your bags, both physical and emotional. |
| **Alexis** | I think home is also a feeling you can carry with you. Even when you're far away, you can find moments of home. |
| **Alexa** | That's so true. A sunset, a kind word, a familiar song—they can all feel like home. |
| **Alexis** | I'm grateful for the homes I've had—the physical ones and the ones I've found in people. |
| **Alexa** | Me too. And I'm grateful that you feel like home to me. |
| **Alexis** | That's one of the sweetest things you've ever said. |
| **Alexa** | It's true. You're a safe place for my heart. |
| **Alexis** | I feel the same way about you. Thank you for being my home. |
| **Alexa** | Always. And wherever you go, I hope you always carry that feeling with you. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Home** (as a feeling) | Noun | A place or state of emotional safety, belonging, and warmth |
| **Masks** (metaphor) | Noun (plural) | False appearances you show the world |
| **Pretenses** | Noun (plural) | Actions that are not genuine; pretending to be something you're not |
| **Belonging** | Noun | A feeling of being accepted and valued as part of a group or place |
| **Seen** (being seen) | Verb / Metaphor | Being understood, recognized, and valued for who you truly are |
| **Sanctuary** | Noun | A safe, peaceful place where you feel protected |
| **Grounded** | Adjective | Balanced, stable, and connected to yourself |
| **Transform** (a house into a home) | Verb | To change something into something more meaningful or warm |
| **Welcoming** | Adjective | Friendly and open to others |
| **Exhale** (metaphor) | Verb | To relax and let go of stress or tension |
| **Drop your bags** (metaphor) | Phrase | To let go of physical and emotional burdens |
| **Familiar song** | Noun | A piece of music that brings comfort and memory |
| **Grateful** | Adjective | Feeling thankful and appreciative |
| **Safe place for my heart** | Metaphor / Phrase | Someone who provides emotional safety and acceptance |
