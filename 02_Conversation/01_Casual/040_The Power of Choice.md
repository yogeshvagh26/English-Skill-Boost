## Topic: The Power of Choice

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been feeling so stuck lately. Like I don't have any control over my life. |
| **Alexa** | That's a heavy feeling. But here's something to remember: even when you can't control your circumstances, you always have a choice in how you respond. |
| **Alexis** | What do you mean? I feel like everything is happening to me. |
| **Alexa** | I know. But you can choose your attitude, your next action, and how you interpret events. That's your power. |
| **Alexis** | So even when things are hard, I still have a choice? |
| **Alexa** | Yes. You can choose to stay in bed or get up. You can choose to focus on what's wrong or what's possible. |
| **Alexis** | I've been choosing to see the negative. That's been my default. |
| **Alexa** | That's a habit, not a permanent truth. And habits can be changed. |
| **Alexis** | So I can choose to shift my mindset? |
| **Alexa** | Absolutely. It's not always easy, but it is always a choice. |
| **Alexis** | I guess I've been giving away my power by thinking I have no choices. |
| **Alexa** | That's the trap. When you believe you're powerless, you stop looking for options. But they're always there. |
| **Alexis** | Like choosing to take a deep breath instead of reacting in anger. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. That tiny pause is a choice. And it can change everything. |
| **Alexis** | I also have a choice in what I give my attention to. I can focus on what I can change. |
| **Alexa** | Yes. You can't control other people, but you can control where you place your energy. |
| **Alexis** | I've been wasting so much energy on things I can't change. |
| **Alexa** | That's a common pattern. But you can choose to redirect that energy toward what matters. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to start making more conscious choices. Even small ones. |
| **Alexa** | That's powerful. Every small choice builds momentum. |
| **Alexis** | I choose to be kinder to myself today. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful choice. And it's yours to make. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm getting my power back. |
| **Alexa** | You never lost it—you just forgot it was there. |

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |

| **Respond** | Verb | To react or act in a certain way |
| **Attitude** | Noun | A person's mindset or way of thinking |
| **Interpret** | Verb | To understand or explain the meaning of something |
| **Default** (as noun) | Noun | A usual or automatic choice |
| **Habit** | Noun | A regular, repeated behavior |
| **Shift your mindset** | Phrase | To change the way you think about things |
| **Giving away my power** | Phrase | Surrendering control over your own life |
| **Trap** (common trap) | Noun / Metaphor | A situation that seems logical but leads to problems |
| **Options** | Noun (plural) | Choices or possibilities |
| **Pause** | Noun | A temporary stop or break |
| **Redirect** (your energy) | Verb | To direct your focus toward something else |
| **Conscious choices** | Noun (plural) | Decisions made with awareness and intention |
| **Momentum** | Noun | The force that keeps you moving forward |
| **Kind to myself** | Phrase | Treating myself with compassion and care |
