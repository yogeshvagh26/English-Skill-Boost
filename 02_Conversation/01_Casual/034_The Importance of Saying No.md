## Topic: The Importance of Saying No

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been feeling so drained lately. I think it's because I keep saying yes to things I don't want to do. |
| **Alexa** | That's a clear sign that your boundaries might be getting crossed. It's so important to protect your energy. |
| **Alexis** | I always feel guilty when I say no. I don't want to disappoint anyone. |
| **Alexa** | I understand that. But saying yes when you mean no builds resentment. That's not good for you or the relationship. |
| **Alexis** | I never thought of it that way. I thought I was being kind by always agreeing. |
| **Alexa** | You're not being kind to yourself, though. And that matters too. |
| **Alexis** | So how do I start setting boundaries without feeling like a bad person? |
| **Alexa** | Start small. You don't have to change everything overnight. |
| **Alexis** | What's a small boundary I could set? |
| **Alexa** | Maybe tell a friend, "I can't talk right now, but I'll call you later." Instead of answering every call immediately. |
| **Alexis** | That feels so simple. But it's hard for me to do. |
| **Alexa** | It is hard at first. But every time you do it, it gets a little easier. |
| **Alexis** | I also need to set boundaries at work. I take on too many tasks. |
| **Alexa** | You can say, "I don't have the capacity for that right now, but I can help next week." |
| **Alexis** | That sounds professional and clear. I'll try that. |
| **Alexa** | And remember, you don't owe anyone a long explanation. A simple no is enough. |
| **Alexis** | I tend to over-explain and justify myself. |
| **Alexa** | That comes from a place of fear. But you have the right to protect your time and energy. |
| **Alexis** | I want to protect my peace. I'm tired of feeling exhausted. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. Boundaries are an act of self-love. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to practice saying no this week. Even if it's uncomfortable. |
| **Alexa** | I'm proud of you. You deserve to feel safe and respected in all your relationships. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I'm going to remind myself that boundaries aren't walls. They're doors. |
| **Alexa** | That's a perfect way to say it. Doors that let in the right people and keep out the ones who drain you. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to start building those doors today. |
| **Alexa** | I'll be here to support you every step of the way. |

| **Protect your energy** | Phrase | To guard your emotional and mental well-being |

| **Disappoint** | Verb | To fail to meet someone's expectations |
