## Topic: Trusting Yourself More

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been doubting myself so much lately. Every time I have to make a decision, I freeze. I keep second-guessing everything. |
| **Alexa** | That sounds so exhausting. When you don't trust yourself, even small choices feel overwhelming. |
| **Alexis** | I always ask everyone else what I should do before I even check in with myself. |
| **Alexa** | It's good to get input, but not at the expense of your own voice. You have inner wisdom that's worth listening to. |
| **Alexis** | But what if I make the wrong decision? |
| **Alexa** | You might. But you'll learn from it. And each time you trust yourself, even if it doesn't go perfectly, you build that muscle. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been waiting for a guarantee that I'll get it right. But that doesn't exist. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. There's no such thing as a perfect decision. There's just the decision you make, and then you make it work. |
| **Alexis** | I like that. "Make it work." That takes the pressure off. |
| **Alexa** | It really does. Trusting yourself isn't about being right all the time, it's about being willing to handle whatever happens next. |
| **Alexis** | How do I start trusting myself more? |
| **Alexa** | Start small. Make a choice without asking anyone else. Something simple, like what to eat or what to wear. |
| **Alexis** | That sounds so basic, but it's huge for me. I always check with others. |
| **Alexa** | That's the habit you can change. Each time you decide on your own, you're telling yourself, "My opinion matters." |
| **Alexis** | I also want to trust my instincts more. I often ignore that inner voice. |
| **Alexa** | Your instincts are built on your experiences and feelings. They're worth paying attention to. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so afraid of being wrong that I stopped trusting myself entirely. |
| **Alexa** | But being wrong isn't a failure. It's information. It helps you adjust and grow. |
| **Alexis** | I want to start acting on my own decisions, even if I'm nervous. |
| **Alexa** | That's a brave step. And I'll be here to remind you that you're more capable than you think. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm finally learning to trust myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful thing. Trusting yourself is the foundation of everything else. |

| **Overwhelming** | Adjective | Too much to handle; overpowering |
| **Inner wisdom** | Phrase / Noun | Your deep, intuitive understanding of yourself |
| **Guarantee** | Noun | A promise or assurance that something will happen |
| **Pressure** (taking the pressure off) | Noun | The feeling of being forced to succeed |
| **Handle** (whatever happens) | Verb | To manage or deal with a situation |
| **Instincts** | Noun (plural) | Natural intuitive feelings or behaviors |
| **Built on** (experiences) | Verb Phrase | Based on or formed by |
| **Paying attention** (to) | Phrase | Focusing your mind on something |
| **Adjust** | Verb | To change slightly to fit a situation |
| **Acting on** (my own decisions) | Verb Phrase | To take action based on your choices |
| **Brave** | Adjective | Facing difficulty or fear without running away |
| **Capable** | Adjective | Having the ability to do something |
| **Foundation** | Noun | The base or core on which something is built |
