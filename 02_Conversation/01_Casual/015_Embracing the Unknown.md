## Topic: Embracing the Unknown

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I have no idea what my life will look like in a year. Everything feels so uncertain, and it's honestly terrifying. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a real feeling. The unknown can feel so heavy. But uncertainty also means possibility. |
| **Alexis** | I used to think I needed to have everything figured out. Now I realize I don't even know what I want. |
| **Alexa** | That's okay. Not knowing is part of the journey. You don't have to have all the answers right now. |
| **Alexis** | But I feel like everyone else knows what they're doing. I feel so behind. |
| **Alexa** | I promise you, most people are just as lost as you feel. They're just better at hiding it. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so afraid of making the wrong choice that I've been making no choice at all. |
| **Alexa** | That's the paralysis of fear. But even standing still is a choice. And it keeps you stuck. |
| **Alexis** | How do I stop being so scared of the unknown? |
| **Alexa** | You start by accepting that you can't control everything. Life will unfold in ways you can't predict. |
| **Alexis** | But what if it all goes wrong? |
| **Alexa** | It might. But you'll figure it out. You've survived every hard thing you've faced so far. |
| **Alexis** | I guess I have. I've been through so much and I'm still here. |
| **Alexa** | That's proof of your strength. You're capable of handling uncertainty. |
| **Alexis** | I want to learn to trust myself more. To believe that I'll be okay, no matter what. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. And trust is built one step at a time. |
| **Alexis** | Maybe I don't need to know the whole path. Maybe I just need to see the next step. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. You don't have to have it all figured out. You just have to keep moving forward. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to try to let go of the need to control everything. It's exhausting. |
| **Alexa** | Letting go isn't giving up. It's freeing yourself to live. |
| **Alexis** | I feel like I can breathe again. |
| **Alexa** | The unknown isn't the enemy. It's where all the magic happens. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Uncertainty** | Noun | The state of not knowing what will happen |
| **Terrifying** | Adjective | Extremely scary |
| **Possibility** | Noun | A chance that something may happen |
| **Paralysis** (of fear) | Noun | Being unable to act because of fear |
| **Stuck** | Adjective | Unable to move forward or make progress |
| **Unfold** (life will unfold) | Verb | To develop or happen naturally over time |
| **Survived** | Verb | Made it through a difficult experience |
| **Proof** (of strength) | Noun | Evidence that something is true |
| **Capable** | Adjective | Having the ability to do something |
| **Handling** (uncertainty) | Verb | Managing or dealing with a situation |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Let go** (of control) | Phrasal Verb | To release your grip on something |
| **Freeing** | Adjective | Making you feel emotionally released and light |
| **Exhausting** | Adjective | Making you feel extremely tired |
| **Magic** (metaphor) | Noun | Something wonderful and full of possibility |
