# Phase 3: Verbs

## Verb Forms

Welcome to Verb Forms! In the previous section, you learned about **types of verbs** (action, linking, stative, etc.). Now it's time to understand how verbs **change form** to express different tenses, voices, and grammatical functions.

Think of it this way: *Verb types tell you WHAT KIND of verb it is. Verb forms tell you HOW the verb CHANGES to show time, aspect, and function. Together, they give you complete mastery over verb usage.*

## Topic Overview: The Five Verb Forms

| Form | Name | Structure | Use | Example |
|------|------|-----------|-----|---------|
| **V1** | Base Form | base verb | Present tense, infinitives, imperatives | **work**, **go** |
| **V2** | Past Simple | verb + -ed (regular) | Simple Past tense | **worked**, **went** |
| **V3** | Past Participle | verb + -ed (regular) | Perfect tenses, passive voice | **worked**, **gone** |
| **V4** | Present Participle | verb + -ing | Continuous tenses, gerunds | **working**, **going** |
| **V5** | Third Person Singular | verb + -s/-es | He/She/It in Simple Present | **works**, **goes** |

## 1. V1 - BASE FORM

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **base form** (V1) is the simplest, most basic form of a verb. It's the form you find in the dictionary. It is used in the present tense (except third person singular), infinitives, imperatives, and after modal verbs.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Structure | Example |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| **Present Tense** (I, you, we, they) | Subject + V1 | I **work** every day. |
| **Infinitive** | to + V1 | I want **to work**. |
| **Imperative** | V1 | **Work** hard! |
| **After Modal Verbs** | modal + V1 | She **can work**. |
| **Subjunctive** | V1 | I suggest that he **work**. |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Present Tense (Non-Third Person)**
```
[I/YOU/WE/THEY] + [V1] + [REST]
I + work + every day.
They + go + to school.
```

**Structure 2: Infinitive**
```
[TO] + [V1] + [REST]
I want + to + go + home.
She needs + to + study + harder.
```

**Structure 3: Imperative**
```
[V1] + [REST]
Work + hard!
Go + to the store!
```

**Structure 4: After Modal Verbs**
```
[MODAL] + [V1] + [REST]
She + can + speak + French.
I + will + help + you.
```

**Structure 5: Subjunctive**
```
[SUGGEST/RECOMMEND/INSIST] + [THAT] + [SUBJECT] + [V1]
I suggest + that + he + leave + now.
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Present: "I **work** at a bank."
- Infinitive: "I want **to go** home."
- Imperative: "**Listen** carefully!"
- Modal: "She **can sing** beautifully."
- Subjunctive: "I suggest that he **study** more."

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "Please **tell** me your name." (imperative)
- "I would like **to visit** Japan one day." (infinitive)
- "They **watch** TV every evening." (present)
- "You **must** **complete** this form." (modal + base)
- "I recommend that she **apply** for the job." (subjunctive)

**Common Verbs - Base Form:**

| Verb | Base Form (V1) |
|------|----------------|
| work | work |
| go | go |
| speak | speak |
| write | write |
| eat | eat |
| study | study |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| She can **to** swim. | She can **swim**. |
| I want **go** home. | I want **to go** home. |
| He suggested that she **goes**. | He suggested that she **go**. |
| **Go** to the store? (question) | **Do you go** to the store? |

## 2. V2 - PAST SIMPLE FORM

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **past simple form** (V2) is used to express actions that were completed in the past. It is used with the Simple Past tense.

**Key Points:**
- Regular verbs: add -ed (work → worked)
- Irregular verbs: change form (go → went)
- Used ONLY for the Simple Past tense
- Never used with auxiliary verbs

**Regular Verb Formation:**

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Most verbs: add -ed | work → worked |
| Verbs ending in -e: add -d | like → liked |
| Verbs ending in consonant + y: change y to ied | study → studied |
| One-syllable CVC: double final consonant | stop → stopped |

**Irregular Verb Examples:**

| Base (V1) | Past Simple (V2) |
|-----------|------------------|
| go | went |
| eat | ate |
| see | saw |
| write | wrote |
| buy | bought |
| speak | spoke |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Positive Sentence**
```
[SUBJECT] + [V2] + [REST]
She + worked + hard.
He + went + home.
```

**Structure 2: Negative Sentence**
```
[SUBJECT] + [DID] + [NOT] + [V1] + [REST]
She + did + not + work + hard.
He + did + not + go + home.
```

**Structure 3: Question**
```
[DID] + [SUBJECT] + [V1] + [REST]?
Did + she + work + hard?
Did + he + go + home?
```

**Important:** In negative and question forms, we use V1 (base form), NOT V2!

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Regular: "She **worked** hard yesterday."
- Irregular: "He **went** home at 6 PM."
- Negative: "I **didn't** **like** the movie."
- Question: "**Did** you **see** the accident?"

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "I **visited** my grandmother last weekend."
- "She **bought** a new car last month."
- "We **ate** dinner at a fancy restaurant."
- "He **didn't** **call** me back."
- "**Did** they **arrive** on time?"

**Common Irregular Verbs (V1 → V2):**

| V1 | V2 | V1 | V2 |
|----|----|----|----|
| go | went | see | saw |
| eat | ate | write | wrote |
| buy | bought | speak | spoke |
| take | took | give | gave |
| have | had | do | did |
| make | made | know | knew |
| think | thought | find | found |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I didn't **went**. | I didn't **go**. |
| She **goed** home. | She **went** home. |
| **Did** she **went**? | **Did** she **go**? |
| I **buyed** a car. | I **bought** a car. |

## 3. V3 - PAST PARTICIPLE

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **past participle** (V3) is used with auxiliary verbs to form perfect tenses and the passive voice.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Structure | Example |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| **Present Perfect** | have/has + V3 | I **have worked**. |
| **Past Perfect** | had + V3 | I **had worked**. |
| **Future Perfect** | will have + V3 | I **will have worked**. |
| **Passive Voice** | be + V3 | It **was done**. |
| **Adjective** | V3 as adjective | **broken** glass |

**Regular vs. Irregular V3:**

| Verb Type | V1 | V2 | V3 |
|-----------|----|----|-----|
| Regular | work | worked | worked |
| Regular | study | studied | studied |
| Irregular | go | went | gone |
| Irregular | eat | ate | eaten |
| Irregular | write | wrote | written |
| Irregular | see | saw | seen |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Present Perfect**
```
[HAVE/HAS] + [V3] + [REST]
I + have + worked + here for 5 years.
She + has + gone + to the store.
```

**Structure 2: Past Perfect**
```
[HAD] + [V3] + [REST]
I + had + worked + before she arrived.
She + had + left + by the time I got there.
```

**Structure 3: Passive Voice**
```
[BE] + [V3] + [REST]
The car + was + stolen.
The house + is + being + built.
```

**Structure 4: V3 as Adjective**
```
[V3] + [NOUN]
broken + glass
written + report
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Present Perfect: "I **have visited** Paris."
- Past Perfect: "She **had eaten** before I arrived."
- Passive: "The letter **was written** by John."
- Adjective: "The **broken** window needs repair."

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "I **have never seen** such a beautiful sunset."
- "She **had already left** by the time we arrived."
- "The project **was completed** on time."
- "The **lost** dog was found."
- "Have you **finished** your homework yet?"

**Common Irregular Verbs (V1 → V2 → V3):**

| V1 | V2 | V3 |
|----|----|-----|
| go | went | gone |
| eat | ate | eaten |
| write | wrote | written |
| see | saw | seen |
| take | took | taken |
| speak | spoke | spoken |
| buy | bought | bought |
| think | thought | thought |
| know | knew | known |
| give | gave | given |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I have **went**. | I have **gone**. |
| She has **did** it. | She has **done** it. |
| The letter was **wrote**. | The letter was **written**. |
| I have **saw** that. | I have **seen** that. |
| The car was **stole**. | The car was **stolen**. |

## 4. V4 - PRESENT PARTICIPLE (-ING Form)

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **present participle** (V4) is the -ing form of the verb. It is used to form continuous tenses, as a gerund, or as an adjective.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Structure | Example |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| **Continuous Tenses** | be + V4 | I **am working**. |
| **Gerund** | V4 as noun | **Running** is fun. |
| **Adjective** | V4 as adjective | **interesting** book |
| **Participial Phrase** | V4 phrase | **Working hard**, I finished. |

**Spelling Rules for V4:**

| Rule | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Most verbs: add -ing | work → working, play → playing |
| Verbs ending in -e: drop e, add -ing | write → writing, make → making |
| One-syllable CVC: double final consonant | run → running, sit → sitting |
| Two-syllable stress on last: double consonant | begin → beginning, prefer → preferring |
| Verbs ending in -ie: change to -ying | lie → lying, die → dying |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Continuous Tense**
```
[BE] + [V4] + [REST]
I + am + working + now.
She + was + reading + when I called.
```

**Structure 2: Gerund as Subject**
```
[V4] + [VERB] + [REST]
Swimming + is + good exercise.
Reading + helps + you learn.
```

**Structure 3: Gerund as Object**
```
[SUBJECT] + [VERB] + [V4]
I + enjoy + reading.
She + loves + dancing.
```

**Structure 4: V4 as Adjective**
```
[V4] + [NOUN]
an interesting + book
a sleeping + baby
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Continuous: "I **am working** right now."
- Gerund: "**Running** is good for health."
- Adjective: "It's an **interesting** movie."
- Participial: "**Feeling** tired, I went to bed."

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "She is **studying** for her exam."
- "**Traveling** broadens your perspective."
- "I love **eating** spicy food."
- "The **shining** stars lit up the sky."
- "**Having** finished my work, I left."

**Common Gerund Phrases:**
- "**Exercising** regularly keeps you healthy."
- "I look forward to **meeting** you."
- "**Making** mistakes is part of learning."
- "She is good at **singing**."

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I am **work**. | I am **working**. |
| She is **read** a book. | She is **reading** a book. |
| **Swim** is fun. | **Swimming** is fun. |
| I enjoy **to read**. | I enjoy **reading**. |

## 5. V5 - THIRD PERSON SINGULAR

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **third person singular** (V5) is the form used with he, she, it (and singular nouns) in the Simple Present tense.

**Key Points:**
- Used ONLY with he, she, it in Simple Present
- Regular verbs: add -s or -es
- Spelling changes for some verbs

**Spelling Rules for V5:**

| Rule | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Most verbs: add -s | work → works, play → plays |
| Verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z: add -es | kiss → kisses, wash → washes |
| Verbs ending in consonant + y: change y to ies | study → studies, fly → flies |
| Verbs ending in vowel + y: add -s | play → plays, say → says |
| Irregular: have → has, do → does, go → goes |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Affirmative**
```
[HE/SHE/IT] + [V5] + [REST]
She + works + at a bank.
He + goes + to school.
```

**Structure 2: Negative**
```
[HE/SHE/IT] + [DOES] + [NOT] + [V1] + [REST]
She + does + not + work + at a bank.
He + does + not + go + to school.
```

**Structure 3: Question**
```
[DOES] + [HE/SHE/IT] + [V1] + [REST]?
Does + she + work + at a bank?
Does + he + go + to school?
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- "She **works** at a hospital."
- "He **studies** every evening."
- "It **rains** a lot here."
- "The dog **barks** loudly."

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "My mother **cooks** delicious food."
- "He **goes** to the gym three times a week."
- "The company **produces** high-quality products."
- "She **has** two brothers."
- "The train **leaves** at 6 PM."

**V5 Forms of Common Verbs:**

| V1 | V5 | V1 | V5 |
|----|----|----|----|
| work | works | do | does |
| go | goes | have | has |
| study | studies | play | plays |
| wash | washes | fix | fixes |
| fly | flies | say | says |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| She **don't** work. | She **doesn't** work. |
| He **go** home. | He **goes** home. |
| **Does** she **works**? | **Does** she **work**? |
| She **have** a car. | She **has** a car. |

## 🏆 Verb Forms in Action: Tense Formation

| Tense | Formula | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **Simple Present** (I/you/we/they) | V1 | I **work**. |
| **Simple Present** (he/she/it) | V5 | She **works**. |
| **Simple Past** | V2 | She **worked**. |
| **Present Continuous** | am/is/are + V4 | She **is working**. |
| **Present Perfect** | have/has + V3 | She **has worked**. |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | have/has been + V4 | She **has been working**. |
| **Past Continuous** | was/were + V4 | She **was working**. |
| **Past Perfect** | had + V3 | She **had worked**. |
| **Future** | will + V1 | She **will work**. |
| **Future Perfect** | will have + V3 | She **will have worked**. |
| **Passive Voice** | be + V3 | It **was done**. |
