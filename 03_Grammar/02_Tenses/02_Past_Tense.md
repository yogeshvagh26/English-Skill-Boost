# Phase 2: Tenses (Most Important)

## Past Tense

Welcome to the Past Tense section! Now that you've mastered the **Present Tenses**, it's time to learn how to talk about the past. This is essential for telling stories, sharing experiences, and describing what happened before now.

Think of it this way: *Present tenses describe what IS happening or what HAS happened up to now. Past tenses describe what WAS happening or what HAD happened before now — allowing you to tell complete stories and share your history.*

---

1. **Learn** the concept and rules

## Topic Overview: The Four Past Tenses

| Tense | Usage | Time Frame | Example |
|-------|-------|------------|---------|
| **Simple Past** | Completed actions, specific past time | Finished past | I **worked** yesterday. |
| **Past Continuous** | Actions in progress at a specific past time | In progress in past | I **was working** when you called. |
| **Past Perfect** | Action completed before another past action | Earlier past | I **had worked** before she arrived. |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | Duration of an action before another past action | Earlier past (ongoing) | I **had been working** for hours before I stopped. |

## 1. SIMPLE PAST TENSE

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **simple past tense** expresses actions that started and finished at a specific time in the past. It is used for completed actions, past habits, and sequences of events.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Description | Examples |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **Completed Actions** | Finished in the past | I **visited** Paris last year. |
| **Past Habits/States** | Things no longer true | He **played** tennis when he was young. |
| **Sequences of Events** | A series of past actions | She **woke up**, **ate** breakfast, and **left**. |
| **Specific Past Time** | With time markers | We **met** in 2010. |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Positive (Affirmative)**

```
[SUBJECT] + [PAST VERB] + [REST]
I + worked + hard.
She + went + to the store.
```

**Structure 2: Negative**

```
[SUBJECT] + [DID] + [NOT] + [BASE VERB] + [REST]
I + did + not + work + hard.
She + did + not + go + to the store.
```

**Structure 3: Yes/No Question**

```
[DID] + [SUBJECT] + [BASE VERB] + [REST]?
Did + you + work + hard?
Did + she + go + to the store?
```

**Structure 4: Wh- Question**

```
[WH-WORD] + [DID] + [SUBJECT] + [BASE VERB] + [REST]?
Where + did + you + work?
Why + did + she + go + there?
```

**Important:** In negative and question forms, the main verb is in BASE FORM (no -ed or irregular form).

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Completed: "I **bought** a new car."
- Past habit: "She **lived** in London for 10 years."
- Sequence: "He **came in**, **sat down**, and **ordered** coffee."
- With time: "We **met** at the conference last week."

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "I **graduated** from university in 2020."
- "She **didn't** like the food at the restaurant."
- "**Did** you **see** the movie last night?"
- "They **stayed** at a beautiful hotel during their vacation."
- "He **woke up** late, **missed** the bus, and **was** late for work."

**Time Expressions Used with Simple Past:**

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| yesterday | I called him **yesterday**. |
| last night/week/month/year | We went **last week**. |
| ago | She left **two hours ago**. |
| in + year | I moved here **in 2020**. |
| when + clause | I was young **when** I lived there. |
| at + time | The meeting started **at 3 PM**. |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I didn't went. | I **didn't go**. |
| Did you called? | **Did you call**? |
| She buyed a car. | She **bought** a car. |
| I have seen him yesterday. | I **saw** him yesterday. |
| He didn't came. | He didn't **come**. |
| We was happy. | We **were** happy. |

**Regular vs. Irregular Verbs:**

| Verb Type | Base Form | Past Form | Example |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Regular** | work | worked | I worked hard. |
| **Regular** | study | studied | She studied all night. |
| **Regular** | stop | stopped | He stopped the car. |
| **Irregular** | go | went | We went home. |
| **Irregular** | see | saw | I saw the movie. |
| **Irregular** | eat | ate | She ate dinner. |
| **Irregular** | have | had | They had a great time. |
| **Irregular** | be | was/were | I was happy. They were late. |

**Common Irregular Verbs List:**

| Base | Past | Past Participle | Base | Past | Past Participle |
|------|------|-----------------|------|------|-----------------|
| go | went | gone | buy | bought | bought |
| eat | ate | eaten | take | took | taken |
| see | saw | seen | come | came | come |
| do | did | done | say | said | said |

## 2. PAST CONTINUOUS TENSE

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **past continuous tense** expresses actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past, often interrupted by another action. It emphasizes the duration or ongoing nature of a past action.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Description | Examples |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **Interrupted Actions** | A longer action interrupted | I **was sleeping** when you called. |
| **Actions at a Specific Time** | In progress at a time | At 8 PM, I **was watching** TV. |
| **Background Actions** | Setting the scene | The sun **was shining** and the birds **were singing**. |
| **Parallel Actions** | Two ongoing actions | She **was cooking** while he **was cleaning**. |
| **Temporary Situations** | Temporary in the past | He **was living** in London temporarily. |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Positive (Affirmative)**

```
[SUBJECT] + [WAS/WERE] + [VERB + ING] + [REST]
I + was + working + hard.
They + were + working + hard.
```

**Structure 2: Negative**

```
[SUBJECT] + [WAS/WERE] + [NOT] + [VERB + ING] + [REST]
I + was + not + working + hard.
They + were + not + working + hard.
```

**Structure 3: Yes/No Question**

```
[WAS/WERE] + [SUBJECT] + [VERB + ING] + [REST]?
Was + I + working + hard?
Were + they + working + hard?
```

**Structure 4: Wh- Question**

```
[WH-WORD] + [WAS/WERE] + [SUBJECT] + [VERB + ING] + [REST]?
What + were + you + doing?
Why + was + she + crying?
```

**Subject-Verb Agreement:**

| Subject | Auxiliary | Verb Form |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| I | **was** | working |
| He/She/It | **was** | working |
| You | **were** | working |
| We | **were** | working |
| They | **were** | working |

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Interrupted: "I **was reading** when the phone rang."
- Specific time: "At 6 PM, she **was cooking** dinner."
- Background: "The children **were playing** in the garden."
- Parallel: "I **was studying** while she **was watching** TV."
- Temporary: "He **was staying** at a hotel for a week."

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "I **was driving** to work when I saw the accident."
- "The lights went out while we **were watching** the movie."
- "At 9 AM, I **was having** breakfast."
- "They **were talking** about the project when I walked in."
- "She **was feeling** sick yesterday, so she stayed home."

**Time Expressions with Past Continuous:**

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| at + specific time | At 5 PM, I **was working**. |
| when + simple past | I **was sleeping** when you called. |
| while + past continuous | She **was reading** while he **was cooking**. |
| as | He **was walking** in as I **was leaving**. |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I was work. | I **was working**. |
| She was write a letter. | She **was writing** a letter. |
| They were go home. | They **were going** home. |
| He came when I was sleep. | He came when I **was sleeping**. |
| I was knowing the answer. | I **knew** the answer. (stative verb) |
| I was thinking. (✓) | I was thinking. (thinking is an action) |

**Important:** Some verbs are not usually used in past continuous (stative verbs):
- know, believe, understand, like, love, hate, want, need, have (possession), etc.

## 3. PAST PERFECT TENSE

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **past perfect tense** expresses an action that happened BEFORE another action in the past. It clarifies the order of past events.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Description | Examples |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **Earlier Past Action** | One action before another | I **had eaten** before she arrived. |
| **Cause and Effect** | Past reason for past result | I **had forgotten** my keys, so I couldn't enter. |
| **Unrealized Hope** | Wishes about the past | I **had wanted** to see you. |
| **Reported Speech** | Past of present perfect | He said he **had finished**. |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Positive (Affirmative)**

```
[SUBJECT] + [HAD] + [PAST PARTICIPLE] + [REST]
I + had + eaten + before you arrived.
She + had + left + already.
```

**Structure 2: Negative**

```
[SUBJECT] + [HAD] + [NOT] + [PAST PARTICIPLE] + [REST]
I + had + not + eaten + before you arrived.
She + had + not + left + yet.
```

**Structure 3: Yes/No Question**

```
[HAD] + [SUBJECT] + [PAST PARTICIPLE] + [REST]?
Had + you + eaten + before you arrived?
Had + she + left + yet?
```

**Structure 4: Wh- Question**

```
[WH-WORD] + [HAD] + [SUBJECT] + [PAST PARTICIPLE] + [REST]?
Why + had + you + left + before I arrived?
What + had + she + done?
```

**Important:** Past perfect shows the EARLIER of two past actions.

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- "I **had eaten** before she arrived." (I ate first)
- "She **had left** by the time I got there." (she left first)
- "He **had finished** his homework, so he went out." (finished first)
- "We **had never seen** such a beautiful sunset." (before that moment)

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "I **had already eaten** dinner, so I wasn't hungry."
- "She **had just finished** the project when her boss asked for changes."
- "They **had lived** in three different countries before they settled here."
- "I **had never been** to Europe until last year."
- "By the time we arrived, the party **had already started**."

**Time Expressions with Past Perfect:**

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| already | I **had already** eaten. |
| just | She **had just** left. |
| never | I **had never** seen her before. |
| by the time | By the time I arrived, she **had left**. |
| before | I had seen him **before** he called me. |
| after | After I **had eaten**, I felt better. |
| ever | Had you **ever** been there before? |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I have eaten before she came. | I **had eaten** before she came. |
| She had went home. | She **had gone** home. |
| I didn't know you have finished. | I didn't know you **had finished**. |
| By the time he came, I ate. | By the time he came, I **had eaten**. |
| He had did it. | He **had done** it. |

**Past Perfect vs. Past Simple:**

| Past Simple (Later Action) | Past Perfect (Earlier Action) |
|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| I **arrived** at the station. | The train **had left**. |
| She **came** to the party. | I **had already left**. |
| He **called** me. | I **had just woken up**. |

**When to Use Past Perfect:**
- ✓ When you need to show which action happened first
- ✓ When you want to explain the reason for something
- ✓ When you use "by the time," "before," "after," "until"

**When NOT to Use Past Perfect:**
- ✗ When actions are in chronological order (use past simple)
  - "I woke up, ate breakfast, and went to work." (not past perfect)

## 4. PAST PERFECT CONTINUOUS TENSE

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **past perfect continuous tense** expresses an action that was in progress for a period of time BEFORE another action in the past. It emphasizes duration and the ongoing nature of an earlier past action.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Description | Examples |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **Duration Before Past** | How long something had been happening | I **had been waiting** for hours when she arrived. |
| **Cause and Effect** | Past reason for past result | I was tired because I **had been working** all day. |
| **Emphasizing Duration** | Focus on length of action | They **had been dating** for five years before they married. |
| **Visible Results** | Results of a past action | She was red-eyed because she **had been crying**. |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Positive (Affirmative)**

```
[SUBJECT] + [HAD] + [BEEN] + [VERB + ING] + [REST]
I + had + been + working + for hours.
She + had + been + waiting + for 30 minutes.
```

**Structure 2: Negative**

```
[SUBJECT] + [HAD] + [NOT] + [BEEN] + [VERB + ING] + [REST]
I + had + not + been + working + for long.
She + had + not + been + waiting + long.
```

**Structure 3: Yes/No Question**

```
[HAD] + [SUBJECT] + [BEEN] + [VERB + ING] + [REST]?
Had + you + been + waiting + long?
Had + she + been + studying + all day?
```

**Structure 4: Wh- Question**

```
[WH-WORD] + [HAD] + [SUBJECT] + [BEEN] + [VERB + ING] + [REST]?
How long + had + you + been + waiting?
Why + had + she + been + crying?
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Duration: "I **had been waiting** for 2 hours when she arrived."
- Cause: "He was exhausted because he **had been running**."
- Duration before event: "They **had been dating** for 3 years before they got married."
- Results: "Her eyes were red. She **had been crying**."

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "I **had been working** at the company for 10 years when I got the promotion."
- "She **had been studying** English for 6 months before she moved to London."
- "We **had been driving** for 5 hours when we finally stopped for lunch."
- "He **had been feeling** unwell for a week before he went to the doctor."
- "The children **had been playing** in the rain, so they were soaking wet."

**Time Expressions with Past Perfect Continuous:**

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| **for** + period | I had been waiting **for** an hour. |
| **since** + point | She had been working here **since** 2020. |
| **before** | I had been living there **before** I moved. |
| **when** | I had been reading **when** you called. |
| **how long** | **How long** had you been waiting? |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I had been know him. | I **had known** him. (stative verb) |
| She had been going to Paris twice. | She **had gone** to Paris twice. (completed action) |
| He had been working since two hours. | He had been working **for** two hours. |
| I had been waiting when she came. (OK) | ✓ (correct if emphasizing duration) |
| They had been being friends. | They **had been** friends. |

**Past Perfect vs. Past Perfect Continuous:**

| Past Perfect (Focus on Completion) | Past Perfect Continuous (Focus on Duration) |
|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| I **had worked** there for 5 years. | I **had been working** there for 5 years. |
| She **had eaten** the cake. | She **had been eating** the cake. |
| They **had finished** the project. | They **had been working** on the project. |

**Important:** Stative verbs cannot be used in past perfect continuous:
- ❌ I had been knowing him.
- ✅ I had known him.

## 🔗 Comparing All Past Tenses

| Tense | Structure | Use | Example | Time Words |
|-------|-----------|-----|---------|------------|
| **Simple Past** | V2 / did + V1 | Completed action | I worked. | yesterday, ago, last week, in 2020 |
| **Past Continuous** | was/were + Ving | Action in progress | I was working. | at 5 PM, when, while |
| **Past Perfect** | had + V3 | Earlier past action | I had worked. | already, just, by the time, before |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | had been + Ving | Duration before past | I had been working. | for, since, how long |

## 📚 Quick Reference Card

| Tense | Formula | When to Use | Example |
|-------|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Simple Past** | S + V2 | Completed past action | I ate dinner. |
| **Past Continuous** | S + was/were + Ving | In progress in past | I was eating. |
| **Past Perfect** | S + had + V3 | Earlier past action | I had eaten. |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | S + had been + Ving | Duration before past | I had been eating. |

## 🔄 Present vs. Past Tenses Comparison

| Present Tense | Past Equivalent | Example (Present) | Example (Past) |
|---------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Simple Present | Simple Past | I work every day. | I worked yesterday. |
| Present Continuous | Past Continuous | I am working now. | I was working at 6 PM. |
| Present Perfect | Past Perfect | I have eaten. | I had eaten before she came. |
| Present Perfect Continuous | Past Perfect Continuous | I have been working. | I had been working for hours. |
