# Topic: Managing a Cross‑Border Expansion / International Regulatory Compliance Project

**Scenario:** You are the Head of International Business Development at a pharmaceutical company. Your company is planning to expand into three new markets—Brazil, India, and South Africa—over the next 18 months. Each country has a different regulatory environment, intellectual property laws, and market access requirements. You are now leading a cross‑functional project team (Legal, Regulatory Affairs, Supply Chain, Marketing, and Finance) to: **assess** the regulatory landscape in each target country, **develop** a market entry strategy, **obtain** necessary approvals and licenses, **address** intellectual property and compliance risks, **manage** the timeline and budget, and **coordinate** with local partners. Your goal is to ensure the expansion is compliant, efficient, and commercially viable.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **You** | *(opening the project kickoff)* Thank you all for joining. We're embarking on a significant expansion into three new markets—Brazil, India, and South Africa. This is a $50M opportunity, but it comes with significant regulatory and compliance challenges. Our goal today is to understand the landscape, assess the risks, and develop a clear, actionable market entry plan for each country. |
| **Regulatory Affairs Lead** | Each country has a distinct regulatory framework. Brazil has a complex approval process, India has specific pricing controls, and South Africa has stringent quality requirements. We'll need dedicated country teams to navigate them. |
| **You** | We'll establish a local regulatory lead for each market. Legal, can you help us with the local legal requirements? |
| **Legal Lead** | Yes—I'll coordinate with local legal counsel in each country to ensure we meet all regulatory and intellectual property requirements. We'll also need to protect our IP—patents, trademarks, and trade secrets. |
| **Supply Chain Lead** | What about the supply chain? We need to ensure we can distribute products efficiently while complying with local sourcing and import regulations. |
| **You** | We'll partner with local distributors who understand the market and have established networks. We'll also conduct a supply chain risk assessment to identify potential bottlenecks. |
| **Marketing Lead** | And what about market access? We need to understand the pricing and reimbursement environment in each country. |
| **You** | We'll engage local market access experts to advise on pricing strategies and reimbursement pathways. We'll also conduct a competitor analysis to position our products effectively. |
| **Finance Lead** | What's the investment required? |
| **You** | We estimate $10M for regulatory approvals, $5M for local partnerships and distribution, and $5M for marketing and launch. Total $20M over 18 months. I'll provide a detailed budget breakdown. |
| **You** | *(assigning tasks)* **Regulatory Affairs**—lead the approval process in each country. **Legal**—oversee IP and compliance. **Supply Chain**—establish local distribution. **Marketing**—develop market entry strategies. **Finance**—manage the budget and financial modeling. I'll coordinate the overall program and report progress to the board quarterly. |
| **You** | *(closing)* This is a significant investment, but the potential is even greater. With the right planning and execution, we can establish a strong presence in these high‑growth markets. Let's work together to make this a success. |
| *(18 months later – progress update)* | |
| **You** | *(to the project team)* I'm pleased to report that we've successfully obtained regulatory approvals in all three countries. We've launched in India and Brazil, and South Africa is on track for next quarter. Revenue is already exceeding projections. |
| **Regulatory Affairs Lead** | The local regulatory teams were instrumental in navigating the approval processes. |
| **Supply Chain Lead** | And our local partners have been delivering effectively. |
| **You** | This is a testament to our cross‑functional collaboration and careful planning. We'll now focus on scaling our presence in these markets. |
