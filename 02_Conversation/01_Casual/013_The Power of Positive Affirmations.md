## Topic: The Power of Positive Affirmations

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been trying something new this week. Every morning, I look in the mirror and say three positive things about myself. It feels strange, but I'm starting to notice a shift. |
| **Alexa** | That's beautiful! Positive affirmations are so powerful. They rewire your brain to believe in your worth. |
| **Alexis** | At first, I felt silly. I didn't believe the words I was saying. But I kept doing it anyway. |
| **Alexa** | That's exactly how it works. You don't have to believe it at first—you just have to say it. Eventually, your mind starts to catch up. |
| **Alexis** | What kind of affirmations do you use? |
| **Alexa** | I say things like, "I am enough," "I am capable," and "I deserve love and kindness." |
| **Alexis** | Those feel so simple, but they're hard to say when you don't feel them. |
| **Alexa** | That's the point. You're planting seeds. Over time, they grow. |
| **Alexis** | I've noticed that when I say them, I feel a little lighter. Like I'm giving myself permission to be okay. |
| **Alexa** | That's the power of affirmations. They're a way of speaking kindness to yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've spent so long being critical of myself that I forgot how to be kind. |
| **Alexa** | It's easy to fall into that habit. But every kind word you say to yourself is a step toward healing. |
| **Alexis** | I want to make this a daily practice. Not just when I feel good, but especially when I feel bad. |
| **Alexa** | That's when affirmations are most needed. They remind you of your strength when you feel weak. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to write my affirmations down and put them on my mirror. |
| **Alexa** | That's a wonderful idea. Visual reminders help reinforce the habit. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to try saying them out loud with conviction, even if I don't feel it. |
| **Alexa** | Yes! Your voice has power. When you speak it, you're declaring it to the world. |
| **Alexis** | I feel like I'm finally learning to be my own cheerleader. |
| **Alexa** | That's the best kind of support. You'll always have yourself. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you for encouraging this practice. I feel like it's changing me from the inside out. |
| **Alexa** | You're doing the work. And that's what makes all the difference. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Affirmations** | Noun (plural) | Positive statements repeated to encourage and uplift yourself |
| **Rewire** (your brain) | Verb | To change the way your mind thinks through repetition |
| **Capable** | Adjective | Having the ability to do something |
| **Deserve** | Verb | To be worthy of something good |
| **Planting seeds** (metaphor) | Phrase | Starting a process that will grow over time |
| **Lighter** (feeling) | Adjective | Less burdened; more free and at ease |
| **Permission** (to be okay) | Noun | The freedom to allow yourself to feel a certain way |
| **Critical** (of myself) | Adjective | Judging or finding fault with yourself |
| **Kind** (to yourself) | Adjective | Gentle, caring, and compassionate toward yourself |
| **Healing** | Noun / Verb | The process of becoming emotionally healthy again |
| **Daily practice** | Phrase | Something you do regularly to improve |
| **Conviction** | Noun | A strong belief or confidence in what you say |
| **Declaring** (it) | Verb | Announcing something clearly and firmly |
| **Cheerleader** (metaphor) | Noun | Someone who supports and encourages you enthusiastically |
| **From the inside out** | Phrase | Changing from within, not just on the surface |
| **Doing the work** | Phrase | Putting in effort to grow and improve |
