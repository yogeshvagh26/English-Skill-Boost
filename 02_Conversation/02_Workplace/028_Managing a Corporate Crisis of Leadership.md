# Topic: Managing a Corporate Crisis of Leadership / CEO Succession Failure

**Scenario:** You are the Chair of the Board at a global manufacturing company. Two years ago, the board appointed a new CEO to drive a major transformation. However, the CEO has failed to deliver on key strategic initiatives, missed financial targets, and lost the confidence of the executive team. The board is now considering removing the CEO and managing the succession. You need to lead the board through this difficult process: **assess** the CEO's performance and the reasons for the failure, **manage** the CEO's departure with dignity, **appoint** an interim CEO, **develop** a succession search plan, **communicate** the change to employees, investors, and the market, **address** legal and regulatory risks, **maintain** business continuity, and **restore** confidence in the company's leadership.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Board Chair** | *(opening the emergency meeting)* We have a serious situation. The CEO has missed every major milestone in the transformation plan. The executive team has lost confidence, and our stock is down 25% since the CEO's appointment. I believe we need to make a change. |
| **Lead Independent Director** | I agree. The CEO's strategy was sound, but execution has been poor. We need to act decisively—but also with dignity. We owe the CEO a respectful transition. |
| **General Counsel** | We need to be careful about legal exposure. We should document the performance issues and follow a fair process. I'll prepare the necessary paperwork for a termination without cause—with a reasonable severance package. |
| **You** | We also need to appoint an interim CEO immediately. I propose the COO takes on the role. They know the business and have the confidence of the executive team. |
| **CEO** *(interim)* | I'm ready to step up. My first priority will be to stabilize the business and reassure employees and clients. |
| **Head of Investor Relations** | What about the market? We need to communicate this change before the opening bell tomorrow. |
| **You** | I'll lead the communication. Key messages: **#1**—We have made a leadership change to accelerate our transformation. **#2**—The interim CEO is an experienced leader who will ensure continuity. **#3**—We have initiated a search for a permanent CEO and will provide updates. |
| **CHRO** | And our employees? They'll be anxious. |
| **You** | We'll send an internal email and hold a town hall within 48 hours. The message: *"We are committed to our strategy and our people. This change will strengthen our ability to execute."* |
| **Lead Independent Director** | What about the succession search? |
| **You** | We'll form a search committee and engage an executive search firm. We aim to have a permanent CEO in place within 6‑9 months. I'll lead the committee and provide regular updates to the board. |
| **You** | *(closing)* This is a difficult moment, but it's also an opportunity to reset and refocus. We owe it to our shareholders, our employees, and our customers to get this right. Let's move forward with clarity and resolve. |
| *(48 hours later – follow‑up)* | |
| **You** | *(to the board)* The announcement has been well‑received. The stock has stabilized, and we've received positive feedback from key investors. The town hall with employees was productive—they're ready to move forward. |
| **Lead Independent Director** | The interim CEO has already started making changes. I'm optimistic about the future. |
| **You** | We'll continue to work closely with the interim CEO and the search committee to ensure a successful transition. |
