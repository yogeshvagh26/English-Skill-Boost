## Topic: The Power of Memories

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I was looking through old photos today, and I felt this wave of memories. Some made me smile, some made me tear up. |
| **Alexa** | That's the power of memories. They hold so much of who we were and who we've become. |
| **Alexis** | I found a picture of us from years ago. We look so young and carefree. |
| **Alexa** | I remember that day! We spent hours just laughing and talking. It feels like a lifetime ago, but also like yesterday. |
| **Alexis** | I think that's what I love about memories—they keep the past alive in a way that still feels real. |
| **Alexa** | They also remind us of how far we've come. We've been through so much since then. |
| **Alexis** | Sometimes I get sad thinking about those days. I miss the simplicity of it. |
| **Alexa** | I understand that. But we can also cherish those moments without getting stuck in them. |
| **Alexis** | How do you hold onto memories without clinging to them? |
| **Alexa** | By appreciating them for what they were and letting them inform who you are now. They're part of your story, not the whole thing. |
| **Alexis** | I've been trying to do that more. To look back with gratitude, not longing. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful shift. Gratitude honors the past, while longing can hold you back. |
| **Alexis** | I think some memories are like anchors—they keep us grounded and remind us of our roots. |
| **Alexa** | And others are like wings—they inspire us to keep moving forward. |
| **Alexis** | I love that. Anchors and wings. |
| **Alexa** | We carry all of them with us. They shape us, but they don't define us. |
| **Alexis** | I'm grateful for the memories we've made together. They're some of my favorites. |
| **Alexa** | Mine too. And we're still making new ones. That's the beauty of it. |
| **Alexis** | I want to keep creating moments worth remembering. |
| **Alexa** | We will. Every day is a chance to add to our collection of memories. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you for being part of mine. |
| **Alexa** | Thank you for being part of mine. Our memories are woven together. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Wave of memories** | Noun / Phrase | A sudden, strong flood of recollections |
| **Carefree** | Adjective | Without worries or responsibilities |
| **Simplicity** (of the past) | Noun | The quality of being uncomplicated and easy |
| **Clinging to** (the past) | Verb | Holding onto something too tightly; unable to let go |
| **Inform** (who you are) | Verb | To shape or influence your present self |
| **Gratitude** | Noun | The quality of being thankful and appreciative |
| **Longing** | Noun | A strong, persistent desire for something |
| **Shift** (in perspective) | Noun | A change in the way you view things |
| **Anchors** (metaphor) | Noun | Things that keep you grounded and stable |
| **Roots** (metaphor) | Noun | Your origins, where you come from |
| **Wings** (metaphor) | Noun | Things that give you the ability to move forward and grow |
| **Define** (us) | Verb | To determine or give meaning to who we are |
| **Woven together** (memories) | Verb Phrase | Connected and intertwined |
