## Topic: The Art of Seeing the Good in Others

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking about how easy it is to notice what's wrong with people, but how rarely we look for what's good. |
| **Alexa** | That's so true. We're often quick to judge, but it takes intention to see the light in someone. |
| **Alexis** | I've been trying to shift my focus—to look for the kindness, the effort, the hidden strengths in people. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful practice. When you look for the good, you often find it. And it changes how you relate to others. |
| **Alexis** | I've noticed that when I assume the best in people, they often rise to meet that expectation. |
| **Alexa** | Yes! People can feel when you believe in them. It's like you're giving them permission to be their best selves. |
| **Alexis** | I used to be so critical of others. I thought it made me discerning, but really it just made me lonely. |
| **Alexa** | Criticism creates distance. But seeing the good creates connection. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be someone who builds people up, not tears them down. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful intention. And it starts with small choices—choosing to see the effort, the heart, the growth. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to see the good in myself. I'm so quick to focus on my flaws. |
| **Alexa** | That's the same muscle. When you practice seeing the good in others, you can also start seeing it in yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I think we all carry hidden goodness. Sometimes it's buried under fear or hurt, but it's there. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a compassionate way to see people. It reminds us that everyone is fighting a battle we know nothing about. |
| **Alexis** | I want to carry that compassion with me. To be someone who sees the best in others, even when it's hard. |
| **Alexa** | You already do. I've seen you offer grace to people when they didn't deserve it. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I'm going to keep practicing. One person, one moment at a time. |
| **Alexa** | The world needs more of that. And you're helping to create it. |

---

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Shift my focus** | Phrase | To change what you are paying attention to |
| **Rise to meet that expectation** | Phrase | To behave in a way that matches what others expect of you |
| **Discerning** | Adjective | Having good judgment, but often used to mean overly critical |
| **Lonely** | Adjective | Feeling isolated and disconnected from others |
| **Connection** | Noun | A feeling of closeness and understanding with someone |
| **Builds people up** | Phrase | Encourages and supports others |
| **Tears them down** | Phrase | Criticizes or discourages others |
| **Flaws** | Noun (plural) | Imperfections or weaknesses |
| **Muscle** (seeing the good as a) | Metaphor | A skill you can strengthen with practice |
| **Buried** (under fear or hurt) | Verb | Hidden or obscured by deeper emotions |
| **Compassionate** | Adjective | Feeling or showing sympathy and kindness |
| **Grace** (offer grace) | Noun | Kindness, patience, and forgiveness toward someone |
