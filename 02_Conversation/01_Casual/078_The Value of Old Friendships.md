## Topic: The Value of Old Friendships

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I ran into an old friend from high school today. We haven't spoken in years, but it felt like no time had passed at all. |
| **Alexa** | That's the magic of old friendships. They carry a piece of your history, and they know the version of you that existed before you became who you are today. |
| **Alexis** | It was so comforting. We laughed about silly memories, and I felt so seen. There's something about someone who knew you "before." |
| **Alexa** | I think that's what makes those friendships so precious. They hold your origin story. They remind you where you came from. |
| **Alexis** | Sometimes I feel guilty that we don't talk as often as we used to. Life gets so busy. |
| **Alexa** | I understand that guilt. But true friendships don't need constant contact. They just need mutual love and respect. You can pick up right where you left off. |
| **Alexis** | That's exactly what happened today. We talked for two hours and it felt like we had never been apart. |
| **Alexa** | That's the sign of a deep connection. Not everyone gets to have that kind of bond. |
| **Alexis** | I think I take those friendships for granted sometimes. I assume they'll always be there. |
| **Alexa** | That's a common trap. But it's beautiful that you're recognizing it. Maybe this is a reminder to reach out more often. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be more intentional about staying in touch. Not just with her, but with all the people who matter. |
| **Alexa** | That's a wonderful intention. Even a simple text or a quick call can mean the world. |
| **Alexis** | I also realize that old friendships teach me about myself. They remind me of who I was and how far I've come. |
| **Alexa** | That's true. They're like mirrors that reflect your growth. And they love you through every version of you. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to message her tonight and suggest we meet up again soon. I don't want to wait another year. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful step. And I'm sure she'll feel the same way. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you for reminding me to value these connections. They're part of my foundation. |
| **Alexa** | They really are. Old friends are like anchors—they keep you grounded while you sail forward. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Old friendships** | Noun / Phrase | Long-standing relationships with people from your past |
| **Precious** | Adjective | Very valuable and special |
| **Origin story** | Noun / Phrase | The history of where you started and how you became who you are |
| **Mutual** | Adjective | Shared by two or more people |
| **Pick up right where you left off** | Idiom / Phrase | To continue a conversation or relationship easily after a break |
| **Deep connection** | Noun / Phrase | A strong emotional bond with someone |
| **Take for granted** | Phrase | To not appreciate something because it's always there |
| **Trap** (of assumption) | Noun / Metaphor | A situation that seems normal but leads to neglect |
| **Intentional** | Adjective | Done with purpose and awareness |
| **Mirrors** (that reflect your growth) | Noun / Metaphor | Things that show you a reflection of yourself |
| **Foundation** | Noun | The base or core on which something is built |
| **Anchors** (metaphor) | Noun | People or things that provide stability and security |
| **Grounded** | Adjective | Balanced, sensible, and connected to reality |
