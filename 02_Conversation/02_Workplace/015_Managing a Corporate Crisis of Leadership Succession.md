# Topic: Managing a Corporate Crisis of Leadership Succession / Unexpected Departure

**Scenario:** You are the Chair of the Board at a global technology company. Your CEO of 15 years has just announced their immediate resignation due to a sudden personal health issue. The news was unexpected, and there is no formal successor in place. The stock dropped 8% in after‑hours trading, and employees are in shock. You now need to convene an emergency meeting with the Lead Independent Director, the General Counsel, the Head of Investor Relations, and the CHRO to: **manage** the immediate communication, **appoint** an interim CEO, **develop** a succession search plan, **communicate** with employees and investors, **maintain** business continuity, and **restore** confidence in the company's leadership.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Board Chair** | *(opening the emergency call)* We have a critical situation. The CEO has resigned effective immediately for personal health reasons. We need to act quickly to stabilize the company and maintain confidence. |
| **Lead Independent Director** | What's the plan for an interim CEO? |
| **You** | I've already spoken to our COO—they've agreed to step into the interim CEO role. They know the business, they're respected, and they provide continuity. I'll announce that to the board and the market today. |
| **General Counsel** | We need to ensure the interim CEO has the legal authority to act—board resolutions, delegations, and regulatory notifications. I'll prepare the necessary documents. |
| **Head of Investor Relations** | What about the investor call? We need to address the market before the opening bell tomorrow. |
| **You** | We'll hold a brief call: **Key messages**—The CEO is stepping down for personal reasons. The COO is appointed as interim CEO. The board has initiated a comprehensive search for a permanent successor. The company's strategy and operations remain unchanged. |
| **CHRO** | And our employees? They'll be anxious. |
| **You** | We'll send an internal email before the market opens: *"Our CEO has stepped down. We wish them well. The COO is our interim CEO. Our focus on serving our customers and executing our strategy remains unwavering. We will keep you informed throughout the transition."* We'll also hold a town hall within 48 hours. |
| **Lead Independent Director** | What about the search for a permanent CEO? |
| **You** | The board will form a search committee. We'll engage an executive search firm and aim to have a successor in place within 6‑9 months. I'll lead the committee and provide regular updates to the board. |
| **CEO** *(interim)* | I'm committed to continuity. I'll work closely with the senior leadership team to ensure a smooth transition. |
| **You** | *(closing)* We have a clear plan: an interim CEO, a succession search process, a communication strategy, and a commitment to continuity. Let's execute with transparency and resolve. We'll reconvene in 24 hours. |
| *(48 hours later – follow-up)* | |
| **You** | *(to the leadership team)* The interim CEO is in place, and the employee town hall was well‑received. The stock has stabilized, and investor feedback has been positive. The search committee has been formed and will begin its work next week. |
| **Lead Independent Director** | We've managed this transition with remarkable composure. |
| **You** | This is a testament to the strength of our leadership team and board. We'll continue to lead with clarity and purpose. |
