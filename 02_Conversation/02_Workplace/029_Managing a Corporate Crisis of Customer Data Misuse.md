# Topic: Managing a Corporate Crisis of Customer Data Misuse / Privacy Violation

**Scenario:** You are the Chief Privacy Officer at a global e‑commerce company. A whistleblower has revealed that the company has been sharing customer data with third‑party advertisers without explicit consent—a violation of the company's privacy policy and multiple data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA). The news has been picked up by the media, and customer trust is eroding. The CEO has asked you to lead the crisis response. You need to convene an emergency meeting with the CEO, General Counsel, Head of PR, Head of IT, and Head of Customer Service to: **investigate** the scope and nature of the data misuse, **stop** the data sharing immediately, **assess** legal and regulatory exposure, **develop** a communication strategy for customers and regulators, **remediate** the breach of trust, **implement** new controls and oversight, and **rebuild** customer confidence.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CEO** | *(opening the emergency call)* We have a crisis. A whistleblower has revealed that we've been sharing customer data with advertisers without proper consent. This is a violation of our privacy policy and the law. We need to act now. |
| **You** | I've already initiated an investigation. We've identified the data sharing practices—they were in place for 12 months. We've stopped the sharing immediately and are working to identify all data that was shared. |
| **General Counsel** | This is a serious legal exposure. We need to notify regulators and potentially affected customers. We're also facing potential class action lawsuits. |
| **You** | We'll engage external legal counsel and coordinate with them on regulatory notifications. We'll also prepare a public statement. |
| **Head of PR** | What's our public message? We need to be transparent but avoid inciting panic. |
| **You** | I propose a two‑part statement: *"We have identified a data sharing practice that was not in line with our privacy policy. We have stopped the practice and are investigating. We are committed to protecting customer data and will provide updates."* |
| **Head of Customer Service** | My team needs a script. Customers will call to ask if their data was shared. |
| **You** | We'll set up a dedicated hotline and provide a script: *"We have identified a data sharing practice and are investigating. We will contact affected customers directly. We take data privacy seriously."* We'll also publish an FAQ on our website. |
| **Head of IT** | We need to implement new controls to prevent this from happening again. |
| **You** | We'll conduct a full data governance audit, implement a new data sharing approval process, and invest in automated monitoring tools. |
| **CEO** | What about the customers affected? How do we earn back their trust? |
| **You** | We'll offer affected customers an apology, a free service upgrade, and access to data privacy education. We'll also be transparent about the changes we're making to protect their data. |
| **You** | *(closing)* To summarize: we've stopped the data sharing, launched an investigation, prepared a public statement, set up a customer hotline, and started implementing new controls. This is a serious failure, but we're going to address it with transparency and accountability. We'll reconvene in 24 hours. |
| *(48 hours later – follow‑up)* | |
| **You** | *(to the leadership team)* The investigation is complete. We've identified the root cause—a lack of oversight in the marketing department. We've implemented new approval processes and enhanced privacy training. Affected customers have been notified and offered remediation. |
| **General Counsel** | We've filed the required regulatory notifications. |
| **Head of PR** | The public response has been measured—customers appreciate our transparency. |
| **CEO** | Now we need to ensure this never happens again. |
| **You** | We'll embed privacy into our governance, conduct quarterly privacy audits, and make the privacy officer a permanent member of the executive committee. |
| **CEO**** | Good. We've taken a hit, but we're stronger now. Let's keep rebuilding trust. |

| **CCPA** | Noun | California Consumer Privacy Act. |

| **Data sharing approval process** | Noun Phrase | A formal procedure for authorizing data transfers. |

| **FAQ** | Noun | Frequently Asked Questions. |
| **Remediation** | Noun | Actions taken to fix a problem and compensate those affected. |
| **Data privacy education** | Noun Phrase | Training and resources on protecting personal information. |
| **Privacy audit** | Noun Phrase | A regular review of privacy practices. |
