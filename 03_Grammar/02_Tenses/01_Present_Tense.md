# Phase 2: Tenses (Most Important)

## Present Tense

Welcome to Phase 2! This is the most crucial section of your grammar journey. **Tenses** are the backbone of English communication — they tell your listener or reader *when* something happens.

Think of it this way: *You've learned the building blocks (Parts of Speech), the structure (Sentence Basics), and how to form different sentences (Types & Structures). Now you need to master TIME — how to express when actions occur.*

---

1. **Learn** the concept and rules
2. **Study** sentence structures and formulas
3. **See** simple and real-life examples
4. **Learn** common mistakes and exceptions
5. **Complete** targeted exercises
6. **Practice** through conversations

8. **Review** using quizzes and spaced repetition

## Topic Overview: The Four Present Tenses

| Tense | Usage | Time Frame | Example |
|-------|-------|------------|---------|
| **Simple Present** | Habits, facts, routines | General time, now | I **work** every day. |
| **Present Continuous** | Actions happening now, temporary actions | Right now, around now | I **am working** right now. |
| **Present Perfect** | Past actions with present relevance, experiences | Past → Present | I **have worked** here for 5 years. |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Actions that started in past and continue | Past → Present (ongoing) | I **have been working** for 3 hours. |

## 1. SIMPLE PRESENT TENSE

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **simple present tense** expresses actions that are habitual, factual, or generally true. It's the most basic and frequently used tense in English.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Description | Examples |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **Facts/Truths** | Things that are always true | Water boils at 100°C. |
| **Habits/Routines** | Actions done regularly | I drink coffee every morning. |
| **Scheduled Events** | Fixed future events | The train leaves at 6 PM. |
| **State Verbs** | Mental states, feelings | I understand the problem. |
| **Instructions** | Step-by-step directions | You turn left at the corner. |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Positive (Affirmative)**

```
[SUBJECT] + [BASE VERB] + [S/ES for he/she/it]
I + work.
She + works.
```

**Structure 2: Negative**

```
[SUBJECT] + [DO/DOES] + [NOT] + [BASE VERB]
I + do + not + work.
She + does + not + work.
```

**Structure 3: Yes/No Question**

```
[DO/DOES] + [SUBJECT] + [BASE VERB]?
Do + you + work?
Does + she + work?
```

**Structure 4: Wh- Question**

```
[WH-WORD] + [DO/DOES] + [SUBJECT] + [BASE VERB]?
Where + do + you + work?
Why + does + she + work?
```

**Subject-Verb Agreement in Simple Present:**

| Subject | Verb Form | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| I | base verb | I work. |
| You | base verb | You work. |
| He/She/It | verb + s/es | She works. |
| We | base verb | We work. |
| They | base verb | They work. |

**Spelling Rules for 3rd Person Singular (-s/-es):**

| Rule | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Most verbs: add -s | work → works, play → plays |
| Verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -s, -x, -z: add -es | watch → watches, wash → washes |
| Verbs ending in consonant + y: change y to ies | study → studies, fly → flies |
| Verbs ending in vowel + y: add -s | play → plays, say → says |
| Irregular: have → has, do → does, go → goes |

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Facts: "The sun rises in the east."
- Habits: "I get up at 7 AM."
- Schedules: "The store opens at 9 AM."
- State verbs: "I believe in hard work."
- Instructions: "First, you add the flour."

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "My brother lives in London." (fact)
- "She teaches English at a university." (routine)
- "The bank closes at 5 PM." (schedule)
- "I don't eat meat." (habit)
- "Does your mother speak Spanish?" (question)

**Time Expressions Used with Simple Present:**

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| always | I **always** drink coffee in the morning. |
| usually | She **usually** arrives at 8 AM. |
| often | We **often** eat out on weekends. |
| sometimes | He **sometimes** works late. |
| rarely | They **rarely** watch TV. |
| never | I **never** eat breakfast. |
| every day/week/month | She studies **every day**. |
| on Mondays | I go to the gym **on Mondays**. |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| She don't like it. | She **doesn't** like it. |
| He work hard. | He **works** hard. |
| Do she come? | **Does** she come? |
| I am agree. | I agree. |
| She is like coffee. | She likes coffee. |
| He doesn't works. | He doesn't work. (no -s after auxiliary) |

## 2. PRESENT CONTINUOUS TENSE

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **present continuous tense** expresses actions that are happening right now, temporary situations, or planned future events.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Description | Examples |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **Actions in Progress** | Happening now | She **is reading** a book. |
| **Temporary Situations** | Not permanent | I **am staying** with my parents. |
| **Planned Future** | Fixed arrangements | We **are meeting** at 7 PM. |
| **Current Trends** | Changes happening | Prices **are rising**. |
| **Annoying Habits** | With "always" | He **is always complaining**. |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Positive (Affirmative)**

```
[SUBJECT] + [BE] + [VERB + ING]
I + am + working.
She + is + working.
They + are + working.
```

**Structure 2: Negative**

```
[SUBJECT] + [BE] + [NOT] + [VERB + ING]
I + am + not + working.
She + is + not + working.
They + are + not + working.
```

**Structure 3: Yes/No Question**

```
[BE] + [SUBJECT] + [VERB + ING]?
Am + I + working?
Is + she + working?
Are + they + working?
```

**Structure 4: Wh- Question**

```
[WH-WORD] + [BE] + [SUBJECT] + [VERB + ING]?
Where + are + you + going?
What + is + she + doing?
```

**Subject-Verb Agreement in Present Continuous:**

| Subject | Be Form | Verb Form |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| I | am | working |
| You | are | working |
| He/She/It | is | working |
| We | are | working |
| They | are | working |

**Spelling Rules for -ING:**

| Rule | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Most verbs: add -ing | work → working, play → playing |
| Verbs ending in -e: drop e, add -ing | write → writing, make → making |
| One-syllable verbs ending CVC: double final consonant | run → running, sit → sitting |
| Two-syllable stress on last syllable: double consonant | refer → referring, begin → beginning |
| Verbs ending in -ie: change to -ying | lie → lying, die → dying |

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Now: "I am studying English right now."
- Temporary: "She is staying with her cousin this week."
- Future: "We are going to the beach tomorrow."
- Trends: "More people are using smartphones."
- Annoying: "He is always losing his keys!"

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "Please be quiet! The baby is sleeping."
- "I'm looking for a new apartment these days." (temporary)
- "We're having a meeting at 2 PM." (future arrangement)
- "What are you doing this weekend?" (future plan)
- "She's working at a restaurant while she studies." (temporary)

**Time Expressions Used with Present Continuous:**

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| now | I am working **now**. |
| right now | She is sleeping **right now**. |
| at the moment | They are playing **at the moment**. |
| today | I am feeling better **today**. |
| this week/month | We are traveling **this month**. |
| currently | He is **currently** studying. |
| Look! / Listen! | **Listen!** Someone is singing. |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I am work. | I am **working**. |
| She is read a book. | She is **reading** a book. |
| They are do their homework. | They are **doing** their homework. |
| I am understanding you. | I understand you. (stative verb) |
| I am having a car. | I have a car. |
| What you doing? | What **are** you doing? |

**Stative Verbs (Not Usually Used in Continuous):**

| Category | Verbs |
|----------|-------|
| **Opinion** | believe, think, understand, know, suppose |
| **Emotion** | love, hate, like, prefer, want, need |
| **Senses** | see, hear, smell, taste, feel |
| **Possession** | have, own, possess, belong |
| **Measurement** | weigh, measure, cost |
| **Other** | seem, appear, be, consist, contain |

## 3. PRESENT PERFECT TENSE

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **present perfect tense** connects past actions to the present. It expresses actions that started in the past and have relevance or continue to the present.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Description | Examples |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **Experiences** | Things you've done | I **have been** to Paris. |
| **Unfinished Actions** | Started past, continue now | She **has lived** here for 10 years. |
| **Recent Actions** | Just happened | They **have arrived**. |
| **Multiple Actions** | Repeated over time | I **have called** three times. |
| **Results/Effects** | Past action, present result | I **have lost** my keys. |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Positive (Affirmative)**

```
[SUBJECT] + [HAVE/HAS] + [PAST PARTICIPLE]
I + have + worked.
She + has + worked.
They + have + worked.
```

**Structure 2: Negative**

```
[SUBJECT] + [HAVE/HAS] + [NOT] + [PAST PARTICIPLE]
I + have + not + worked.
She + has + not + worked.
```

**Structure 3: Yes/No Question**

```
[HAVE/HAS] + [SUBJECT] + [PAST PARTICIPLE]?
Have + I + worked?
Has + she + worked?
```

**Structure 4: Wh- Question**

```
[WH-WORD] + [HAVE/HAS] + [SUBJECT] + [PAST PARTICIPLE]?
Where + have + you + been?
Why + has + she + left?
```

**Subject-Verb Agreement in Present Perfect:**

| Subject | Auxiliary | Past Participle |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| I | have | worked |
| You | have | worked |
| He/She/It | **has** | worked |
| We | have | worked |
| They | have | worked |

**Past Participle Forms:**

| Verb Type | Examples |
|-----------|----------|
| **Regular** | work → worked, play → played, visit → visited |
| **Irregular - Same** | cut → cut, put → put, set → set |
| **Irregular - Change** | write → written, speak → spoken, take → taken |
| **Irregular - Completely Different** | go → gone, be → been, see → seen |

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Experiences: "I have visited Tokyo."
- Unfinished: "She has worked here for five years."
- Recent: "They have just arrived."
- Multiple: "I have called you three times."
- Results: "I have lost my keys."

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "Have you ever eaten sushi?" (experience)
- "I haven't seen that movie yet." (unfinished expectation)
- "She has already finished her homework." (recent completion)
- "We have known each other since college." (unfinished time)
- "The company has made significant progress." (result)

**Time Expressions with Present Perfect:**

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| ever | Have you **ever** been to India? |
| never | I have **never** traveled alone. |
| already | She has **already** left. |
| yet | Have you finished **yet**? |
| just | They have **just** arrived. |
| since | I have worked here **since** 2020. |
| for | She has lived here **for** five years. |
| recently | We have **recently** moved. |

**"For" vs. "Since":**

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|-------------|-----|---------|
| **For** | Period of time | I have lived here **for** 5 years. |
| **Since** | Specific point in time | I have lived here **since** 2020. |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I have went. | I have **gone/been**. |
| She has did it. | She has **done** it. |
| I have seen him yesterday. | I **saw** him yesterday. (specific past time) |
| Have you ever been to Paris? | ✓ (correct!) |
| I have been to there. | I have **been there**. |
| I have a car since 2020. | I **have had** a car since 2020. |

**Important: Present Perfect vs. Past Simple**

| Use Past Simple When... | Use Present Perfect When... |
|------------------------|---------------------------|
| Specific past time (yesterday, last year, in 2010) | Unspecified past time (ever, never, already) |
| Action is finished and has no present connection | Action has present relevance or continues |
| The time is important | The experience/result is important |

## 4. PRESENT PERFECT CONTINUOUS TENSE

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** The **present perfect continuous tense** emphasizes the duration or ongoing nature of an action that started in the past and continues to the present.

**Key Uses:**

| Use | Description | Examples |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| **Ongoing Actions** | Started past, still happening | I **have been studying** for hours. |
| **Recent Actions with Results** | Just stopped, visible result | She **has been crying**. |
| **Repeated Actions** | Over a period | I **have been calling** all day. |
| **Emphasizing Duration** | How long something has been happening | They **have been waiting** for 30 minutes. |

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Positive (Affirmative)**

```
[SUBJECT] + [HAVE/HAS] + [BEEN] + [VERB + ING]
I + have + been + working.
She + has + been + working.
They + have + been + working.
```

**Structure 2: Negative**

```
[SUBJECT] + [HAVE/HAS] + [NOT] + [BEEN] + [VERB + ING]
I + have + not + been + working.
She + has + not + been + working.
```

**Structure 3: Yes/No Question**

```
[HAVE/HAS] + [SUBJECT] + [BEEN] + [VERB + ING]?
Have + I + been + working?
Has + she + been + working?
```

**Structure 4: Wh- Question**

```
[WH-WORD] + [HAVE/HAS] + [SUBJECT] + [BEEN] + [VERB + ING]?
How long + have + you + been + working?
Why + has + she + been + crying?
```

**Subject-Verb Agreement:**

| Subject | Auxiliary | Been | Verb + ing |
|---------|-----------|------|------------|
| I | have | been | working |
| You | have | been | working |
| He/She/It | **has** | been | working |
| We | have | been | working |
| They | have | been | working |

### 👀 See: Examples

**Simple Examples:**
- Ongoing: "I have been studying for three hours."
- Results: "She has been crying." (her eyes are red)
- Repeated: "I have been calling you all day."
- Duration: "How long have you been waiting?"

**Real-Life Examples:**
- "She has been working at this company for over a decade."
- "We have been planning this trip for months."
- "I have been feeling tired lately."
- "He has been practicing the piano since he was a child."
- "The students have been studying for their exams."
- "I've been waiting for you for almost an hour!"

**Time Expressions with Present Perfect Continuous:**

| Expression | Example |
|------------|---------|
| **for** + period | I have been studying **for** three hours. |
| **since** + point | I have been studying **since** 2 PM. |
| **lately/recently** | I have been feeling tired **lately**. |
| **all day/week** | I have been working **all day**. |
| **How long** | **How long** have you been waiting? |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| I have been know him. | I **have known** him. (stative verb) |
| I have been working since two hours. | I have been working **for** two hours. |
| She has been going to Paris. | She has been to Paris. (completed experience) |
| I have been studying since morning. | ✓ (correct!) |
| How long are you waiting? | How long **have you been waiting**? |
| I am working here for 5 years. | I **have been working** here for 5 years. |

**Important: Present Perfect vs. Present Perfect Continuous**

| Present Perfect (Focus on Result) | Present Perfect Continuous (Focus on Duration) |
|-----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| I **have read** 50 pages. (completed) | I **have been reading** for 2 hours. (ongoing) |
| She **has eaten** the cake. (finished) | She **has been eating** the cake. (still going) |
| **How many** have you made? (quantity) | **How long** have you been working? (duration) |

**Stative Verbs:** Don't use stative verbs in present perfect continuous. Use present perfect instead.
- ❌ I have been knowing him.
- ✅ I have known him.

## 🔗 Comparing All Present Tenses

| Tense | Structure | Use | Example | Time Words |
|-------|-----------|-----|---------|------------|
| **Simple Present** | V1 / V1+s | Habits, facts | I work. | always, usually, every day |
| **Present Continuous** | am/is/are + Ving | Now, temporary | I am working. | now, right now, currently |
| **Present Perfect** | have/has + V3 | Experience, result | I have worked. | ever, never, already, yet |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | have/has been + Ving | Duration, ongoing | I have been working. | for, since, all day |

## 📚 Quick Reference Card

| Tense | Formula | When to Use | Example |
|-------|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Simple Present** | S + V1/V1+s | Habits, facts | I drink coffee. |
| **Present Continuous** | S + be + Ving | Now, temporary | I am drinking coffee. |
| **Present Perfect** | S + have/has + V3 | Experience, result | I have drunk coffee. |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | S + have/has + been + Ving | Duration, ongoing | I have been drinking coffee. |
