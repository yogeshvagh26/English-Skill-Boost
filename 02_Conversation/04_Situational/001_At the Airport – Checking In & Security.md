### 1. Situation Name : At the Airport – Checking In & Security

---

### 2. Conversation

*Setting: A traveler (Sarah) is at the airline check-in counter, then proceeds to the security checkpoint.*

| Speaker             | Dialogue                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Agent (Check-in)** | Good morning! Welcome to SkyWings. May I see your passport and ticket, please?                                                                                      |
| **Sarah**           | Good morning. Here you are.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Agent (Check-in)** | Thank you. Are you checking any bags today?                                                                                                                          |
| **Sarah**           | Yes, I have one suitcase to check in.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Agent (Check-in)** | Great. Please place it on the scale. … Okay, that's under the weight limit. Where are you heading today?                                                             |
| **Sarah**           | I'm flying to London.                                                                                                                                                |
| **Agent (Check-in)** | Lovely. Could you please confirm your destination and flight number? It's flight SW402 to Heathrow. And would you prefer a window or an aisle seat?                 |
| **Sarah**           | An aisle seat, please. I like to get up and stretch my legs during the flight.                                                                                       |
| **Agent (Check-in)** | Not a problem. Here's your boarding pass. Your gate is B12. Boarding begins at 10:30 AM, about 45 minutes before departure. Have a safe flight!                     |
| **Sarah**           | Thank you so much!                                                                                                                                                   |
| *(Sarah moves to the security checkpoint)*                                                                                                                           |
| **Security Officer** | Next in line, please. Place your carry-on bag on the conveyor belt. Do you have any liquids, aerosols, or gels in your hand luggage?                                 |
| **Sarah**           | Yes, I have a small shampoo bottle.                                                                                                                                  |
| **Security Officer** | Is it under 100 milliliters? If so, it needs to go in a clear, resealable plastic bag.                                                                               |
| **Sarah**           | Oh, right. I'll put it in the bag now.                                                                                                                               |
| **Security Officer** | Thank you. Please take off your jacket and empty your pockets into the tray. Laptops and electronic devices must be taken out of your bag.                           |
| **Sarah**           | Sure, done.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Security Officer** | Please walk through the scanner. … All clear. You can collect your belongings. Have a pleasant journey!                                                              |
| **Sarah**           | Thanks!                                                                                                                                                              |

### 3. Vocabulary and Expressions

| Word/Phrase            | Part of Speech | Meaning                                                        | Example Sentence                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Check-in**           | Noun / Verb    | The process of registering at the airport for your flight      | *You must arrive early to check in for international flights.*                             |
| **Boarding pass**      | Noun           | The document that allows you to get on the plane               | *Don't lose your boarding pass; you'll need it to board.*                                  |
| **Gate**               | Noun           | The specific area where passengers board their flight          | *Flight 402 departs from gate C3.*                                                         |
| **Aisle seat**         | Noun phrase    | A seat next to the corridor (not by the window)                | *I prefer an aisle seat so I can easily go to the restroom.*                               |
| **Window seat**        | Noun phrase    | A seat beside the airplane window                              | *From the window seat, you can see the clouds.*                                            |
| **Carry-on (luggage)** | Noun           | A small bag taken into the airplane cabin                      | *My carry-on is too big for the overhead bin.*                                             |
| **Conveyor belt**      | Noun           | A moving surface that carries luggage through security         | *Place your bag flat on the conveyor belt.*                                                |
| **Scale**              | Noun           | A machine that measures weight                                 | *The scale shows your bag weighs 22 kilograms.*                                            |
| **Weight limit**       | Noun phrase    | The maximum allowed weight for checked baggage                 | *If you exceed the weight limit, you'll have to pay extra.*                                |
| **Liquids / Aerosols** | Noun           | Fluid substances or spray products (restricted on flights)     | *All liquids must be in containers of 100ml or less.*                                      |
| **Resealable bag**     | Noun phrase    | A plastic bag that can be closed again after opening           | *Put your toiletries in a resealable bag for security.*                                    |
| **Scanner**            | Noun           | A machine that checks for prohibited items                     | *Walk through the full-body scanner.*                                                      |
| **Boarding**           | Noun           | The act of passengers getting onto the airplane                | *Boarding will commence in ten minutes.*                                                   |
| **Departure**          | Noun           | The time when the plane leaves the gate                        | *Our departure is scheduled for 11:15 AM.*                                                 |
| **Stretch your legs**  | Idiom / Phrase | To stand up and walk around after sitting for a long time      | *I always stretch my legs during long layovers.*                                           |
| **All clear**          | Phrase         | A signal that everything is safe and approved                  | *The security officer gave me the all clear to collect my items.*                          |

### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
    - *"May I see your…?"* – Polite request used by service staff.
    - *"Would you prefer A or B?"* – Offering a choice (window vs. aisle).
    - *"Please place [object] on/in [place]."* – Giving instructions.
    - *"Do you have any…?"* – Asking about specific items.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
    - *"Here you are."* – When handing something over.
    - *"Not a problem."* – A friendly way to say "you're welcome" or "it's fine."
    - *"Have a safe flight / pleasant journey."* – Common well-wishes for travelers.
    - *"Next in line, please."* – Used to call the next customer.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
    1.  **Speak clearly and concisely** – Airport staff are busy, so short, direct answers (e.g., *"Yes, one suitcase."*) work best.
    2.  **Use polite forms** – Always say "please" and "thank you." It makes interactions smoother.
    3.  **Confirm details** – When they give you your gate and time, repeat it back (e.g., *"Gate B12 at 10:30 – got it."*) to avoid mistakes.
    4.  **Ask for clarification** – If you don't understand an instruction (e.g., about liquids), it's okay to say, *"Could you repeat that, please?"* or *"Could you explain that again?"*
