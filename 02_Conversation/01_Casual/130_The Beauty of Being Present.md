## Topic: The Beauty of Being Present

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I feel like I'm always somewhere else. Even when I'm with people, my mind is wandering. |
| **Alexa** | That's so common these days. We're constantly distracted by our phones, our to-do lists, our worries. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be more present. I want to actually experience my life, not just rush through it. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. Being present is a gift you give to yourself and to others. |
| **Alexis** | How do I start? It feels like my brain is always running. |
| **Alexa** | Start with something simple. When you're having a conversation, put your phone away and look at the person. |
| **Alexis** | That sounds so basic, but I definitely don't do it. I always have my phone nearby. |
| **Alexa** | It's a habit. But you can break it. Just try it for one conversation today. |
| **Alexis** | I will. What else can I do? |
| **Alexa** | When you eat, just eat. Don't scroll or watch something. Taste your food. |
| **Alexis** | I've been eating in front of screens for so long. I don't even remember what it's like to just eat. |
| **Alexa** | That's how we lose touch with the present. We're always layering distractions on top of moments. |
| **Alexis** | I think I'm scared of silence. When there's no distraction, I have to sit with my thoughts. |
| **Alexa** | That's a real fear. But those thoughts are part of you. And they're not as scary as you think. |
| **Alexis** | I've been avoiding them by staying busy. Maybe it's time to stop. |
| **Alexa** | That's brave. Sitting with yourself is one of the most healing things you can do. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to try to be more present today. Just one moment at a time. |
| **Alexa** | That's all it takes. One moment, then another. And I'll be right here with you. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm finally learning to slow down. |
| **Alexa** | And that's where life really happens. In the slow moments. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Present** (being present) | Adjective | Fully focused on the current moment |
| **Wandering** (mind) | Verb | Moving away from the present moment into thoughts |
| **Distracted** | Adjective | Unable to concentrate because something is taking your attention |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Habit** | Noun | A regular, repeated behavior |
| **Conversation** | Noun | An informal talk between two or more people |
| **Scrolling** | Verb | Moving content on a screen continuously |
| **Layering distractions** | Phrase | Adding unnecessary things on top of experiences |
| **Silence** | Noun | The absence of sound; a quiet state |
| **Sit with your thoughts** | Phrase | To be alone with your own mind and feelings |
| **Avoiding** | Verb | Intentionally staying away from something uncomfortable |
| **Healing** | Noun / Verb | The process of becoming emotionally healthy again |
| **Brave** | Adjective | Facing difficulty or fear without running away |
| **Slow down** | Phrasal Verb | To reduce speed; to become less rushed |
