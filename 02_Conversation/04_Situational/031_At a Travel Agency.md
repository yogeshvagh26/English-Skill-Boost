### 2. Conversation

*Setting: A travel agency office. Linda is planning a vacation to Hawaii. She speaks with the travel agent, Mr. Thompson.*

| Speaker               | Dialogue                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Travel Agent (Mr. Thompson)** | Good morning! Welcome to Dream Vacations. How can I help you today?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Linda**             | Hi! I'm planning a vacation to Hawaii, and I'd like some help booking flights and accommodation. I've never used a travel agent before, so I'm not sure how it works.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | No problem at all. That's what we're here for. Let me start by asking a few questions. When are you planning to travel, and for how long?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Linda**             | I'd like to go in mid-August, and I'm thinking of staying for about 7 nights.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | Great. August is a popular time, but we can still find good options. Do you have a specific island in mind – Oahu, Maui, or the Big Island?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Linda**             | I was thinking of Oahu, mainly to visit Honolulu and Waikiki Beach. But I'm open to suggestions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | Oahu is a fantastic choice for first-timers. It has a great mix of city life, beaches, and cultural attractions. Now, what's your budget for the entire trip – including flights, hotel, and some activities?                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Linda**             | I'd like to keep it under $3,500 per person if possible.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | That's a reasonable budget. Let me check some flight options. Are you flexible with your travel dates? Sometimes flying mid-week can be cheaper.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Linda**             | I could leave on a Tuesday instead of a Saturday if it saves money.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | Great. *(types on the computer)* … I see a round-trip flight from New York to Honolulu for $780 per person on a Tuesday departure. That's a good deal. Do you prefer a direct flight, or are you okay with one layover?                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Linda**             | I'd prefer a direct flight if the price difference isn't too big.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | Direct flights are about $150 more each way. I'd recommend the direct option for convenience, especially for a long-haul flight.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Linda**             | Let's go with direct then. What about hotels?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | I have several hotel options in Waikiki. There's a 3-star hotel for $220 per night, a 4-star for $350, and a luxury resort for $600. The 3-star includes breakfast and is right near the beach.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Linda**             | I think the 3-star sounds fine. I don't need luxury – I'll be out exploring most of the time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | Good choice. The total for flights and hotel for 7 nights would be: flights $780 + hotel $1,540 ($220 x 7) = $2,320 per person. That leaves you some room for activities and meals.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Linda**             | That sounds reasonable. What activities do you recommend?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | I can book a package that includes a Pearl Harbor tour, a Polynesian Cultural Center visit, and a sunset dinner cruise for an additional $350 per person. Or you can book those yourself when you arrive.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Linda**             | I'll take the package. It sounds convenient. Does that include transportation?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | Yes, the package includes hotel pick-up and drop-off for the tours. Also, do you need a rental car while you're there? Public transport is good, but a car gives you more freedom to explore the island.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Linda**             | I think I'll skip the rental car for now. I can use taxis or rideshares.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | Understood. So your total per person for flights, hotel, and activity package is $2,320 + $350 = $2,670, which is well within your budget. Would you like me to book this for you?                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Linda**             | Yes, please. What's the next step?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | I'll need your full name as it appears on your passport, your date of birth, and a credit card for the deposit. The full payment is due 30 days before departure. I'll also need your email and phone number for the confirmation and e-tickets.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Linda**             | Here's my passport information and my card. *(provides details)*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | Thank you. I'm going to send a confirmation email with your itinerary and the terms and conditions. Please review it carefully, especially the cancellation policy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Linda**             | What's the cancellation policy?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Mr. Thompson**      | You can cancel up to 14 days before departure for a full refund. After that, there's a 50% cancellation fee. Within 7 days, it's non-refundable. Do you sell that too?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

That's still within my budget.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

You'll receive a final invoice with the payment due date.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

I'm really excited about this trip!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

If you have any questions before you leave, just call or email me.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

Thanks again!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"I'm planning a vacation to [destination]."* – Stating your travel plans.
  - *"What's your budget?"* – Asking about financial limits.
  - *"Are you flexible with your travel dates?"* – Asking about date flexibility for cost savings.
  - *"I'd recommend [option]."* – Giving advice based on preferences.
  - *"The total comes to [amount]."* – Summarizing costs.
  - *"Would you like me to book this for you?"* – Offering to finalize the booking.
  - *"Please review the itinerary carefully."* – Reminding to check details.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"No problem at all."* – Reassuring the customer.
  - *"That's a reasonable budget."* – Confirming the budget is acceptable.
  - *"Good choice."* – Approving a selection.
  - *"Understood."* – Acknowledging preferences.
  - *"That's good to know."* – Responding to important information.
  - *"I'll consider that."* – Showing openness to a suggestion.
  - *"I'm really excited about this trip!"* – Expressing enthusiasm.
  - *"Have a wonderful vacation!"* – A kind farewell.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Know your budget and preferences** – Before talking to an agent, decide on a budget range, preferred travel dates, and must-haves (e.g., direct flight, beachfront hotel). This helps the agent find the best options quickly.
  2. **Be flexible if possible** – Ask: *"What if I shifted my dates by a day or two? Would that lower the cost?"* Flexibility can save you money.
  3. **Ask about package deals** – Agents can bundle flights, hotels, and activities at a discount. Ask: *"Do you have any packages that include tours or meals?"* This often saves money.
  4. **Understand all fees and policies** – Before paying, ask: *"What's the cancellation policy?"* and *"Are there any hidden fees?"* Also ask about baggage fees, resort fees, and taxes.
  5. **Consider travel insurance** – Ask: *"Do you recommend travel insurance?"* and *"What does it cover?"* This protects you in case of emergencies, cancellations, or lost luggage.
  6. **Review documents carefully** – Once you receive your itinerary and e-tickets, check all details: names, dates, times, and flight numbers. Say: *"I'll review everything and let you know if I have questions."* This avoids errors.
  7. **Ask about local transportation** – Agents can advise on getting around: *"Should I rent a car or use public transport?"* They often know the best options.
  8. **Keep the agent's contact information** – Say: *"If I have questions before my trip, how can I reach you?"* This ensures you have support if issues arise.
