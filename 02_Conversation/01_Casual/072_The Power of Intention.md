## Topic: The Power of Intention

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been waking up feeling so scattered lately. I go through the motions, but I don't feel like I'm actually moving forward. |
| **Alexa** | It sounds like you're missing a sense of direction. Have you ever tried setting an intention for your day? |
| **Alexis** | An intention? Like a goal? |
| **Alexa** | Similar, but gentler. A goal is about achieving something specific. An intention is about how you want to show up—the energy you want to bring. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so focused on results that I forgot about the process. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. When you set an intention, you're choosing how you want to feel and act, regardless of the outcome. |
| **Alexis** | That sounds freeing. I always feel so pressured to perform. |
| **Alexa** | Intention helps with that. It shifts your focus from “What do I need to get done?” to “Who do I want to be today?” |
| **Alexis** | How do I even start? What does it look like? |
| **Alexa** | This morning, I set the intention to be patient and curious. So when things went wrong, I paused and asked, “What can I learn?” instead of reacting. |
| **Alexis** | That's beautiful. I usually just get frustrated and spiral. |
| **Alexa** | It takes practice. But you can start small. Each morning, pick one word or phrase that you want to guide your day. |
| **Alexis** | Like “calm” or “open” or “kind”? |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. And then throughout the day, when you feel off, come back to that word. It's like a compass. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been living on autopilot. I react to everything without choosing how I want to respond. |
| **Alexa** | That's the beauty of intention. It brings you back to choice. You're not a victim of your circumstances—you're a participant. |
| **Alexis** | I want to try this tomorrow. I'll set an intention to be present and gentle with myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. And remember, it doesn't have to be perfect. It's just a guide. |
| **Alexis** | I think this might help me feel less anxious. Instead of trying to control everything, I can just focus on how I want to be. |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. You can't control the world, but you can control your response to it. Intention gives you that power. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I've been missing this piece. |
| **Alexa** | You're welcome. I'm excited to hear how your day goes tomorrow. |
| **Alexis** | I'll let you know. I think this could be a real shift for me. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Intention** | Noun | A purpose or attitude you choose to guide your actions and mindset |
| **Scattered** | Adjective | Disorganized, unable to focus or think clearly |
| **Going through the motions** | Idiom / Phrase | Doing things without real engagement or enthusiasm |
| **Direction** | Noun | A sense of purpose or guidance toward a goal |
| **Gentler** (than a goal) | Adjective | Softer, less demanding, and more compassionate |
| **Pressure** | Noun | The feeling of being forced to achieve something |
| **Perform** | Verb | To act or do something, often to impress others |
| **Shift** (your focus) | Verb | To move your attention from one thing to another |
| **Spiral** | Verb | To worsen quickly and feel out of control |
| **Compass** (metaphor) | Noun | Something that guides or directs you |
| **Autopilot** | Noun / Metaphor | Acting without conscious thought or effort |
| **Circumstances** | Noun (plural) | The conditions or facts of a situation |
| **Participant** | Noun | Someone who actively takes part in something |
| **Guide** (as a noun) | Noun | Something that helps you navigate |
| **Control** (your response) | Verb | To manage or direct your actions |
| **Shift** (as a noun) | Noun | A change in perspective or approach |
