## Topic: The Courage to Ask for What You Want

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been realizing that I never ask for what I truly want. I just hope people will figure it out. |
| **Alexa** | That's so common. We often expect others to read our minds, but they can't. |
| **Alexis** | I'm scared that if I ask, I'll seem needy or demanding. |
| **Alexa** | That's a fear that holds so many back. But asking isn't demanding—it's communicating. |
| **Alexis** | I've been too afraid to ask for a raise at work, even though I know I deserve it. |
| **Alexa** | That's a perfect place to start. You've earned it. The worst they can say is no. |
| **Alexis** | But what if they say no? I'd feel so embarrassed. |
| **Alexa** | You'd feel uncomfortable for a moment, but you'd also know where you stand. And you could ask what you need to do to get there. |
| **Alexis** | I also never ask for help when I need it. I just struggle alone. |
| **Alexa** | Asking for help is a strength. It shows you know your limits and trust others. |
| **Alexis** | I'm starting to realize that staying silent is a form of self-betrayal. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful insight. When you don't ask for what you want, you're telling yourself that your needs don't matter. |
| **Alexis** | I want to change that. I want to speak up for myself. |
| **Alexa** | Start with something small. Ask for a coffee the way you like it. Ask a friend for their opinion. Practice. |
| **Alexis** | That sounds so simple, but it's a big step for me. |
| **Alexa** | It is. And each time you do it, you're building the muscle of self-advocacy. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been waiting for permission to ask. |
| **Alexa** | You don't need permission. You have the right to ask for what you want. The answer might not always be yes, but you'll never know if you don't ask. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to practice this week. I'll start with one thing. |
| **Alexa** | I'm proud of you. You're learning to honor your own voice. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm finally taking up space. |
| **Alexa** | You deserve to. Your wants and needs are valid. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Courage** | Noun | The ability to face fear and difficulty without backing down |
| **Read your mind** | Phrase | To know what someone is thinking without them saying it |
| **Needy** | Adjective | Wanting too much attention or emotional support |
| **Demanding** | Adjective | Making a lot of expectations or requirements |
| **Communicating** | Verb | Sharing information, thoughts, or feelings |
| **Deserve** | Verb | To be worthy of something good |
| **Embarrassed** | Adjective | Feeling ashamed or self-conscious |
| **Limits** | Noun (plural) | Boundaries or capacities you cannot exceed |
| **Self-betrayal** | Noun | Acting against your own needs or values |
| **Insight** | Noun | A deep understanding of something |
| **Practice** | Verb | To do something repeatedly to improve |
| **Self-advocacy** | Noun | Standing up for your own rights and needs |
| **Permission** | Noun | The freedom to allow yourself to do something |
| **Valid** | Adjective | Reasonable, acceptable, and well-founded |
| **Taking up space** (metaphor) | Phrase | Being present and visible without shrinking back |
