## Topic: The Gift of Encouragement

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I don't think I tell you this enough, but you've been such a light in my life. You're always there to lift me up when I'm down. |
| **Alexa** | That means so much to me. And it goes both ways—you've been a source of encouragement for me too, more than you probably realize. |
| **Alexis** | I think sometimes we forget how powerful a few kind words can be. Just knowing someone believes in you can make all the difference. |
| **Alexa** | That's the gift of encouragement. It's not about giving advice or fixing anything—it's about being a witness to someone's strength. |
| **Alexis** | I've had moments where I was ready to give up, and a simple "you can do this" from you helped me keep going. |
| **Alexa** | I've been there too. And it's those small words that remind you that you're not alone. |
| **Alexis** | I think encouragement is a form of love. It's saying, "I see you struggling, and I believe in you anyway." |
| **Alexa** | That's so beautifully put. It's not pretending things are easy—it's acknowledging the difficulty and still holding hope. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be more intentional about encouraging others. You never know how much someone might need it. |
| **Alexa** | It's one of the simplest ways to make someone's day. A kind word costs nothing, but it can mean everything. |
| **Alexis** | I've been on the receiving end so many times, and it always stays with me. |
| **Alexa** | That's because encouragement touches something deep. It reminds you of your own strength. |
| **Alexis** | I want to practice noticing when someone needs a little boost. Even a smile or a nod can be encouraging. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. Sometimes it's the smallest gestures that carry the most weight. |
| **Alexis** | I hope that in some way, I can be that voice of hope for others the way you've been for me. |
| **Alexa** | You already are. I've seen the way you support the people around you. You're a natural encourager. |
| **Alexis** | That means more than you know. |
| **Alexa** | It's true. You have a way of making people feel seen and capable. That's a gift. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to carry that with me. And I'm going to keep lifting others up, like you've lifted me. |
| **Alexa** | And I'll keep doing the same for you. That's what we do for each other—we hold the light. |
| **Alexis** | I'm so grateful for that. For you. |
| **Alexa** | I'm grateful for you too. Always. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Encouragement** | Noun | Words or actions that give someone confidence, hope, or support |
| **Light** (in my life) | Metaphor | Someone who brings positivity and warmth |
| **Lift me up** | Phrasal Verb | To make someone feel happier or more hopeful |
| **Source of encouragement** | Phrase | A person who consistently offers support and positivity |
| **Believes in you** | Phrase | Has confidence and trust in your abilities |
| **Witness** (to someone's strength) | Noun | Someone who sees and acknowledges another's resilience |
| **Give up** | Phrasal Verb | To stop trying; to quit |
| **Intentionally** | Adverb | With purpose and awareness |
| **Costs nothing** | Phrase | Is free; doesn't require money or effort |
| **Stays with me** | Phrase | Remains in my memory |
| **Touches something deep** | Phrase | Affects someone emotionally on a profound level |
| **Boost** (a little boost) | Noun | A small increase in confidence or energy |
| **Intentions** | Noun (plural) | Plans or purposes behind actions |
| **Carries the most weight** | Phrase | Has the greatest importance or impact |
| **Natural encourager** | Phrase | Someone who is instinctively supportive of others |
| **Hold the light** | Metaphor | To be a source of hope and guidance for someone |
