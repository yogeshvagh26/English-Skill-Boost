# Topic: Managing a Corporate Crisis of Workplace Violence / Security Threat

**Scenario:** You are the Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) at a large corporate office. A former employee who was terminated six months ago has made threatening social media posts targeting the company and specific employees. The posts include references to "coming back" and "settling scores." The police have been notified, and a threat assessment is underway. You need to convene an emergency crisis team (CEO, Head of Security, General Counsel, Head of PR, Head of Facilities, and Employee Assistance Program lead) to: **assess** the credibility of the threat, **communicate** with employees about the situation, **coordinate** with law enforcement, **enhance** physical and digital security measures, **support** affected employees, **manage** media inquiries, **develop** a communication strategy, and **plan** for business continuity while maintaining a safe workplace environment.

---

## Dialogue

| **Speaker** | **Dialogue / Text** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CEO** | *(opening the emergency call)* We have a serious situation. A former employee has made online threats against the company and specific individuals. Police are involved. We need to act immediately to protect our people and maintain calm. |
| **You** | I've been in contact with law enforcement. They've assessed the threat as credible but not imminent. The individual is not known to be armed, but we can't take any risks. |
| **Head of Security** | We're enhancing security: increased guards at all entrances, badge‑only access to all floors, and a visitor screening process. We're also monitoring the individual's online activity. |
| **General Counsel** | We need to be careful about what we communicate—both to employees and the media. We should avoid creating panic while ensuring people are informed and cautious. |
| **You** | We'll send an internal communication to all employees: *"We are aware of a security concern involving a former employee. We are working with law enforcement and have enhanced security measures. Your safety is our top priority. Please report any suspicious activity."* |
| **Head of PR** | What about the media? We may get inquiries. |
| **You** | Our holding statement: *"We are aware of a situation involving a former employee. We are cooperating with law enforcement and have taken additional security measures. Employee safety is our top priority. We have no further comment at this time."* |
| **EAP Lead** | We need to support employees who may be anxious. I'll set up confidential counseling sessions and ensure our EAP line is fully staffed. |
| **Head of Facilities** | I'll conduct a security walkthrough of all facilities today and implement any additional physical security measures. |
| **CEO** | What about the individuals specifically targeted? We need to protect them directly. |
| **You** | We've offered them security escorts and the option to work from home for the next week. We've also given them contact details for law enforcement. |
| **You** | *(closing)* To summarize: we've enhanced security, communicated with employees, coordinated with law enforcement, supported affected individuals, and prepared a media response. We'll continue to monitor the situation and update everyone as needed. We'll reconvene in 24 hours. |
| *(48 hours later – follow‑up)* | |
| **You** | *(to the leadership team)* The individual has been taken into custody. Law enforcement has confirmed there is no immediate threat. We've notified employees and lifted the enhanced security measures. |
| **Head of Security** | We're keeping some additional monitoring in place as a precaution. |
| **EAP Lead** | The counseling sessions have been helpful—several employees have taken advantage of them. |
| **CEO** | We handled this with professionalism and care. Let's conduct a post‑incident review to strengthen our protocols. |
| **You** | I'll lead a debrief with the crisis team next week to identify lessons learned and update our emergency response plan. |

## Vocabulary

| **Word / Phrase** | **Part of Speech** | **Meaning** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Workplace violence** | Noun Phrase | Any act of physical or psychological harm in the workplace. |
| **Security threat** | Noun Phrase | An action or situation that jeopardizes safety. |
| **Threat assessment** | Noun Phrase | An evaluation of the credibility and severity of a threat. |
| **Law enforcement** | Noun Phrase | Police and government agencies responsible for public safety. |
| **Custody** | Noun | The state of being held by authorities. |
| **Holding statement** | Noun Phrase | A preliminary public communication. |

| **Security escorts** | Noun Phrase | Personnel who accompany individuals to ensure safety. |

| **Visitor screening** | Noun Phrase | Checking the identity and purpose of visitors. |
| **Monitoring** | Noun | Continuous observation and surveillance. |
| **Suspicious activity** | Noun Phrase | Behavior that appears unusual or potentially harmful. |
| **Confidential counseling** | Noun Phrase | Private mental health support. |
| **Post‑incident review** | Noun Phrase | An analysis of an event to improve future responses. |
| **Debrief** | Noun | A meeting to discuss what happened and what was learned. |
| **Emergency response plan** | Noun Phrase | A documented procedure for handling crises. |

| **Precaution** | Noun | A measure taken in advance to prevent harm. |
