### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"Which bus goes to [destination]?"* – Asking for the correct bus route.
  - *"How much is the fare?"* – Inquiring about the cost.
  - *"How often does the bus come?"* – Asking about frequency.
  - *"Do you have exact change?"* – Asking about payment readiness.
  - *"Could you let me know when we get near [place]?"* – Requesting a stop alert.
  - *"Next stop is [place]."* – The driver announcing stops.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"The next one should be here in about 5 minutes."* – Estimating arrival time.
  - *"That's a good idea."* – Agreeing with a suggestion.
  - *"I'll keep an eye on that."* – Paying attention to something.
  - *"Enjoy your visit!"* – A kind wish for someone going to a place.
  - *"Have a great day!"* – A warm farewell.
  - *"Welcome aboard!"* – A greeting when you enter a bus or train.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Know your route and stop** – Before getting on the bus, know which route you need and where to get off. Ask: *"Does this bus go to [destination]?"* to confirm. If unsure, say: *"Can you tell me when we get close to [place]?"* to the driver.
  2. **Have payment ready** – Buses often require exact change. Ask: *"Do you give change?"* If not, prepare the exact amount or buy a pass in advance from a machine. Learn the difference between *"single ride"* and *"day pass"* to save money.
  3. **Watch the display and listen for announcements** – Many buses have electronic signs showing stops. Listen for announcements: *"Next stop is..."* If you're unsure, ask a fellow passenger: *"Excuse me, is this the stop for [destination]?"*
  4. **Signal the driver** – When your stop is coming up, press the *"stop button"* or pull the cord. Don't wait until the last second. Say to yourself: *"I need to press the button now."* This ensures the driver knows to stop.
  5. **Be aware of peak hours** – Buses are more crowded during rush hours. If you're new, avoid these times if possible. Ask: *"When is the bus less crowded?"* to plan your trip better.
  6. **Know how to ask for help** – If you get lost or miss your stop, ask the driver: *"I missed my stop – can you tell me how to get back?"* or ask another passenger: *"Can you help me find my way to [place]?"* Most people are willing to help.
