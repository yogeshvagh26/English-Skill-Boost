## Topic: The Art of Letting Go

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been holding onto so much lately—old resentments, past mistakes, things I can't change. It's exhausting. |
| **Alexa** | That's a heavy weight to carry. Letting go isn't about forgetting—it's about freeing yourself. |
| **Alexis** | But how do I actually let go? It feels like my mind just clings to things. |
| **Alexa** | Start by acknowledging what you're holding onto. Name it. Sometimes just saying it out loud takes away some of its power. |
| **Alexis** | I'm holding onto the guilt of a mistake I made years ago. I still think about it all the time. |
| **Alexa** | That mistake taught you something. You've grown from it. You don't have to keep carrying it. |
| **Alexis** | I also hold onto grudges. I replay conversations where I felt wronged. |
| **Alexa** | Holding a grudge is like drinking poison and expecting the other person to get sick. It only hurts you. |
| **Alexis** | That's a powerful image. I've been poisoning myself with resentment. |
| **Alexa** | Letting go doesn't mean you condone what happened. It means you're choosing peace over pain. |
| **Alexis** | What about letting go of control? I try to control everything, and it just makes me anxious. |
| **Alexa** | You can't control life. You can only respond to it. Letting go of control means trusting that you'll handle whatever comes. |
| **Alexis** | I want to release all of this. I'm tired of carrying it. |
| **Alexa** | You can start today. Pick one thing—just one—and intentionally release it. |
| **Alexis** | I think I'll start with the guilt. I've apologized and made amends. It's time to forgive myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful choice. Forgiving yourself is one of the most freeing acts there is. |
| **Alexis** | I feel like I've been holding onto a rock that I can finally put down. |
| **Alexa** | That's exactly what letting go feels like. Lighter. Freer. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to practice this every day. |
| **Alexa** | I'll be here to remind you that you deserve to be free. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Letting go** | Phrase | Releasing emotional attachment to something |
| **Exhausting** | Adjective | Making you feel extremely tired |
| **Acknowledge** | Verb | To recognize and accept something as true |
| **Cling** (to things) | Verb | To hold onto something tightly (mentally or emotionally) |
| **Guilt** | Noun | A feeling of responsibility for something wrong |
| **Grudges** | Noun (plural) | Feelings of resentment toward someone |
| **Replay** (conversations) | Verb | Thinking about past events repeatedly |
| **Wronged** | Verb | Treated unfairly or unjustly |
| **Poisoning** (metaphor) | Verb | Slowly harming yourself |
| **Resentment** | Noun | A feeling of bitterness or anger |
| **Condone** | Verb | To accept or allow something wrong |
| **Peace** | Noun | A state of mental calm and quiet |
| **Release** | Verb | To set free from mental or emotional burden |
| **Forgive** (yourself) | Verb | To stop blaming yourself |
| **Freeing** | Adjective | Making you feel emotionally released and light |
| **Lighter** (feeling) | Adjective | Less burdened; more free and at ease |
| **Deserve** | Verb | To be worthy of something good |
