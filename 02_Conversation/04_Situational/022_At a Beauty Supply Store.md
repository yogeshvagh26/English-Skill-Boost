### Useful Phrases to Remember
- **Hair care:** *"I have dry/damaged/color-treated hair."* / *"I need a shampoo for oily scalp."* / *"Do you have products for curly hair?"* / *"Is this sulfate-free?"*
- **Skincare:** *"I have sensitive/combination/dry skin."* / *"What cleanser do you recommend?"* / *"Do you have a toner or serum?"* / *"Is this suitable for acne-prone skin?"*
- **Makeup:** *"I need a foundation with medium/full coverage."* / *"What shade matches my skin tone?"* / *"Do you have a matte lipstick in this color?"* / *"Can I try this before buying?"*
- **Tools:** *"Do you sell makeup brushes?"* / *"What's the best brush for foundation?"* / *"Do you have a beauty blender?"*
- **Samples and testing:** *"Can I take a sample home to try?"* / *"Do you have testers for this product?"* / *"Is it okay to try this on my hand?"*

### Practical Speaking Tips
1.  **Learn common beauty terms**: Practice words like *"serum"*, *"moisturizer"*, *"exfoliant"*, *"primer"*, *"setting spray"*, *"highlighter"*, *"bronzer"*, *"blush"*. This helps you shop with confidence.
2.  **Know your skin tone and undertone**: Use phrases like *"fair"*, *"light"*, *"medium"*, *"tan"*, *"dark"* and *"warm"*, *"cool"*, *"neutral"*. Say: *"I have light skin with cool undertones."* – this helps the salesperson find the right shade.
3.  **Ask about ingredients**: If you have allergies, ask: *"Does this contain fragrance/parabens/sulfates?"* – many shops have "clean" beauty sections.
4.  **Test products carefully**: For foundation, test on your jawline, not your hand – your hand is often a different color. Say: *"Can I test this on my jawline?"*
5.  **Check return policies**: Beauty products can be tricky. Ask: *"What's the return policy if this doesn't work for me?"* – many stores accept returns within 30 days.
6.  **Use samples**: If you're unsure, ask: *"Can I get a sample to try at home?"* – many stores give free samples.
