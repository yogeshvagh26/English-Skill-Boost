# Topic 24: Vocabulary Building

## 1. Concept Explanation

**Vocabulary** is the collection of words you know and can use. A strong vocabulary allows you to:

* Express your thoughts **precisely** (not just "good" but *"exceptional,"* *"splendid,"* *"superb"*)

* Understand **native speakers** more easily

* Read **complex texts** with confidence

* Sound more **educated** and **professional**

#### Difficult words explained:

* ***Active vocabulary*** = Words you can use correctly in speaking and writing.

* ***Passive vocabulary*** = Words you understand when you see/hear them but don't use.

* ***The 80/20 Rule for Vocabulary:*** 80% of your communication comes from just **2,000–3,000 high-frequency words.** But to sound fluent and sophisticated, you need to expand beyond this.


#### The 80/20 Rule for Vocabulary Building:
Focus on:

1. **High-frequency words** (the most common words in English).

2. **Word families** (learn related words together: *happy* → *happiness*, *unhappy*, *happily*, *unhappily*).

3. **Collocations** (words that naturally go together: *make a decision*, *do your homework*, *heavy rain*).

4. **Contextual learning** (learn words in sentences, not in isolation).


---

## 2. Rules (Effective Vocabulary Building Strategies)

| Strategy | Explanation | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Learn in Context** | Don't memorize lists of isolated words. Learn words in **sentences**. | *The abundant harvest ensured everyone had enough food.* (Not just "abundant = plentiful") |
| **2. Learn Word Families** | Learn **root words**, **prefixes**, **suffixes**, and related forms. | ***Act*** → *action, active, activate, activity, actor, actress* |
| **3. Learn Collocations** | Learn words that **naturally go together**. | *make a mistake* (not "do a mistake"), *heavy rain* (not "strong rain"), *fast food* (not "quick food") |
| **4. Use Spaced Repetition** | Review words at **increasing intervals** to move them from short-term to long-term memory. | Use flashcards (**Anki**, **Quizlet**) to review daily. |
| **5. Use the "Five C's"** | **Connect** (to your life), **Context** (a sentence), **Commit** (to memory), **Check** (with a dictionary), **Chat** (use it in conversation). | When you learn a new word, use it in a **sentence about yourself**. |
| **6. Read and Listen Actively** | Expose yourself to English **daily**. When you see/hear a new word, **write it down** and look it up. | Read **news articles**, listen to **podcasts**, watch **movies with subtitles**. |

## 3. Structure (How to Learn a New Word)

When you encounter a new word, use this **systematic approach**:

#### Step 1: Identify the word and its part of speech.
* *Abundant* (**adjective**)

#### Step 2: Understand its meaning (in simple English).
* *Abundant* = **existing in large amounts; more than enough.**

#### Step 3: Find synonyms (similar words) and antonyms (opposites).
* **Synonyms:** *plentiful, ample, bountival.*
* **Antonyms:** *scarce, rare, limited.*

#### Step 4: Learn its pronunciation.
* *Abundant* → **/əˈbʌn.dənt/**

#### Step 5: See it in a sentence (context).
* *The region is **abundant** in natural resources.*

#### Step 6: Write your own sentence.
* *My garden is **abundant** with vegetables in the summer.*

## 6. Common Mistakes (Vocabulary-Related)

| Common Mistake | Why it is wrong | Correct Version |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I do a mistake.* | Mistake collocates with **'make'**, not **'do'**. | **I make a mistake.** |
| *This soup tastes goodly.* | **'Goodly'** is not an adverb. Use **'good'** *(adjective)* after taste. | **This soup tastes good.** |
| *The begin of the movie was boring.* | **'Begin'** is a verb. Need the noun **'beginning'**. | **The beginning of the movie was boring.** |
| *He is very success.* | **'Success'** is a noun. Need the adjective **'successful'**. | **He is very successful.** |
| *I have a big problem.* <br>*(In this context, 'big' is acceptable, but 'serious' or 'major' is more precise.)* | While **'big'** is not wrong, using **precise vocabulary** makes you sound more advanced. | **I have a serious problem.** / **a major issue.** |

## 12. Quick Revision (Tips for Vocabulary Building)

| Tip | Why it works | How to do it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Read daily** | Exposes you to **new words** | Read **news**, **blogs**, **books**, or **articles**. |
