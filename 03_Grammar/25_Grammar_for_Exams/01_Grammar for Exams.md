## 1. Error Detection

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** **Error detection** is the skill of identifying grammatical errors in sentences. It requires knowledge of all grammar rules and attention to detail.

**Key Points:**
- Identify the error
- Know the rule
- Find the correction
- Look for common error types

### 📐 Study: Common Error Types

**1. Subject-Verb Agreement Errors:**
- "She **don't** like coffee." → "She **doesn't** like coffee."
- "The dogs **is** barking." → "The dogs **are** barking."
- "Everyone **are** here." → "Everyone **is** here."

**2. Tense Errors:**
- "I **have went** home." → "I **have gone** home."
- "She **is** here yesterday." → "She **was** here yesterday."
- "I **will call** you when I **will arrive**." → "I **will call** you when I **arrive**."

**3. Article Errors:**
- "I saw **a** apple." → "I saw **an** apple."
- "She is **a** honest person." → "She is **an** honest person."
- "I saw **the** dog in the park." (if general) → "I saw **a** dog in the park."

**4. Preposition Errors:**
- "She is good **in** math." → "She is good **at** math."
- "I am afraid **from** spiders." → "I am afraid **of** spiders."
- "He depends **of** me." → "He depends **on** me."

**5. Pronoun Errors:**
- "**Me and John** are friends." → "**John and I** are friends."
- "I gave it to **she**." → "I gave it to **her**."
- "**Him** is a doctor." → "**He** is a doctor."

**6. Adjective/Adverb Errors:**
- "She runs **quick**." → "She runs **quickly**."
- "This is **more better**." → "This is **better**."
- "She sings **beautiful**." → "She sings **beautifully**."

**7. Conjunction Errors:**
- "**Although** he was tired, **but** he went." → "**Although** he was tired, he went."
- "I like coffee **and** tea." (✓) → No error
- "**Because** I was tired." (fragment) → "**Because** I was tired, I went to bed."

**8. Comparative/Superlative Errors:**
- "She is **more taller** than me." → "She is **taller** than me."
- "This is the **more best** option." → "This is the **best** option."
- "This is **more expensive** than that." (✓) → No error

**9. Parallelism Errors:**
- "I like **swimming**, **running**, and **to read**." → "I like **swimming**, **running**, and **reading**."
- "She is **kind**, **smart**, and **has beauty**." → "She is **kind**, **smart**, and **beautiful**."

**10. Word Order Errors:**
- "**What** you **are** doing?" → "**What are** you doing?"
- "**Where** she **lives**?" → "**Where does** she live?"
- "I wonder **where is she**." → "I wonder **where she is**."

### 👀 See: Examples

**Error Detection (Sample Questions):**

1. "She don't like coffee."
   - Error: Verb agreement
   - Correction: "She **doesn't** like coffee."

2. "The man which lives here is my uncle."
   - Error: Relative pronoun
   - Correction: "The man **who** lives here is my uncle."

3. "I have went to the store."
   - Error: Verb form
   - Correction: "I have **gone** to the store."

4. "She is good in math."
   - Error: Preposition
   - Correction: "She is good **at** math."

5. "Me and John are friends."
   - Error: Pronoun case
   - Correction: "**John and I** are friends."

**Strategy for Error Detection:**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Read the sentence carefully |
| 2 | Identify the subject and verb |
| 3 | Check subject-verb agreement |
| 4 | Check verb tense |
| 5 | Check articles and prepositions |
| 6 | Check pronouns and word order |
| 7 | Check if the sentence sounds natural |

### ⚠️ Learn: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| Missing the error | Read slowly and carefully |
| Overlooking subject-verb agreement | Always check agreement |
| Ignoring article errors | Check every noun |
| Missing word order errors | Check question word order |

## 2. Sentence Improvement

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** **Sentence improvement** is the skill of making sentences grammatically correct, clearer, or more effective. It requires knowledge of grammar rules and style.

**Key Points:**
- Identify the weak area
- Know the rule
- Find the best improvement
- Choose the most natural option

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Improving Verb Forms**
```
[INCORRECT] → [CORRECT]
I have went → I have gone
She don't like → She doesn't like
```

**Structure 2: Improving Word Order**
```
[INCORRECT] → [CORRECT]
What you are doing? → What are you doing?
Where she lives? → Where does she live?
```

**Structure 3: Improving Phrasing**
```
[INCORRECT] → [CORRECT]
In spite of the fact that → Although
Due to the fact that → Because
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Sentence Improvement (Sample Questions):**

1. "She is more taller than me."
   - Improvement: "She is **taller** than me."

2. "The reason why I left is because I was tired."
   - Improvement: "The reason why I left is **that** I was tired."
   - Or: "I left **because** I was tired."

3. "He is a man who is honest."
   - Improvement: "He is **an honest man**."
   - Or: "He is a man **of honesty**."

4. "This is the most best option."
   - Improvement: "This is **the best** option."

5. "I would like to know that what is your name."
   - Improvement: "I would like to know **what your name is**."

**Common Sentence Improvements:**

| Weak Sentence | Improved Sentence |
|---------------|-------------------|
| The man who is sitting there is my uncle. | The man **sitting** there is my uncle. |
| She is a person who is very kind. | She is a **very kind person**. |
| In spite of the fact that it was raining, we went. | **Although** it was raining, we went. |
| Due to the fact that I was tired, I left. | **Because** I was tired, I left. |
| The reason why I came is because I wanted to help. | I came **because** I wanted to help. |

**Strategy for Sentence Improvement:**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Read the sentence carefully |
| 2 | Identify what is wrong or weak |
| 3 | Think of the rule or principle |
| 4 | Choose the best improvement |
| 5 | Check if the new sentence sounds natural |

## 3. Fill in the Blanks

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** **Fill in the blanks** is a test of grammar knowledge where you must choose the correct word(s) to complete a sentence. It requires understanding of context and grammar rules.

**Key Points:**
- Understand the context
- Know the grammar rule
- Choose the correct option
- Check the whole sentence

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Tense Fill-in**
```
I _____ (go) to school yesterday.
I **went** to school yesterday.
```

**Structure 2: Article Fill-in**
```
I saw _____ apple.
I saw **an** apple.
```

**Structure 3: Preposition Fill-in**
```
She is good _____ math.
She is good **at** math.
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Fill in the Blanks (Sample Questions):**

1. "I _____ (go) to school every day."
   - Answer: **go**

2. "She _____ (work) here since 2020."
   - Answer: **has worked**

3. "I saw _____ apple on the table."
   - Answer: **an**

4. "She is good _____ math."
   - Answer: **at**

5. "_____ it was raining, I went out."
   - Answer: **Although**

**Common Fill-in Types:**

| Type | Example | Answer |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Tense** | I _____ (go) yesterday. | went |
| **Article** | I saw _____ dog. | a |
| **Preposition** | She is afraid _____ spiders. | of |
| **Conjunction** | I was tired, _____ I went to bed. | so |
| **Pronoun** | _____ book is this? | Whose |
| **Modal** | You _____ see a doctor. | should |

**Strategy for Fill in the Blanks:**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Read the whole sentence |
| 2 | Identify the missing part |
| 3 | Look at the context |
| 4 | Apply the grammar rule |
| 5 | Check if the sentence makes sense |

## 4. Rearrangement

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** **Rearrangement** is the skill of putting jumbled words or sentences into the correct order to form a meaningful sentence or passage.

**Key Points:**
- Identify the subject
- Find the verb
- Look for logical connections
- Check the final order

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Word Rearrangement**
```
[JUMBLED] → [CORRECT]
like / I / coffee → I like coffee.
go / to / school / I / every day → I go to school every day.
```

**Structure 2: Sentence Rearrangement**
```
[JUMBLED] → [CORRECT]
1. It was raining.
2. I stayed home.
→ I stayed home because it was raining.
→ Because it was raining, I stayed home.
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Word Rearrangement:**

| Jumbled | Correct |
|---------|---------|
| like / I / coffee | **I like coffee.** |
| go / to / school / I / every day | **I go to school every day.** |
| is / she / a / doctor | **She is a doctor.** |
| yesterday / went / I / to / the store | **I went to the store yesterday.** |
| you / do / like / coffee / ? | **Do you like coffee?** |

**Sentence Rearrangement:**

| Jumbled | Correct |
|---------|---------|
| 1. It was raining.
2. I stayed home. | **Because it was raining, I stayed home.** |
| 1. She studied hard.
2. She passed the exam. | **She studied hard, so she passed the exam.** |
| 1. The sun was setting.
2. The birds were singing. | **The sun was setting, and the birds were singing.** |

**Strategy for Rearrangement:**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Identify the subject |
| 2 | Find the verb |
| 3 | Look for logical connections |
| 4 | Check the word order (SVO) |
| 5 | Read the final sentence |

## 5. Cloze Test

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** A **cloze test** is a passage with missing words that you must fill in using context and grammar rules. It tests your overall understanding of English.

**Key Points:**
- Read the entire passage first
- Understand the context
- Use grammar and vocabulary knowledge
- Check the whole passage

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Cloze Passage**
```
[PASSAGE WITH BLANKS]
_____ is a beautiful day. The sun _____ shining. I _____ to the park.
→ **It** is a beautiful day. The sun **is** shining. I **will go** to the park.
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Sample Cloze Test:**

Read the passage and fill in the blanks:

"Yesterday, I _____ (1) to the store. I _____ (2) milk and bread. _____ (3) I arrived home, I _____ (4) a sandwich. It _____ (5) delicious."

**Answers:**
1. **went**
2. **bought**
3. **When**
4. **made**
5. **was**

**Passage with Options:**

"_____ (1) I was young, I _____ (2) to be a doctor. I _____ (3) hard and _____ (4) my dream."

Options:
1. A) When B) While C) As D) Since
2. A) want B) wanted C) had wanted D) have wanted
3. A) study B) studied C) had studied D) have studied
4. A) achieved B) achieve C) had achieved D) have achieved

**Answers:**
1. A) When
2. B) wanted
3. B) studied
4. A) achieved

**Strategy for Cloze Tests:**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Read the entire passage first |
| 2 | Identify the context |
| 3 | Think of possible answers |
| 4 | Apply grammar rules |
| 5 | Check if the passage makes sense |

## 6. Para Jumbles

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** **Para jumbles** (paragraph jumbles) are exercises where sentences are jumbled and you must arrange them in the correct logical order to form a coherent paragraph.

**Key Points:**
- Identify the topic sentence
- Look for logical connections
- Find pronouns and connectors
- Check the flow of ideas

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Para Jumble**
```
[JUMBLED SENTENCES] → [LOGICAL ORDER]
A. It was raining.
B. I stayed home.
C. Because I was tired.
→ **B. I stayed home. C. Because I was tired. A. It was raining.**
(Logical order: Main idea → Reason → Context)
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Sample Para Jumble:**

**Arrange the sentences in logical order:**

A. In conclusion, technology has changed our lives.
B. First, it has improved communication.
C. Technology has had many benefits.
D. Second, it has made information accessible.

**Answer:** C → B → D → A

**Explanation:**
- C: Topic sentence (overview)
- B: First reason
- D: Second reason
- A: Conclusion

**Another Example:**

A. They are also great companions.
B. Dogs make wonderful pets.
C. They are loyal and protective.
D. For these reasons, dogs are loved by many.

**Answer:** B → C → A → D

**Explanation:**
- B: Topic sentence
- C: First supporting detail
- A: Second supporting detail
- D: Conclusion

**Strategy for Para Jumbles:**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Read all sentences |
| 2 | Identify the topic sentence |
| 3 | Look for logical connections |
| 4 | Find pronouns (they, he, she, it) |
| 5 | Find connectors (however, therefore, first, second) |
| 6 | Arrange in logical order |
| 7 | Read the completed paragraph |

## 7. Editing

### 📚 Learn: The Concept

**Definition:** **Editing** is the skill of finding and correcting grammatical errors in a passage. It requires careful reading and knowledge of all grammar rules.

**Key Points:**
- Read the passage carefully
- Identify errors
- Correct the errors
- Check the whole passage

### 📐 Study: Sentence Structures

**Structure 1: Editing Passage**
```
[PASSAGE WITH ERRORS] → [CORRECTED PASSAGE]
She don't like coffee. She prefer tea.
→ She **doesn't** like coffee. She **prefers** tea.
```

### 👀 See: Examples

**Sample Editing Passage:**

**Original Passage (with errors):**

"She go to school every day. She is a good student. She studies hard and she pass all her exams. Her parents is proud of her."

**Corrected Passage:**

"She **goes** to school every day. She is a good student. She studies hard and she **passes** all her exams. Her parents **are** proud of her."

**Errors Found:**
1. "go" → "goes" (subject-verb agreement)
2. "pass" → "passes" (subject-verb agreement)
3. "is" → "are" (subject-verb agreement)

**Another Example:**

**Original Passage (with errors):**

"The man which lives next door is a doctor. He is very kind. He have helped many people. His patients likes him."

**Corrected Passage:**

"The man **who** lives next door is a doctor. He is very kind. He **has** helped many people. His patients **like** him."

**Errors Found:**
1. "which" → "who" (relative pronoun for people)
2. "have" → "has" (subject-verb agreement)
3. "likes" → "like" (subject-verb agreement)

**Strategy for Editing:**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Read the entire passage |
| 2 | Identify errors (subject-verb, tense, articles, prepositions) |
| 3 | Correct each error |
| 4 | Read the corrected passage |
| 5 | Check if it makes sense |
