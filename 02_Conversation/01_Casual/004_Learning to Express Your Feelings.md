## Topic: Learning to Express Your Feelings

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been holding so much inside lately. I don't know how to tell people how I really feel. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a heavy place to be. When we don't express our feelings, they just build up inside us. |
| **Alexis** | I'm scared that if I share how I feel, I'll be judged or rejected. |
| **Alexa** | That's a real fear. But keeping everything inside also comes with a cost. |
| **Alexis** | What do you mean? |
| **Alexa** | When you don't express yourself, you carry a weight that gets heavier over time. It affects your relationships and your own peace. |
| **Alexis** | I've noticed that I feel distant from people, even when I'm with them. Like there's a wall between us. |
| **Alexa** | That wall is built from the words you haven't said. |
| **Alexis** | I want to start sharing more. But I don't even know where to begin. |
| **Alexa** | Start small. Pick one person you trust and tell them one thing you've been feeling. Just one. |
| **Alexis** | That sounds simple, but it's terrifying. |
| **Alexa** | It is. But each time you do it, it gets a little easier. And the people who care about you will meet you with kindness. |
| **Alexis** | What if they don't respond well? |
| **Alexa** | That's a possibility. But you can't control how others react. You can only control whether you speak your truth. |
| **Alexis** | I've been keeping so much inside that I don't even know what I feel anymore. |
| **Alexa** | That's a common result of suppressing emotions. Maybe you can start by writing down your feelings. Just for yourself. |
| **Alexis** | That's a good idea. I can start journaling. Just to get it out of my head. |
| **Alexa** | Yes. And once you see your feelings on paper, they become more clear and less overwhelming. |
| **Alexis** | Then maybe I can share some of it with someone I trust. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful progression. You're learning to honor your own heart. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been pretending I'm okay when I'm not. And it's been exhausting. |
| **Alexa** | It is exhausting. But you don't have to pretend anymore. You're allowed to feel what you feel. |
| **Alexis** | I want to let myself feel. Fully. Without judgment. |
| **Alexa** | That's a brave step. And you don't have to do it alone. I'm here to listen. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm finally learning to be honest with myself. |
| **Alexa** | And that's where all honesty begins. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Expressing feelings** | Verb Phrase | Communicating your emotions verbally or through writing |
| **Build up** (inside) | Phrasal Verb | To accumulate or grow stronger over time |
| **Judged** | Verb | Criticized or formed a negative opinion about someone |
| **Rejected** | Verb | Dismissed or not accepted |
| **Cost** (of holding inside) | Noun (metaphor) | The emotional toll of suppressing feelings |
| **Wall** (metaphor) | Noun | Emotional barrier that prevents closeness |
| **Terrifying** | Adjective | Extremely scary |
| **Meet you with kindness** | Phrase | To respond with care and compassion |
| **Speak your truth** | Phrase | To express your honest feelings |
| **Suppressing emotions** | Verb Phrase | Pushing feelings down and not acknowledging them |
| **Journaling** | Noun / Verb | The practice of writing down thoughts and feelings |
| **Overwhelming** | Adjective | Too much to handle; overpowering |
| **Honor your own heart** | Phrase | To respect and acknowledge your own feelings and needs |
| **Exhausting** | Adjective | Making you feel extremely tired |
| **Allowed** | Verb / Adjective | Having permission; being free to do something |
| **Judgment** (without judgment) | Noun | Forming a critical opinion about yourself |
| **Brave** | Adjective | Facing difficulty or fear without running away |
