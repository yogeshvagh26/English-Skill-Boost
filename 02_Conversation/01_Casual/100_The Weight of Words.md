## Topic: The Weight of Words

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking about how much weight words carry. Something said in a moment can stay with you for years. |
| **Alexa** | That's so true. Words can heal, and they can also hurt. We don't always realize the impact they have. |
| **Alexis** | I've been on both sides. I've said things I regret, and I've had things said to me that I still remember. |
| **Alexa** | It's a heavy responsibility, but also a beautiful one. The right words at the right time can change everything. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be more mindful about what I say. Not just avoiding hurt, but actively choosing words that uplift. |
| **Alexa** | That's a lovely intention. It's not about being perfect—it's about being intentional. |
| **Alexis** | I think sometimes I speak before I think, especially when I'm emotional. And then I regret it. |
| **Alexa** | That's so human. But you can also learn to pause. A breath before speaking can make all the difference. |
| **Alexis** | I've also been trying to notice the words I use with myself. I'm often harsher with myself than with anyone else. |
| **Alexa** | The words you say to yourself shape your self-worth. Be gentle with that inner voice. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be someone whose words are a safe place for others. |
| **Alexa** | You already are. I've felt safe in your words many times. |
| **Alexis** | That means a lot. I think we underestimate how much our words can be a gift. |
| **Alexa** | They really can. A kind word can turn someone's day around. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to carry this awareness with me. To speak with more care and more love. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful way to live. Your words are your legacy. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you for always speaking kindly to me. It's made such a difference in my life. |
| **Alexa** | I'm glad. You deserve kind words—spoken and unspoken. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Carry weight** (words) | Phrase | To have significant emotional impact or importance |
| **Impact** (of words) | Noun | The strong effect that words can have on someone |
| **Regret** | Noun / Verb | Feeling sad or disappointed about something you did or said |
| **Mindful** | Adjective | Being aware and intentional about your actions |
| **Uplift** (with words) | Verb | To make someone feel better or more hopeful |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Pause** (before speaking) | Verb / Noun | To stop briefly before continuing |
| **Harsh** (with yourself) | Adjective | Critical and severe in judgment |
| **Self-worth** | Noun | The value you place on yourself |
| **Gentle** (with yourself) | Adjective | Kind, soft, and forgiving toward yourself |
| **Safe place** | Phrase | A space or person where you feel emotionally secure |
| **Gift** (words as a) | Metaphor | Something valuable and kind that you offer freely |
| **Awareness** | Noun | Conscious knowledge and attention to your actions |
| **Legacy** (your words as) | Noun | What you leave behind; how you are remembered |
| **Unspoken** (words) | Adjective | Not said out loud, but still felt or expressed through actions |
