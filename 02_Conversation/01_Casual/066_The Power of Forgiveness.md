## Topic: The Power of Forgiveness

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been holding onto a grudge against someone for a long time. I think about it every day, and it's exhausting. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a heavy burden to carry. Holding onto anger and resentment can feel like a full-time job. |
| **Alexis** | I know I should forgive them, but I feel like if I do, I'm letting them off the hook. |
| **Alexa** | I used to think that too. But forgiveness isn't about excusing what they did. It's about freeing yourself from the weight of it. |
| **Alexis** | So it's more about me than them? |
| **Alexa** | Exactly. When you forgive, you're not saying what they did was okay. You're saying you refuse to carry it anymore. |
| **Alexis** | I've been carrying this anger for years. I think it's affected my health, my sleep, my relationships. |
| **Alexa** | That's the cost of unforgiveness. It doesn't hurt the other person—it hurts you. |
| **Alexis** | What if I'm not ready to forgive? |
| **Alexa** | Then you can start by being willing to forgive. Even that small intention is a step. |
| **Alexis** | I've also been hard on myself for mistakes I've made. I don't know how to forgive myself either. |
| **Alexa** | Self-forgiveness can be even harder. But you deserve the same compassion you'd give a friend. |
| **Alexis** | I keep replaying my mistakes in my head. I tell myself I should have known better. |
| **Alexa** | But you did the best you could with what you knew at the time. You've grown since then. |
| **Alexis** | That's true. I'm not the same person I was when I made those mistakes. |
| **Alexa** | That's what forgiveness is—acknowledging that you've grown and that your past doesn't define you. |
| **Alexis** | I want to let go of the guilt and the anger. I'm tired of carrying it. |
| **Alexa** | That's a brave choice. Forgiveness is a practice, not a one-time event. You may have to choose it again and again. |
| **Alexis** | I'm going to start today. I'll try to forgive both them and myself. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful step. And remember, it's okay if it takes time. Be patient with yourself. |
| **Alexis** | I feel like a weight is already lifting just by talking about it. |
| **Alexa** | That's the freedom of forgiveness. It sets you free. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Grudge** | Noun | A persistent feeling of resentment toward someone |
| **Exhausting** | Adjective | Making you feel extremely tired and drained |
| **Burden** | Noun | A heavy load or responsibility; something difficult to carry |
| **Resentment** | Noun | A feeling of bitterness or anger because of unfair treatment |
| **Excusing** (what they did) | Verb | Justifying or overlooking a wrong action |
| **Freeing yourself** | Verb Phrase | Releasing yourself from emotional burden |
| **Unforgiveness** | Noun | The state of not forgiving; holding onto anger |
| **Willing** (to forgive) | Adjective | Ready and prepared to do something |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Self-forgiveness** | Noun | The act of forgiving yourself for past mistakes |
| **Compassion** | Noun | Sympathy and concern for the suffering of others (or yourself) |
| **Replaying** (mistakes) | Verb | Thinking about past events repeatedly |
| **Acknowledge** | Verb | To recognize and accept something as true |
| **Defines you** | Verb Phrase | Determines who you are |
| **Guilt** | Noun | A feeling of responsibility for something wrong |
| **Practice** (not a one-time event) | Noun | Something you do repeatedly to improve |
| **Patient** (with yourself) | Adjective | Able to wait calmly and give yourself time |
| **Weight lifting** (metaphor) | Phrase | Feeling a reduction of emotional burden |
| **Sets you free** | Phrase | Releases you from emotional chains |
