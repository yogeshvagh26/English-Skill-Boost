### 1. Situation Name : At a Bank – Opening an Account & Making a Transaction

---

### 2. Conversation

*Setting: A local bank branch. Mr. Patel wants to open a new savings account and also deposit a check. He speaks with a bank teller (Karen) and then a personal banker (James).*

| Speaker             | Dialogue                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Karen (Teller)**  | Good morning! Welcome to First National Bank. How can I help you today?                                                                                                                                    |
| **Mr. Patel**       | Good morning. I'd like to open a new savings account, please.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Karen**           | Wonderful. For that, you'll need to speak with one of our personal bankers. Let me check if James is available. … Yes, he's free. Please have a seat over there, and he'll be with you in just a moment.   |
| *(Mr. Patel moves to James's desk)*                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **James (Banker)**  | Hi there, I'm James. I understand you'd like to open a savings account. May I see your identification, please? A driver's license or passport will work.                                                  |
| **Mr. Patel**       | Certainly. Here's my passport.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **James**           | Thank you. And do you have a second form of ID, like a utility bill or bank statement with your current address?                                                                                          |
| **Mr. Patel**       | Yes, I have my electricity bill. Here you go.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **James**           | Perfect. What type of savings account are you interested in? We have a basic savings account with a low interest rate but no monthly fees, and a high-yield savings account with better rates but a minimum balance requirement. |
| **Mr. Patel**       | I think the high-yield one sounds better. What's the minimum balance?                                                                                                                                     |
| **James**           | The minimum balance is $500. If your balance falls below that, there's a monthly maintenance fee of $10.                                                                                                  |
| **Mr. Patel**       | That's fine. I plan to keep well over that amount. Let's go with the high-yield account.                                                                                                                  |
| **James**           | Great choice. I'll need you to fill out this application form. It asks for your full name, date of birth, occupation, and contact details.                                                                 |
| *(Mr. Patel fills out the form)*                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Mr. Patel**       | Here's the completed form.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **James**           | Thank you. I'll enter this into our system. … All done. Your new account number is 8847-2109. You'll receive your debit card in the mail within 5 to 7 business days.                                     |
| **Mr. Patel**       | Excellent. Also, I have a check that I'd like to deposit into my new account. Can I do that here?                                                                                                         |
| **James**           | Yes, absolutely. You can go back to the teller counter, and Karen will process that deposit for you. Just endorse the check (sign the back) before handing it over.                                      |
| **Mr. Patel**       | Got it. Thank you, James.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| *(Mr. Patel returns to Karen at the counter)*                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Mr. Patel**       | Hi Karen, I'd like to deposit this check into my new account.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Karen**           | Sure. May I see your account number or the temporary paperwork James gave you?                                                                                                                            |
| **Mr. Patel**       | Here's the paperwork.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Karen**           | Perfect. Is this a personal check or a cashier's check?                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Mr. Patel**       | It's a personal check from my cousin.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Karen**           | Okay. Please endorse it on the back. … Thank you. How much is this for?                                                                                                                                   |
| **Mr. Patel**       | $350.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Karen**           | I'll process that now. Just so you know, personal checks usually take 1 to 2 business days to clear. The funds will show in your account as "pending" until then.                                        |
| **Mr. Patel**       | That's fine. I'm not in a hurry.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Karen**           | All done. Here's your deposit receipt. Is there anything else I can assist you with?                                                                                                                      |
| **Mr. Patel**       | No, that's everything. Thanks so much for your help!                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Karen**           | You're welcome. Have a great day, Mr. Patel!                                                                                                                                                              |

### 3. Vocabulary and Expressions

| Word/Phrase                       | Part of Speech | Meaning                                                                         | Example Sentence                                                                        |
| --------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Personal banker**               | Noun phrase    | A bank employee who helps customers with accounts, loans, and financial advice  | *My personal banker helped me choose the right credit card.*                            |
| **Identification (ID)**           | Noun           | An official document proving who you are (passport, driver's license)           | *You'll need to show valid identification to open an account.*                          |
| **Utility bill**                  | Noun phrase    | A bill for services like electricity, water, or gas (used as proof of address)  | *A recent utility bill confirms your residential address.*                              |
| **Interest rate**                 | Noun phrase    | The percentage of money the bank pays you for keeping funds in an account       | *High-yield accounts offer a better interest rate.*                                     |
| **Monthly fee**                   | Noun phrase    | A recurring charge the bank takes from your account each month                  | *Some banks waive the monthly fee if you maintain a minimum balance.*                   |
| **Minimum balance**               | Noun phrase    | The smallest amount of money you must keep in the account to avoid penalties    | *If you drop below the minimum balance, you'll be charged a fee.*                       |
| **Maintenance fee**               | Noun phrase    | A fee charged for keeping the account active and managed                        | *The maintenance fee is deducted automatically on the first of the month.*              |
| **Application form**              | Noun phrase    | A document you fill out with personal details to request a service              | *Please complete this application form in blue or black ink.*                           |
| **Debit card**                    | Noun phrase    | A card linked to your bank account for making purchases or withdrawals          | *You can use your debit card at ATMs and stores.*                                       |
| **Business days**                 | Noun phrase    | Weekdays (Monday–Friday), excluding public holidays                             | *The transfer will take 2 to 3 business days to process.*                               |
| **Deposit**                       | Verb / Noun    | To put money into a bank account / the money you put in                         | *I'd like to deposit this cash into my checking account.*                               |
| **Endorse (a check)**             | Verb           | To sign the back of a check to authorize its deposit or cashing                 | *Don't forget to endorse the check before handing it to the teller.*                    |
| **Temporary paperwork**           | Noun phrase    | A provisional document given until the permanent one arrives                    | *Your temporary paperwork includes your new account number.*                            |
| **Personal check / Cashier's check** | Noun phrase  | A check from an individual's account / a check guaranteed by the bank           | *A cashier's check is more secure than a personal check.*                               |
| **Pending**                       | Adjective      | Showing that a transaction is being processed but not yet completed             | *The deposit is pending until the check clears.*                                        |
| **Clear (a check)**               | Verb           | When funds from a check are fully transferred and available to use              | *It usually takes two days for a check to clear.*                                       |

### 4. Key Takeaways

- **Common Sentence Patterns:**
  - *"I'd like to open / apply for…"* – Starting a request for a new service.
  - *"May I see your [document]?"* – Requesting identification or paperwork.
  - *"What's the minimum balance / interest rate?"* – Asking about account conditions.
  - *"I'll enter this into our system."* – Tells the customer their data is being recorded.
  - *"I'll process that now."* – Confirming that a transaction is being handled.
  - *"The funds will show as 'pending' until…"* – Explaining delays or holds on money.

- **Useful Phrases to Remember:**
  - *"Here you go."* – Handing over documents or items.
  - *"Got it."* – A quick way to say "I understand" or "I've taken note."
  - *"I'm not in a hurry."* – Reassuring the staff that you can wait, reducing pressure.
  - *"All done."* – Means the task is complete.
  - *"Is there anything else I can assist you with?"* – A standard, polite closing question from service staff.

- **Practical Speaking Tips:**
  1. **Prepare documents beforehand** – Banks always ask for multiple IDs and proof of address. Bring at least two forms (passport + utility bill) to avoid delays.
  2. **Clarify fees and conditions** – Don't just accept an account; ask *"Are there any hidden fees?"* or *"What happens if I go below the minimum balance?"* to avoid surprises.
  3. **Know the difference between check types** – A *"personal check"* takes time to clear; a *"cashier's check"* or *"certified check"* is guaranteed and clears faster. This helps you plan when the money will be available.
  4. **Use active listening words** – When staff give you numbers (account number, fees), repeat them back (e.g., *"So the minimum balance is $500, and the fee is $10 – got it."*) to confirm understanding and show attentiveness.
