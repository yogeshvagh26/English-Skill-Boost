## Topic: The Power of Community

### Dialogue

| Speaker | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Alexis** | I've been feeling so isolated lately. Even though I'm surrounded by people, I feel like I'm going through everything alone. |
| **Alexa** | That's such a heavy feeling. But you're not alone. Community isn't just about being physically around others—it's about being truly seen and supported. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been hiding parts of myself because I'm afraid of being judged. But that hiding is making me feel more alone. |
| **Alexa** | The irony is that the more we hide, the more disconnected we feel. Real connection comes from showing up as our authentic selves. |
| **Alexis** | I want to find my people. I want to feel like I belong somewhere. |
| **Alexa** | Belonging starts with you. When you show up as yourself, you attract the right people. |
| **Alexis** | But what if I show up and no one accepts me? |
| **Alexa** | That's a real fear. But the people who are meant to be in your life will welcome the real you. And you'll feel less alone. |
| **Alexis** | I've been thinking about joining a group—maybe a book club or a hiking group. Just to be around people who share my interests. |
| **Alexa** | That's a wonderful idea. Shared interests are a natural foundation for connection. |
| **Alexis** | I'm nervous, though. I'm not great at meeting new people. |
| **Alexa** | You don't have to be great at it. You just have to be open. And remember, everyone there is probably looking for connection too. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been waiting for others to come to me. But maybe I need to take the first step. |
| **Alexa** | That's a powerful shift. Sometimes you have to be the one to build the bridge. |
| **Alexis** | I also want to be more present in the communities I already have—my friends, my neighbors. I've been so distant. |
| **Alexa** | That's a beautiful intention. Connection doesn't have to be grand—it can be a simple check-in or a shared meal. |
| **Alexis** | I think I've been so focused on my own struggles that I forgot others might be struggling too. We can support each other. |
| **Alexa** | That's the heart of community. We lift each other up when we're down. We celebrate together when we're up. |
| **Alexis** | I want to be part of something bigger than myself. I want to contribute and receive. |
| **Alexa** | You already do. You just need to open your eyes to the connections that are already there. |
| **Alexis** | Thank you. I feel like I'm starting to see the web of support that's around me. |
| **Alexa** | It's always been there. You just had to look. |

### Vocabulary with Meanings

| Word / Phrase | Type | Meaning |
|---------------|------|---------|
| **Isolated** | Adjective | Feeling alone and disconnected from others |
| **Authentic** | Adjective | Real and genuine; not fake |
| **Belong** | Verb | To feel accepted and valued as part of a group |
| **Show up** | Phrasal Verb | To be present and engaged, especially when it's difficult |
| **Attract** (the right people) | Verb | To draw people toward you because of who you are |
| **Foundation** (of connection) | Noun | The base or core on which something is built |
| **Open** (to new people) | Adjective | Willing to meet and connect with others |
| **Shift** (in approach) | Noun | A change in perspective or action |
| **Bridge** (metaphor) | Noun | Something that connects two people or groups |
| **Distant** | Adjective | Emotionally detached or disconnected from others |
| **Intention** | Noun | A plan or purpose behind an action |
| **Check-in** | Noun | A brief message or visit to see how someone is doing |
| **Lift each other up** | Phrase | To encourage, support, and help one another |
| **Contribute and receive** | Verb Phrase | To give to and accept help from a community |
| **Web of support** | Metaphor | The interconnected network of people who care for you |
